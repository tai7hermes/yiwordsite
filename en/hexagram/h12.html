否
Standstill

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision.

LineOriginalTranslation
JudgmentH12-JUDGMENT否之匪人，不利君子貞，大往小來。People fail to understand or unite with one another. This does not favor the noble person’s steadfast course. The great departs and the small approaches.
Line 1#067H12-L1初六，拔茅茹以其彙，貞吉。亨。Grass pulled up, and bringing with it other stalks with whose roots it is connected. With steadfastness (on the part of its subject), good fortune follows and progress.
Line 2#068H12-L2六二，包承，小人吉，大人否。亨。One is patient and obedient. To the lesser person (comporting himself so) good fortune follows. If the great person (comport himself) as the distress and obstruction require, he will have success.
Line 3#069H12-L3六三，包羞。Hamed of the purpose folded (in his breast).
Line 4#070H12-L4九四，有命，无咎，疇離祉。One is acting in accordance with the ordination (of Heaven), and committing no blame. His companions will come and share in his happiness.
Line 5#071H12-L5九五，休否，大人吉。其亡其亡，繫于苞桑。The great person brings obstruction to an end. Good fortune. Stay alert to the danger of collapse, as if tied firmly to a cluster of mulberry trees.
Line 6#072H12-L6上九，傾否，先否後喜。The overthrow (and removal of) the condition of distress and obstruction. Before this there was that condition. Hereafter there will be joy.
