Taming Great

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision. · hexagram_id: H26 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Judgment H26-JUDGMENT

Original：大畜：利貞，不家食吉，利涉大川。

Translation：Great taming. Steadfastness is favorable. Not eating at home brings good fortune. It is favorable to cross the great river.

Line 1 H26-L1

Original：初九：有厲利已。

Translation：One is in a position of peril. It will be favorable for him to stop his advance.

Line 2 H26-L2

Original：九二：輿說輹。

Translation：A carriage with the strap under it removed.

Line 3 H26-L3

Original：九三：良馬逐，利艱貞。曰閑輿衛，利有攸往。

Translation：Good horses follow one another. Stay steadfast through difficulty. Practice driving and defense each day. It is favorable to go forward.

Line 4 H26-L4

Original：六四：童牛之牿，元吉。

Translation：The young bull, (and yet) having the piece of wood over his horns. Great good fortune follows.

Line 5 H26-L5

Original：六五：豶豕之牙，吉。

Translation：The teeth of a castrated hog. Good fortune follows.

Line 6 H26-L6

Original：上九：何天之衢，亨。

Translation：One is (as) in command of the firmament of heaven. There will be progress.
