JudgmentH26-JUDGMENT大畜：利貞，不家食吉，利涉大川。Great taming. Steadfastness is favorable. Not eating at home brings good fortune. It is favorable to cross the great river.
Line 1#151H26-L1初九：有厲利已。One is in a position of peril. It will be favorable for him to stop his advance.
Line 2#152H26-L2九二：輿說輹。A carriage with the strap under it removed.
Line 3#153H26-L3九三：良馬逐，利艱貞。曰閑輿衛，利有攸往。Good horses follow one another. Stay steadfast through difficulty. Practice driving and defense each day. It is favorable to go forward.
Line 4#154H26-L4六四：童牛之牿，元吉。The young bull, (and yet) having the piece of wood over his horns. Great good fortune follows.
Line 5#155H26-L5六五：豶豕之牙，吉。The teeth of a castrated hog. Good fortune follows.
Line 6#156H26-L6上九：何天之衢，亨。One is (as) in command of the firmament of heaven. There will be progress.
