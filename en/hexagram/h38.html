睽
Opposition
H38 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision.

LineOriginalTranslation
JudgmentH38-JUDGMENT睽：小事吉。Opposition. Good fortune in small matters.
Line 1H38-L1初九：悔亡，喪馬勿逐，自復；見惡人无咎。That (to its subject) reason for regret will disappear. He has lost his horses, but he should not seek for them; — they will return of themselves. Should he meet with bad men, he will not err (in communicating with them).
Line 2H38-L2九二：遇主于巷，无咎。One is happening to meet with his lord in a bye-passage. There is no blame.
Line 3H38-L3六三：見輿曳，其牛掣，其人天且劓，无初有終。The cart is dragged back and the oxen are restrained; the man is shaved and his nose cut. No good beginning, but a good end.
Line 4H38-L4九四：睽孤，遇元夫，交孚，厲无咎。Isolated through opposition, one meets a trustworthy person and joins in sincerity. Danger, but no blame.
Line 5H38-L5六五：悔亡，厥宗噬膚，往何咎。That (to its subject) reason for regret will disappear. With his relative (and minister he unites closely and readily) as if he were biting through a piece of skin. When he goes forward (with this help), what error can there be ?
Line 6H38-L6上九：睽孤， 見豕負涂，載鬼一車， 先張之弧，后說之弧，匪寇婚媾，往遇雨Isolated through opposition, one sees a mud-covered pig and a wagon full of ghosts. The bow is first drawn, then put aside: this is not a robber but a marriage seeker. Going forward and meeting rain brings good fortune.
