睽 Opposition

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision. · hexagram_id: H38 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Judgment H38-JUDGMENT

Original：睽：小事吉。

Translation：Opposition. Good fortune in small matters.

Line 1 H38-L1

Original：初九：悔亡，喪馬勿逐，自復；見惡人无咎。

Translation：That (to its subject) reason for regret will disappear. He has lost his horses, but he should not seek for them; — they will return of themselves. Should he meet with bad men, he will not err (in communicating with them).

Line 2 H38-L2

Original：九二：遇主于巷，无咎。

Translation：One is happening to meet with his lord in a bye-passage. There is no blame.

Line 3 H38-L3

Original：六三：見輿曳，其牛掣，其人天且劓，无初有終。

Translation：The cart is dragged back and the oxen are restrained; the man is shaved and his nose cut. No good beginning, but a good end.

Line 4 H38-L4

Original：九四：睽孤，遇元夫，交孚，厲无咎。

Translation：Isolated through opposition, one meets a trustworthy person and joins in sincerity. Danger, but no blame.

Line 5 H38-L5

Original：六五：悔亡，厥宗噬膚，往何咎。

Translation：That (to its subject) reason for regret will disappear. With his relative (and minister he unites closely and readily) as if he were biting through a piece of skin. When he goes forward (with this help), what error can there be ?

Line 6 H38-L6

Original：上九：睽孤， 見豕負涂，載鬼一車， 先張之弧，后說之弧，匪寇婚媾，往遇雨

Translation：Isolated through opposition, one sees a mud-covered pig and a wagon full of ghosts. The bow is first drawn, then put aside: this is not a robber but a marriage seeker. Going forward and meeting rain brings good fortune.
