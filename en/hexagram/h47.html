困 Oppression

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision. · hexagram_id: H47 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Judgment H47-JUDGMENT

Original：困：亨，貞大人吉，无咎，有言不信。

Translation：Oppression brings success. For the great person, perseverance brings good fortune and no blame. Words are not believed.

Line 1 H47-L1

Original：初六：臀困于株木，入于幽谷，三歲不覿。

Translation：One is with bare buttocks straitened under the stump of a tree. He enters a dark valley, and for three years has no prospect (of deliverance).

Line 2 H47-L2

Original：九二：困于酒食，朱紱方來，利用亨祀，征凶，无咎。

Translation：Oppressed amid food and drink. A high official in red knee bands is coming. It is favorable to make an offering. Going forward brings misfortune, but no blame.

Line 3 H47-L3

Original：六三：困于石，據于蒺藜，入于其宮，不見其妻，凶。

Translation：One is straitened before a (frowning) rock. He lays hold of thorns. He enters his palace, and does not see his wife. Misfortune follows.

Line 4 H47-L4

Original：九四：來徐徐，困于金車，吝，有終。

Translation：He comes slowly, oppressed by a metal carriage. Regret, but there is an end.

Line 5 H47-L5

Original：九五：劓刖，困于赤紱，乃徐有說，利用祭祀。

Translation：The nose and feet are injured, and one is constrained by officials in red knee bands. Relief comes slowly. It is favorable to make an offering.

Line 6 H47-L6

Original：上六：困于葛藟，于臲卼，曰動悔。有悔，征吉。

Translation：Oppressed by creeping vines and an unstable position. If one says movement brings regret, there is regret; setting forth brings good fortune.
