Judgment H14-JUDGMENT

Original：大有：元亨。

Translation：Great possession brings supreme success.

Line 1 H14-L1

Original：初九：无交害，匪咎，艱則无咎。

Translation：There is no approach to what is injurious, and there is no blame. Let there be a realisation of the difficulty (and danger of the position), and there will be no blame (to the end).

Line 2 H14-L2

Original：九二：大車以載，有攸往，无咎。

Translation：We have a large wagon with its load. In whatever direction advance is made, there will be no blame.

Line 3 H14-L3

Original：九三：公用亨于天子，小人弗克。

Translation：Us a feudal prince presenting his offerings to the Son of Heaven. A small man would be unequal (to such a duty).

Line 4 H14-L4

Original：九四：匪其彭，无咎。

Translation：One is keeping his great resources under restraint. There is no blame.

Line 5 H14-L5

Original：六五：厥孚交如，威如；吉。

Translation：The sincerity of its subject reciprocated by that of all the others (represented in the hexagram). He should display a proper majesty, and good fortune follows.

Line 6 H14-L6

Original：上九：自天佑之，吉无不利。

Translation：Help comes from heaven. Good fortune. Everything is favorable.
