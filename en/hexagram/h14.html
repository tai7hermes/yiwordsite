Great
H14 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision.

LineOriginalTranslation
JudgmentH14-JUDGMENT大有：元亨。Great possession brings supreme success.
Line 1H14-L1初九：无交害，匪咎，艱則无咎。There is no approach to what is injurious, and there is no blame. Let there be a realisation of the difficulty (and danger of the position), and there will be no blame (to the end).
Line 2H14-L2九二：大車以載，有攸往，无咎。We have a large wagon with its load. In whatever direction advance is made, there will be no blame.
Line 3H14-L3九三：公用亨于天子，小人弗克。Us a feudal prince presenting his offerings to the Son of Heaven. A small man would be unequal (to such a duty).
Line 4H14-L4九四：匪其彭，无咎。One is keeping his great resources under restraint. There is no blame.
Line 5H14-L5六五：厥孚交如，威如；吉。The sincerity of its subject reciprocated by that of all the others (represented in the hexagram). He should display a proper majesty, and good fortune follows.
Line 6H14-L6上九：自天佑之，吉无不利。Help comes from heaven. Good fortune. Everything is favorable.
