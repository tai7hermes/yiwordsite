JudgmentH61-JUDGMENT中孚：豚魚吉，利涉大川，利貞。Inner Truth moves even pigs and fish. Good fortune. It is favorable to cross the great river; perseverance is favorable.
Line 1H61-L1初九：虞吉，有他不燕。One is resting (in himself). Good fortune follows. If he sought to any other, he would not find rest.
Line 2H61-L2九二：鳴鶴在陰，其子和之，我有好爵，吾與爾靡之。One is (like) the crane crying out in her hidden retirement, and her young ones responding to her. (It is as if it were said), ' I have a cup of good spirits,' (and the response were), I will partake of it with you.'
Line 3H61-L3六三：得敵，或鼓或罷，或泣或歌。One is having met with his mate. Now he beats his drum, and now he leaves off. Now he weeps, and now he sings.
Line 4H61-L4六四：月几望，馬匹亡，无咎。One is (like) the moon nearly full, and (like) a horse (in a chariot) whose fellow disappears. There is no blame.
Line 5H61-L5九五：有孚攣如，无咎。One is perfectly sincere, and linking (others) to him in closest union. There is no blame.
Line 6H61-L6上九：翰音登于天，貞凶。A cock’s call rises toward heaven. Perseverance brings misfortune.
