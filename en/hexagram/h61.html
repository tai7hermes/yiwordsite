Judgment H61-JUDGMENT

Original：中孚：豚魚吉，利涉大川，利貞。

Translation：Inner Truth moves even pigs and fish. Good fortune. It is favorable to cross the great river; perseverance is favorable.

Line 1 H61-L1

Original：初九：虞吉，有他不燕。

Translation：One is resting (in himself). Good fortune follows. If he sought to any other, he would not find rest.

Line 2 H61-L2

Original：九二：鳴鶴在陰，其子和之，我有好爵，吾與爾靡之。

Translation：One is (like) the crane crying out in her hidden retirement, and her young ones responding to her. (It is as if it were said), ' I have a cup of good spirits,' (and the response were), I will partake of it with you.'

Line 3 H61-L3

Original：六三：得敵，或鼓或罷，或泣或歌。

Translation：One is having met with his mate. Now he beats his drum, and now he leaves off. Now he weeps, and now he sings.

Line 4 H61-L4

Original：六四：月几望，馬匹亡，无咎。

Translation：One is (like) the moon nearly full, and (like) a horse (in a chariot) whose fellow disappears. There is no blame.

Line 5 H61-L5

Original：九五：有孚攣如，无咎。

Translation：One is perfectly sincere, and linking (others) to him in closest union. There is no blame.

Line 6 H61-L6

Original：上九：翰音登于天，貞凶。

Translation：A cock’s call rises toward heaven. Perseverance brings misfortune.
