謙
Modesty
H15 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision.

LineOriginalTranslation
JudgmentH15-JUDGMENT謙：亨，君子有終。Modesty brings success. The noble person carries matters through to completion.
Line 1#085爻H15-L1初六：謙謙君子，用涉大川，吉。Us the noble person who adds humility to humility. (Even) the great stream may be crossed with this, and good fortune follows.
Line 2#086爻H15-L2六二：鳴謙，貞吉。Us humility that has made itself recognised. With steadfastness good fortune follows.
Line 3#087爻H15-L3九三：勞謙君子，有終吉。The noble person of (acknowledged) merit. He will maintain his success to the end, and have good fortune.
Line 4#088爻H15-L4六四：无不利，撝謙。One, whose action would be in every way favorable, stirring up (the more) his humility.
Line 5#089爻H15-L5六五：不富，以其鄰，利用侵伐，无不利。One who, without being rich, is able to employ his neighbors. He may favorablely use the force of arms. All his movements will be favorable.
Line 6#090爻H15-L6上六：鳴謙，利用行師，征邑國。Us humility that has made itself recognised. The subject of it will with advantage put his hosts in motion; but (he will only) punish his own towns and state.
