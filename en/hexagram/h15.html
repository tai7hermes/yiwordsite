謙 Modesty

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision. · hexagram_id: H15 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Judgment H15-JUDGMENT

Original：謙：亨，君子有終。

Translation：Modesty brings success. The noble person carries matters through to completion.

Line 1 H15-L1

Original：初六：謙謙君子，用涉大川，吉。

Translation：Us the noble person who adds humility to humility. (Even) the great stream may be crossed with this, and good fortune follows.

Line 2 H15-L2

Original：六二：鳴謙，貞吉。

Translation：Us humility that has made itself recognised. With steadfastness good fortune follows.

Line 3 H15-L3

Original：九三：勞謙君子，有終吉。

Translation：The noble person of (acknowledged) merit. He will maintain his success to the end, and have good fortune.

Line 4 H15-L4

Original：六四：无不利，撝謙。

Translation：One, whose action would be in every way favorable, stirring up (the more) his humility.

Line 5 H15-L5

Original：六五：不富，以其鄰，利用侵伐，无不利。

Translation：One who, without being rich, is able to employ his neighbors. He may favorablely use the force of arms. All his movements will be favorable.

Line 6 H15-L6

Original：上六：鳴謙，利用行師，征邑國。

Translation：Us humility that has made itself recognised. The subject of it will with advantage put his hosts in motion; but (he will only) punish his own towns and state.
