復
Return
H24 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision.

LineOriginalTranslation
JudgmentH24-JUDGMENT復：亨。出入无疾，朋來无咎。反復其道，七日來復，利有攸往。Return brings success. Going out and coming back cause no distress. Friends arrive without blame. After seven days comes return. It is favorable to have somewhere to go.
Line 1H24-L1初九：不復遠，无袛悔，元吉。One is returning (from an error) of no great extent, which would not proceed to anything requiring repentance. Great good fortune follows.
Line 2H24-L2六二：休復，吉。The admirable return (of its subject). Good fortune follows.
Line 3H24-L3六三：頻復，厲无咎。One who has made repeated returns. The position is perilous, but there will be no blame.
Line 4H24-L4六四：中行獨復。One is moving right in the centre (among those represented by the other broken lines), and yet returning alone (to his proper path).
Line 5H24-L5六五：敦復，无悔。The noble return of its subject. There will be no ground for repentance.
Line 6H24-L6上六：迷復，凶，有災眚。用行師，終有大敗，以其國君，凶；至于十年，不克Losing the way back brings misfortune and disaster. Using the army ends in a great defeat that harms the ruler. Even after ten years, the damage cannot be repaired.
