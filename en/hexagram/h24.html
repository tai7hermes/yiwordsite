復 Return

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision. · hexagram_id: H24 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Judgment H24-JUDGMENT

Original：復：亨。出入无疾，朋來无咎。反復其道，七日來復，利有攸往。

Translation：Return brings success. Going out and coming back cause no distress. Friends arrive without blame. After seven days comes return. It is favorable to have somewhere to go.

Line 1 H24-L1

Original：初九：不復遠，无袛悔，元吉。

Translation：One is returning (from an error) of no great extent, which would not proceed to anything requiring repentance. Great good fortune follows.

Line 2 H24-L2

Original：六二：休復，吉。

Translation：The admirable return (of its subject). Good fortune follows.

Line 3 H24-L3

Original：六三：頻復，厲无咎。

Translation：One who has made repeated returns. The position is perilous, but there will be no blame.

Line 4 H24-L4

Original：六四：中行獨復。

Translation：One is moving right in the centre (among those represented by the other broken lines), and yet returning alone (to his proper path).

Line 5 H24-L5

Original：六五：敦復，无悔。

Translation：The noble return of its subject. There will be no ground for repentance.

Line 6 H24-L6

Original：上六：迷復，凶，有災眚。用行師，終有大敗，以其國君，凶；至于十年，不克

Translation：Losing the way back brings misfortune and disaster. Using the army ends in a great defeat that harms the ruler. Even after ten years, the damage cannot be repaired.
