豐
Abundance
H55 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision.

LineOriginalTranslation
JudgmentH55-JUDGMENT豐：亨。王假之，勿憂，宜日中。Abundance brings success. The king attains it. Do not grieve; be like the sun at midday.
Line 1#325H55-L1初九：遇其配主，雖旬无咎，往有尚。One is meeting with his mate. Though they are both of the same character, there will be no blame. Advance will call forth approval.
Line 2#326H55-L2六二：豐其蔀，日中見斗，往得疑疾，有孚發若，吉。The screen is so dense that the Northern Dipper can be seen at noon. Going forward brings suspicion. Sincerity that becomes clear brings good fortune.
Line 3#327H55-L3九三：豐其沛，日中見沫，折其右肱，无咎。One is with an (additional) screen of a large and thick banner, through which at midday he can see (the small) Mei star. (In the darkness) he breaks his right arm; but there will be no blame.
Line 4#328H55-L4九四：豐其蔀，日中見斗，遇其夷主，吉。One is in a tent so large and thick that at midday he can see from it the constellation of the Bushel. But he meets with the subject of the (first) line, solid like himself. Good fortune follows.
Line 5#329H55-L5六五：來章，有慶譽，吉。People of ability and distinction gather around. Congratulations and praise follow. Good fortune.
Line 6#330H55-L6上六：豐其屋，蔀其家，窺其戶，闃其无人，三歲不觌，凶。His house is made abundant, yet his household is screened off. He peers through the gate and sees no one; for three years he sees no one. Misfortune.
