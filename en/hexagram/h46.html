Upward Pushing

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision. · hexagram_id: H46 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Judgment H46-JUDGMENT

Original：升：元亨，用見大人，勿恤，南征吉。

Translation：Ascending brings supreme success. It is favorable to see the great person; do not worry. Advance toward the south brings good fortune.

Line 1 H46-L1

Original：初六：允升，大吉。

Translation：One is advancing upwards with the welcome (of those above him). Great good fortune follows.

Line 2 H46-L2

Original：九二：孚乃利用禴，无咎。

Translation：One is with that sincerity which will make even the (small) offerings of the vernal sacrifice acceptable. There is no blame.

Line 3 H46-L3

Original：九三：升虛邑。

Translation：Cending upwards (as into) an empty city.

Line 4 H46-L4

Original：六四：王用亨于岐山，吉无咎。

Translation：The king presents an offering at Mount Qi. Good fortune and no blame.

Line 5 H46-L5

Original：六五：貞吉，升階。

Translation：One is firmly correct, and therefore enjoying good fortune. He ascends the stairs (with all due ceremony).

Line 6 H46-L6

Original：上六：冥升，利于不息之貞。

Translation：Ascending in darkness. It is favorable to persist unceasingly in what is right.
