Upward Pushing

OriginalOriginalTranslation
Judgment
H46-JUDGMENT		 升：元亨，用見大人，勿恤，南征吉。 Ascending brings supreme success. It is favorable to see the great person; do not worry. Advance toward the south brings good fortune.
Line 1
H46-L1		 初六：允升，大吉。 One is advancing upwards with the welcome (of those above him). Great good fortune follows.
Line 2
H46-L2		 九二：孚乃利用禴，无咎。 One is with that sincerity which will make even the (small) offerings of the vernal sacrifice acceptable. There is no blame.
Line 3
H46-L3		 九三：升虛邑。 Cending upwards (as into) an empty city.
Line 4
H46-L4		 六四：王用亨于岐山，吉无咎。 The king presents an offering at Mount Qi. Good fortune and no blame.
Line 5
H46-L5		 六五：貞吉，升階。 One is firmly correct, and therefore enjoying good fortune. He ascends the stairs (with all due ceremony).
Line 6
H46-L6		 上六：冥升，利于不息之貞。 Ascending in darkness. It is favorable to persist unceasingly in what is right.
