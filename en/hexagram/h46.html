JudgmentH46-JUDGMENT升：元亨，用見大人，勿恤，南征吉。Ascending brings supreme success. It is favorable to see the great person; do not worry. Advance toward the south brings good fortune.
Line 1#271H46-L1初六：允升，大吉。One is advancing upwards with the welcome (of those above him). Great good fortune follows.
Line 2#272H46-L2九二：孚乃利用禴，无咎。One is with that sincerity which will make even the (small) offerings of the vernal sacrifice acceptable. There is no blame.
Line 3#273H46-L3九三：升虛邑。Cending upwards (as into) an empty city.
Line 4#274H46-L4六四：王用亨于岐山，吉无咎。The king presents an offering at Mount Qi. Good fortune and no blame.
Line 5#275H46-L5六五：貞吉，升階。One is firmly correct, and therefore enjoying good fortune. He ascends the stairs (with all due ceremony).
Line 6#276H46-L6上六：冥升，利于不息之貞。Ascending in darkness. It is favorable to persist unceasingly in what is right.
