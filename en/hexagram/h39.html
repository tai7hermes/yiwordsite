蹇
Obstruction
H39 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision.

LineOriginalTranslation
JudgmentH39-JUDGMENT蹇：利西南，不利東北；利見大人，貞吉。Obstruction. The southwest is favorable; the northeast is not. It is favorable to see the great person. Perseverance brings good fortune.
Line 1H39-L1初六：往蹇，來譽。From the first line, broken, we learn that advance (on the part of its subject) will lead to (greater) difficulties, while remaining stationary will afford ground for praise.
Line 2H39-L2六二：王臣蹇蹇，匪躬之故。The minister of the king struggling with difficulty on difficulty, and not with a view to his own advantage.
Line 3H39-L3九三：往蹇來反。One is advancing, (but only) to (greater) difficulties. He remains stationary, and returns (to his former associates).
Line 4H39-L4六四：往蹇來連。One is advancing, (but only) to (greater) difficulties. He remains stationary, and unites (with the subject of the line above).
Line 5H39-L5九五：大蹇朋來。One is struggling with the greatest difficulties, while friends are coming to help him.
Line 6H39-L6上六：往蹇來碩，吉；利見大人。Going brings obstruction; returning brings great merit. Good fortune. It is favorable to see the great person.
