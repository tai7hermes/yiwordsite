蹇 Obstruction

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision. · hexagram_id: H39 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Judgment H39-JUDGMENT

Original：蹇：利西南，不利東北；利見大人，貞吉。

Translation：Obstruction. The southwest is favorable; the northeast is not. It is favorable to see the great person. Perseverance brings good fortune.

Line 1 H39-L1

Original：初六：往蹇，來譽。

Translation：From the first line, broken, we learn that advance (on the part of its subject) will lead to (greater) difficulties, while remaining stationary will afford ground for praise.

Line 2 H39-L2

Original：六二：王臣蹇蹇，匪躬之故。

Translation：The minister of the king struggling with difficulty on difficulty, and not with a view to his own advantage.

Line 3 H39-L3

Original：九三：往蹇來反。

Translation：One is advancing, (but only) to (greater) difficulties. He remains stationary, and returns (to his former associates).

Line 4 H39-L4

Original：六四：往蹇來連。

Translation：One is advancing, (but only) to (greater) difficulties. He remains stationary, and unites (with the subject of the line above).

Line 5 H39-L5

Original：九五：大蹇朋來。

Translation：One is struggling with the greatest difficulties, while friends are coming to help him.

Line 6 H39-L6

Original：上六：往蹇來碩，吉；利見大人。

Translation：Going brings obstruction; returning brings great merit. Good fortune. It is favorable to see the great person.
