Through Biting

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision. · hexagram_id: H21 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Judgment H21-JUDGMENT

Original：噬嗑：亨。利用獄。

Translation：Biting through the obstruction brings success. It is favorable to administer justice.

Line 1 H21-L1

Original：初九：屨校滅趾，无咎。

Translation：One with his feet in the stocks and deprived of his toes. There is no blame.

Line 2 H21-L2

Original：六二：噬膚滅鼻，无咎。

Translation：Biting through tender meat until the nose is covered. There is no blame.

Line 3 H21-L3

Original：六三：噬臘肉，遇毒；小吝，无咎。

Translation：Biting dried meat and finding something unpleasant. There is minor regret, but no serious blame.

Line 4 H21-L4

Original：九四：噬乾胏，得金矢，利艱貞，吉。

Translation：One gnawing the flesh dried on the bone, and getting the pledges of money and arrows. It is favorable to him to recognize the difficulty of his task and be firm, in which case good fortune follows.

Line 5 H21-L5

Original：六五：噬乾肉，得黃金，貞厲，无咎。

Translation：One gnawing at dried flesh, and finding the yellow gold. He should be steadfast and principled, realising the peril (of his position). There is no blame.

Line 6 H21-L6

Original：上九：何校滅耳，凶。

Translation：One wearing the cangue, and deprived of his ears. Misfortune follows.
