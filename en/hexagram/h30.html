Clinging
The
H30 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision.

LineOriginalTranslation
JudgmentH30-JUDGMENT離：利貞。亨。畜牝牛，吉。Clinging brings benefit through steadfastness. Raising a cow brings good fortune.
Line 1H30-L1初九：履錯然，敬之无咎。One ready to move with confused steps. But he treads at the same time reverently, and there will be no mistake.
Line 2H30-L2六二：黃離，元吉。One is in his place in yellow. Great good fortune follows.
Line 3H30-L3九三：日昃之離，不鼓缶而歌，則大耋之嗟，凶。One is in a position like that of the declining sun. Instead of playing on his instrument of earthenware, and singing to it, he utters the groans of an old man of eighty. Misfortune follows.
Line 4H30-L4九四：突如其來如，焚如，死如，棄如。The manner of its subject's coming. How abrupt it is, as with fire, with death, to be rejected (by all)!
Line 5H30-L5六五：出涕沱若，戚嗟若，吉。Tears flow in streams and there is sorrowful sighing. Good fortune.
Line 6H30-L6上九：王用出征，有嘉折首，獲匪其醜，无咎。The king employing its subject in his punitive expeditions. Achieving admirable (merit), he breaks (only) the chiefs (of the rebels). Where his prisoners were not their associates, he does not punish. There is no blame.
