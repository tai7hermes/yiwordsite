JudgmentH04-JUDGMENT蒙：亨。匪我求童蒙，童蒙求我。初筮告，再三瀆，瀆則不告。利貞。Progress is possible. The teacher does not seek the inexperienced student; the student comes to the teacher. A sincere first question receives instruction. Repeated questioning becomes disrespectful and is not answered. Steadfastness is favorable.
Line 1H04-L1初六：發蒙，利用刑人，用說桎梏，以往吝。(has respect to) the dispelling of ignorance. It is favorable to use punishment (for that purpose), and to remove the shackles (from the mind). But going on in that way (of punishment) will cause regret.
Line 2H04-L2九二：包蒙吉，納婦吉，子克家。One is exercising forbearance with the ignorant, in which good fortune follows; and admitting (even the goodness of women, which will also be fortunate. (He may be described also as) a son able to (sustain the burden of) his family.
Line 3H04-L3六三：勿用取女，見金夫，不有躬，无攸利。(seems to say) that one should not marry a woman whose emblem it might be, for that, when she sees a man of wealth, she will not keep her person from him, and in no wise will advantage come from her.
Line 4H04-L4六四：困蒙，吝。Bound in chains of ignorance. There will be regret.
Line 5H04-L5六五：童蒙，吉。A simple lad without experience. Good fortune follows.
Line 6H04-L6上九：擊蒙，不利為寇，利禦寇。Correcting ignorance should prevent harm, not cause it. Striking the inexperienced person is not favorable; protecting that person from harm is favorable.
