益 Increase

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision. · hexagram_id: H42 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

OriginalOriginalTranslation
Judgment
H42-JUDGMENT		 益：利有攸往。利涉大川。 Increase. It is favorable to have somewhere to go; it is favorable to cross the great river.
Line 1
H42-L1		 初九：利用為大作，元吉，无咎。 It is favorable to undertake a great work. Great good fortune; no blame.
Line 2
H42-L2		 六二：或益之，十朋之龜弗克違，永貞吉。王用享于帝，吉。 Someone adds ten pairs of tortoise shells, and this cannot be refused. Lasting steadfastness brings good fortune. The king presents an offering to the highest power. Good fortune.
Line 3
H42-L3		 六三：益之用凶事，无咎。有孚中行，告公用圭。 Increase comes through difficult events, yet there is no blame. Act sincerely and follow the middle path. Report to the prince with the jade tablet of office.
Line 4
H42-L4		 六四：中行，告公從。利用為依遷國。 One is pursuing the due course. His advice to his prince is followed. He can with advantage be relied on in such a movement as that of removing the capital.
Line 5
H42-L5		 九五：有孚惠心，勿問元吉。有孚惠我德。 With a sincere heart, bring benefit without asking. Great good fortune; others sincerely recognize the kindness.
Line 6
H42-L6		 上九：莫益之，或擊之，立心勿恆，凶。 No one increases him; someone strikes him. He does not keep his heart constant. Misfortune.
