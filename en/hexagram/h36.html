Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision.

LineOriginalTranslation
JudgmentH36-JUDGMENT明夷：利艱貞。Darkening of the light. It is favorable to remain steadfast in hardship.
Line 1H36-L1初九：明夷于飛，垂其翼。君子于行，三日不食，有攸往，主人有言。The light is darkened, like a bird flying with drooping wings. The noble person travels without eating for three days and may be criticized wherever they go.
Line 2H36-L2六二：明夷，夷于左股，用拯馬壯，吉。One is, (in the condition indicated by) Ming I, wounded in the left thigh. He saves himself by the strength of a (swift) horse; and is fortunate.
Line 3H36-L3九三：明夷于南狩，得其大首，不可疾貞。One is, (in the condition indicated by) Ming I, hunting in the south, and taking the great chief (of the darkness). He should not be eager to make (all) correct (at once).
Line 4H36-L4六四：入于左腹，獲明夷之心，于出門庭。One is (just) entered into the left side of the belly (of the dark land). (But) he is able to carry out the mind appropriate (in the condition indicated by) Ming 1, quitting the gate and courtyard (of the lord of darkness).
Line 5H36-L5六五：箕子之明夷，利貞。The count of K\ fulfilled the condition indicated by Ming I. It is favorable to be steadfast and principled.
Line 6H36-L6上六：不明晦，初登于天，后入于地。The case where there is no light, but (only) obscurity. (Its subject) had at first ascended to (the top of) the sky; his future will be to go into the earth.
