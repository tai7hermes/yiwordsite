Judgment
H64-JUDGMENT		 未濟：亨。小狐汔濟，濡其尾，无攸利。 Before Completion brings success. The young fox nearly crosses but wets its tail. Nothing is favorable.
Line 1
H64-L1		 初六：濡其尾，吝。 One is (like a fox) whose tail gets immersed. There will be regret.
Line 2
H64-L2		 九二：曳其輪，貞吉。 One is dragging back his (carriage-)wheel. With steadfast conduct good fortune follows.
Line 3
H64-L3		 六三：未濟，征凶，利涉大川。 One is, with (the state of things) not yet remedied, advancing on; which will lead to evil. But there will be advantage in (trying to) cross the great stream.
Line 4
H64-L4		 九四：貞吉，悔亡，震用伐鬼方，三年有賞于大國。 Steadfastness brings good fortune, and regret disappears. Act decisively in the campaign against the Demon Region. After three years, rewards come from the great kingdom.
Line 5
H64-L5		 六五：貞吉，无悔，君子之光，有孚，吉。 Steadfastness brings good fortune, with no regret. The noble person shines with sincerity. Good fortune.
Line 6
H64-L6		 上九：有孚于飲酒，无咎，濡其首，有孚失是。 There is sincerity in drinking wine. No blame. But if one wets the head, sincerity in what is right is lost.
