Completion Before

Judgment H64-JUDGMENT

Original：未濟：亨。小狐汔濟，濡其尾，无攸利。

Translation：Before Completion brings success. The young fox nearly crosses but wets its tail. Nothing is favorable.

Line 1 H64-L1

Original：初六：濡其尾，吝。

Translation：One is (like a fox) whose tail gets immersed. There will be regret.

Line 2 H64-L2

Original：九二：曳其輪，貞吉。

Translation：One is dragging back his (carriage-)wheel. With steadfast conduct good fortune follows.

Line 3 H64-L3

Original：六三：未濟，征凶，利涉大川。

Translation：One is, with (the state of things) not yet remedied, advancing on; which will lead to evil. But there will be advantage in (trying to) cross the great stream.

Line 4 H64-L4

Original：九四：貞吉，悔亡，震用伐鬼方，三年有賞于大國。

Translation：Steadfastness brings good fortune, and regret disappears. Act decisively in the campaign against the Demon Region. After three years, rewards come from the great kingdom.

Line 5 H64-L5

Original：六五：貞吉，无悔，君子之光，有孚，吉。

Translation：Steadfastness brings good fortune, with no regret. The noble person shines with sincerity. Good fortune.

Line 6 H64-L6

Original：上九：有孚于飲酒，无咎，濡其首，有孚失是。

Translation：There is sincerity in drinking wine. No blame. But if one wets the head, sincerity in what is right is lost.
