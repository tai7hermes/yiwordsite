Judgment H07-JUDGMENT

Original：師：貞丈人吉，无咎。

Translation：The army needs discipline and an experienced leader. Then there is good fortune and no blame.

Line 1 H07-L1

Original：初六：師出以律，否臧，凶。

Translation：The host going forth according to the rules (for such a movement). If these be not good, misfortune follows.

Line 2 H07-L2

Original：九二：在師中吉，无咎；王三錫命。

Translation：The leader stands at the center of the army. Good fortune and no blame. The king grants authority three times.

Line 3 H07-L3

Original：六三：師或輿尸，凶。

Translation：The host may, possibly, have many inefficient leaders. Misfortune follows.

Line 4 H07-L4

Original：六四：師左次，无咎。

Translation：The host in retreat. There is no blame.

Line 5 H07-L5

Original：六五：田有禽，利執言，无咎。長子帥師，弟子輿尸，貞凶。

Translation：There is game in the field, and it is favorable to capture it. There is no blame. Let the eldest son lead the army. If inexperienced followers carry the dead, steadfastness still ends in misfortune.

Line 6 H07-L6

Original：上六：大君有命，開國承家，小人勿用。

Translation：The great ruler delivering his charges, (appointing some) to be rulers of states, and others to undertake the headship of clans; but lesser people should not be employed (in such positions).
