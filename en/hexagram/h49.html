革
Revolution
H49 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision.

LineOriginalTranslation
JudgmentH49-JUDGMENT革：巳日乃孚，元亨。利貞。悔亡。Revolution is believed when its time has arrived. Supreme success; perseverance is favorable. Regret disappears.
Line 1H49-L1初九：鞏用黃牛之革。Bound securely with the hide of a yellow ox. The time for change has not yet come.
Line 2H49-L2六二：巳日乃革之，征吉，无咎。One is making his changes after some time has passed. Action taken will be fortunate. There is no blame.
Line 3H49-L3九三：征凶，貞厲，革言三就，有孚。That action taken by its subject will be evil. Though he be steadfast and principled, his position is perilous. If the change (he contemplates) have been three times fully discussed, he will be believed in.
Line 4H49-L4九四：悔亡，有孚改命，吉。Reason for regret disappearing (from its subject). He should be believed in; and though he change (existing) ordinances, good fortune follows.
Line 5H49-L5九五：大人虎變，未占有孚。The great person (producing his changes) as the tiger (does when he) changes (his stripes). Before he divines (and proceeds to action), faith has been reposed in him.
Line 6H49-L6上六：君子豹變，小人革面，征凶，居貞吉。The noble person changes like a leopard; the lesser person merely changes the face. Going forward brings misfortune; remaining correct brings good fortune.
