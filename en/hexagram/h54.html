Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision. · hexagram_id: H54 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Judgment H54-JUDGMENT

Original：歸妹：征凶，无攸利。

Translation：The Marrying Maiden. Going forward brings misfortune; nothing is favorable.

Line 1 H54-L1

Original：初九：歸妹以娣，跛能履，征吉。

Translation：The marrying maiden becomes a secondary wife. A lame person can still walk. Going forward brings good fortune.

Line 2 H54-L2

Original：九二：眇能視，利幽人之貞。

Translation：Her blind of one eye, and yet able to see. There will be advantage in her maintaining the steadfastness of a solitary widow.

Line 3 H54-L3

Original：六三：歸妹以須，反歸以娣。

Translation：The younger sister who was to be married off in a mean position. She returns and accepts an ancillary position.

Line 4 H54-L4

Original：九四：歸妹愆期，遲歸有時。

Translation：The younger sister who is to be married off protracting the time. She may be late in being married, but the time will come.

Line 5 H54-L5

Original：六五：帝乙歸妹，其君之袂，不如其娣之袂良，月幾望，吉。

Translation：King Di Yi gives his younger sister in marriage. The princess’s sleeves are less splendid than those of her attendant. The moon is almost full. Good fortune.

Line 6 H54-L6

Original：上六：女承筐无實，士刲羊无血，无攸利。

Translation：The woman holds a basket with no fruit; the man slaughters a sheep with no blood. Nothing is favorable.
