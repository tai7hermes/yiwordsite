Marrying
The

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision.

LineOriginalTranslation
JudgmentH54-JUDGMENT歸妹：征凶，无攸利。The Marrying Maiden. Going forward brings misfortune; nothing is favorable.
Line 1#319H54-L1初九：歸妹以娣，跛能履，征吉。The marrying maiden becomes a secondary wife. A lame person can still walk. Going forward brings good fortune.
Line 2#320H54-L2九二：眇能視，利幽人之貞。Her blind of one eye, and yet able to see. There will be advantage in her maintaining the steadfastness of a solitary widow.
Line 3#321H54-L3六三：歸妹以須，反歸以娣。The younger sister who was to be married off in a mean position. She returns and accepts an ancillary position.
Line 4#322H54-L4九四：歸妹愆期，遲歸有時。The younger sister who is to be married off protracting the time. She may be late in being married, but the time will come.
Line 5#323H54-L5六五：帝乙歸妹，其君之袂，不如其娣之袂良，月幾望，吉。King Di Yi gives his younger sister in marriage. The princess’s sleeves are less splendid than those of her attendant. The moon is almost full. Good fortune.
Line 6#324H54-L6上六：女承筐无實，士刲羊无血，无攸利。The woman holds a basket with no fruit; the man slaughters a sheep with no blood. Nothing is favorable.
