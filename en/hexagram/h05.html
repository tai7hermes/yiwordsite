需 Waiting

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision. · hexagram_id: H05 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Judgment H05-JUDGMENT

Original：需：有孚，光亨。貞吉，利涉大川。

Translation：With sincerity, success becomes clear. Steadfastness brings good fortune. It is favorable to cross the great river.

Line 1 H05-L1

Original：初九：需于郊，利用恆，无咎。

Translation：One is waiting in the distant border. It will be well for him constantly to maintain (the purpose thus shown), in which case there will be no blame.

Line 2 H05-L2

Original：九二：需于沙，小有言，終吉。

Translation：One is waiting on the sand (of the mountain stream). He will (suffer) the small (injury of) being spoken (against), but in the end good fortune follows.

Line 3 H05-L3

Original：九三：需于泥，致寇至。

Translation：One is in the mud (close by the stream). He as a result invites the approach of injury.

Line 4 H05-L4

Original：六四：需于血，出自穴。

Translation：One is waiting in (the place of) blood. But he will get out of the cavern.

Line 5 H05-L5

Original：九五：需于酒食，貞吉。

Translation：One is waiting amidst the appliances of a feast. Through his steadfast conduct good fortune follows.

Line 6 H05-L6

Original：上六：入于穴，有不速之客三人來，敬之終吉。

Translation：One is entered into the cavern. (But) there are three guests coming, without being urged, (to his help). If he receives them respectfully, good fortune follows in the end.
