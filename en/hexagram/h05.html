需
Waiting
H05 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision.

LineOriginalTranslation
JudgmentH05-JUDGMENT需：有孚，光亨。貞吉，利涉大川。With sincerity, success becomes clear. Steadfastness brings good fortune. It is favorable to cross the great river.
Line 1H05-L1初九：需于郊，利用恆，无咎。One is waiting in the distant border. It will be well for him constantly to maintain (the purpose thus shown), in which case there will be no blame.
Line 2H05-L2九二：需于沙，小有言，終吉。One is waiting on the sand (of the mountain stream). He will (suffer) the small (injury of) being spoken (against), but in the end good fortune follows.
Line 3H05-L3九三：需于泥，致寇至。One is in the mud (close by the stream). He as a result invites the approach of injury.
Line 4H05-L4六四：需于血，出自穴。One is waiting in (the place of) blood. But he will get out of the cavern.
Line 5H05-L5九五：需于酒食，貞吉。One is waiting amidst the appliances of a feast. Through his steadfast conduct good fortune follows.
Line 6H05-L6上六：入于穴，有不速之客三人來，敬之終吉。One is entered into the cavern. (But) there are three guests coming, without being urged, (to his help). If he receives them respectfully, good fortune follows in the end.
