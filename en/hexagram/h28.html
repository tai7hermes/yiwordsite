Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision. · hexagram_id: H28 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Judgment H28-JUDGMENT

Original：大過：棟橈，利有攸往，亨。

Translation：The supporting beam bends under the weight. It is favorable to go somewhere. Success is possible.

Line 1 H28-L1

Original：初六：藉用白茅，无咎。

Translation：One placing mats of the white mao grass under things set on the ground. There is no blame.

Line 2 H28-L2

Original：九二：枯楊生稊，老夫得其女妻，无不利。

Translation：A withered poplar puts forth new shoots; an old husband takes a young wife. Nothing is unfavorable.

Line 3 H28-L3

Original：九三：棟橈，凶。

Translation：A beam that is weak. Misfortune follows.

Line 4 H28-L4

Original：九四：棟隆，吉。有它吝。

Translation：A beam curving upwards. Good fortune follows. If (the subject of it) looks for other (help but that of line one), there will be cause for regret.

Line 5 H28-L5

Original：九五：枯楊生華，老婦得其士夫，无咎无譽。

Translation：A decayed willow producing flowers, or an old wife in possession of her young husband. There will be occasion neither for blame nor for praise.

Line 6 H28-L6

Original：上六：過涉滅頂，凶，无咎。

Translation：Wading so deeply that the water covers the head brings misfortune, but no blame.
