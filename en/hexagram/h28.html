OriginalOriginalTranslation
Judgment
H28-JUDGMENT		 大過：棟橈，利有攸往，亨。 The supporting beam bends under the weight. It is favorable to go somewhere. Success is possible.
Line 1
H28-L1		 初六：藉用白茅，无咎。 One placing mats of the white mao grass under things set on the ground. There is no blame.
Line 2
H28-L2		 九二：枯楊生稊，老夫得其女妻，无不利。 A withered poplar puts forth new shoots; an old husband takes a young wife. Nothing is unfavorable.
Line 3
H28-L3		 九三：棟橈，凶。 A beam that is weak. Misfortune follows.
Line 4
H28-L4		 九四：棟隆，吉。有它吝。 A beam curving upwards. Good fortune follows. If (the subject of it) looks for other (help but that of line one), there will be cause for regret.
Line 5
H28-L5		 九五：枯楊生華，老婦得其士夫，无咎无譽。 A decayed willow producing flowers, or an old wife in possession of her young husband. There will be occasion neither for blame nor for praise.
Line 6
H28-L6		 上六：過涉滅頂，凶，无咎。 Wading so deeply that the water covers the head brings misfortune, but no blame.
