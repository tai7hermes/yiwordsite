Still Keeping

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision. · hexagram_id: H52 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Judgment H52-JUDGMENT

Original：艮：艮其背，不獲其身，行其庭，不見其人，无咎。

Translation：Keeping Still: keep the back still so the self is no longer felt. Walk in the courtyard without seeing the people there. No blame.

Line 1 H52-L1

Original：初六：艮其趾，无咎，利永貞。

Translation：One is keeping his toes at rest. There will be no blame; but it will be favorable for him to be persistently steadfast and principled.

Line 2 H52-L2

Original：六二：艮其腓，不拯其隨，其心不快。

Translation：One is keeping the calves of his legs at rest. He cannot help (the subject of the line above) whom he follows, and is dissatisfied in his mind.

Line 3 H52-L3

Original：九三：艮其限，列其夤，厲薰心。

Translation：One is keeping his loins at rest, and separating the ribs (from the body below). The situation is perilous, and the heart glows with suppressed excitement.

Line 4 H52-L4

Original：六四：艮其身，无咎。

Translation：One is keeping his trunk at rest. There is no blame.

Line 5 H52-L5

Original：六五：艮其輔，言有序，悔亡。

Translation：Keep the jaws still and speak in an orderly way. Regret disappears.

Line 6 H52-L6

Original：上九：敦艮，吉。

Translation：Noble stillness. Good fortune.
