Still
Keeping
H52 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision.

LineOriginalTranslation
JudgmentH52-JUDGMENT艮：艮其背，不獲其身，行其庭，不見其人，无咎。Keeping Still: keep the back still so the self is no longer felt. Walk in the courtyard without seeing the people there. No blame.
Line 1H52-L1初六：艮其趾，无咎，利永貞。One is keeping his toes at rest. There will be no blame; but it will be favorable for him to be persistently steadfast and principled.
Line 2H52-L2六二：艮其腓，不拯其隨，其心不快。One is keeping the calves of his legs at rest. He cannot help (the subject of the line above) whom he follows, and is dissatisfied in his mind.
Line 3H52-L3九三：艮其限，列其夤，厲薰心。One is keeping his loins at rest, and separating the ribs (from the body below). The situation is perilous, and the heart glows with suppressed excitement.
Line 4H52-L4六四：艮其身，无咎。One is keeping his trunk at rest. There is no blame.
Line 5H52-L5六五：艮其輔，言有序，悔亡。Keep the jaws still and speak in an orderly way. Regret disappears.
Line 6H52-L6上九：敦艮，吉。Noble stillness. Good fortune.
