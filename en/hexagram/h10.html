履
Treading
H10 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision.

LineOriginalTranslation
JudgmentH10-JUDGMENT履虎尾，不咥人，亨。Treading on a tiger’s tail without being bitten. Progress and success.
Line 1#055爻H10-L1初九：素履，往无咎。One is treading his accustomed path. If he goes forward, there will be no blame.
Line 2#056爻H10-L2九二：履道坦坦，幽人貞吉。One is treading the path that is level and easy; a quiet and solitary man, to whom, if he is steadfast and principled, good fortune follows.
Line 3#057爻H10-L3六三：眇能視，跛能履，履虎尾，咥人，凶。武人為于大君。A one-eyed man (who thinks he) can see; a lame man (who thinks he) can walk well; one who treads on the tail of a tiger and is bitten. (All this indicates) ill fortune. We have a (mere) bravo acting the part of a great ruler.
Line 4#058爻H10-L4九四：履虎尾，愬愬終吉。One is treading on the tail of a tiger. He becomes full of apprehensive caution, and in the end good fortune follows.
Line 5#059爻H10-L5九五：夬履，貞厲。The resolute tread of its subject. Though he be steadfast and principled, there will be peril.
Line 6#060爻H10-L6上九：視履考祥，其旋元吉。Tells us to look at (the whole course) that is trodden, and examine the presage which that gives. If it be complete and without failure, there will be great good fortune.
