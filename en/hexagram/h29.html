Abysmal The

Judgment H29-JUDGMENT

Original：習坎：有孚，維心亨。行有尚。

Translation：Repeated danger. Sincerity in the heart brings success, and action earns respect.

Line 1 H29-L1

Original：初六：習坎，入于坎窞，凶。

Translation：One is in the double defile, and (yet) entering a cavern within it. Misfortune follows.

Line 2 H29-L2

Original：九二：坎有險，求小得。

Translation：In the abyss there is danger. Seek only a small gain.

Line 3 H29-L3

Original：六三：來之坎坎，險且枕，入于坎窞，勿用。

Translation：One is, whether he comes or goes (=descends or ascends), confronted by a defile. All is peril to him and unrest. (His endeavours) will lead him into the cavern of the pit. There should be no action (in such a case).

Line 4 H29-L4

Original：六四：樽酒簋貳，用缶，納約自牖，終无咎。

Translation：A simple jar of wine, two bowls of grain, and plain earthenware are passed through the window. Sincere simplicity brings no blame in the end.

Line 5 H29-L5

Original：九五：坎不盈，祗既平，无咎。

Translation：The water of the defile not yet full, (so that it might flow away); but order will (soon) be brought about. There is no blame.

Line 6 H29-L6

Original：上六：係用徽纆，寘于叢棘，三歲不得，凶。

Translation：One is bound with cords of three strands or two strands, and placed in the thicket of thorns. But in three years he does not learn the course for him to pursue. Misfortune follows.
