Judgment H58-JUDGMENT

Original：兌：亨。利貞。

Translation：Joy brings success. Perseverance is favorable.

Line 1 H58-L1

Original：初九：和兌，吉。

Translation：The pleasure of (inward) harmony. Good fortune follows.

Line 2 H58-L2

Original：九二：孚兌，吉，悔亡。

Translation：Sincere joy brings good fortune. Regret disappears.

Line 3 H58-L3

Original：六三：來兌，凶。

Translation：One is bringing round himself whatever can give pleasure. Misfortune follows.

Line 4 H58-L4

Original：九四：商兌，未寧，介疾有喜。

Translation：One is deliberating about what to seek his pleasure in, and not at rest. He borders on what would be injurious, but there will be cause for joy.

Line 5 H58-L5

Original：九五：孚于剝，有厲。

Translation：One is trusting in one who would injure him. The situation is perilous.

Line 6 H58-L6

Original：上六：引兌。

Translation：Seductive pleasure.
