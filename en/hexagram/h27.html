頤
Nourishment
H27 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision.

LineOriginalTranslation
JudgmentH27-JUDGMENT頤：貞吉。觀頤，自求口實。Nourishment. Steadfastness brings good fortune. Observe what a person nourishes and how they seek nourishment for themselves.
Line 1#157H27-L1初九：舍爾靈龜，觀我朵頤，凶。(seems to be thus addressed), 'You leave your efficacious tortoise, and look at me till your lower jaw hangs down.' Misfortune follows.
Line 2#158H27-L2六二：顛頤，拂經，于丘頤，征凶。One looking downwards for nourishment, which is contrary to what is proper; or seeking it from the height (above), advance towards which will lead to evil.
Line 3#159H27-L3六三：拂頤，貞凶，十年勿用，无攸利。Going against the right way of nourishment brings misfortune if continued. Do not act this way for ten years. Nothing is favorable.
Line 4#160H27-L4六四：顛頤吉，虎視眈眈，其欲逐逐，无咎。One looking downwards for (the power to) nourish. Good fortune follows. Looking with a tiger's downward unwavering glare, and with his desire that impels him to spring after spring, he will fall into no blame.
Line 5#161H27-L5六五：拂經，居貞吉，不可涉大川。One acting contrary to what is regular and proper; but if he remains in firmness, good fortune follows. He should not, (however, try to) cross the great stream.
Line 6#162H27-L6上九：由頤，厲吉，利涉大川。Him from whom comes the nourishing. His position is perilous, but good fortune follows. It is favorable to cross the great stream.
