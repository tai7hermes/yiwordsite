頤 Nourishment

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision. · hexagram_id: H27 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Judgment H27-JUDGMENT

Original：頤：貞吉。觀頤，自求口實。

Translation：Nourishment. Steadfastness brings good fortune. Observe what a person nourishes and how they seek nourishment for themselves.

Line 1 H27-L1

Original：初九：舍爾靈龜，觀我朵頤，凶。

Translation：(seems to be thus addressed), 'You leave your efficacious tortoise, and look at me till your lower jaw hangs down.' Misfortune follows.

Line 2 H27-L2

Original：六二：顛頤，拂經，于丘頤，征凶。

Translation：One looking downwards for nourishment, which is contrary to what is proper; or seeking it from the height (above), advance towards which will lead to evil.

Line 3 H27-L3

Original：六三：拂頤，貞凶，十年勿用，无攸利。

Translation：Going against the right way of nourishment brings misfortune if continued. Do not act this way for ten years. Nothing is favorable.

Line 4 H27-L4

Original：六四：顛頤吉，虎視眈眈，其欲逐逐，无咎。

Translation：One looking downwards for (the power to) nourish. Good fortune follows. Looking with a tiger's downward unwavering glare, and with his desire that impels him to spring after spring, he will fall into no blame.

Line 5 H27-L5

Original：六五：拂經，居貞吉，不可涉大川。

Translation：One acting contrary to what is regular and proper; but if he remains in firmness, good fortune follows. He should not, (however, try to) cross the great stream.

Line 6 H27-L6

Original：上九：由頤，厲吉，利涉大川。

Translation：Him from whom comes the nourishing. His position is perilous, but good fortune follows. It is favorable to cross the great stream.
