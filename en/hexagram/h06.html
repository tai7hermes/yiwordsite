訟 Conflict

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision. · hexagram_id: H06 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Judgment H06-JUDGMENT

Original：訟：有孚，窒，惕，中吉，終凶。利見大人，不利涉大川。

Translation：Even when your position is sincere, conflict brings obstruction. Caution in the middle brings good fortune; pressing the dispute to the end brings misfortune. It is favorable to see the great person, but not to cross the great river.

Line 1 H06-L1

Original：初六：不永所事，小有言，終吉。

Translation：One is not perpetuating the matter about which (the contention is). He will suffer the small (injury) of being spoken against, but the end will be fortunate.

Line 2 H06-L2

Original：九二：不克訟，歸而逋，其邑人三百戶无眚。

Translation：One is unequal to the contention. If he retire and keep concealed (where) the inhabitants of his city are (only) three hundred families, he will incur no blame.

Line 3 H06-L3

Original：六三：食舊德，貞厲，終吉。或從王事，无成。

Translation：One is keeping in the old place assigned for his support, and firmly correct. Perilous as the position is, good fortune follows in the end. Should he perhaps engage in the king's business, he will not (claim the merit of) achievement.

Line 4 H06-L4

Original：九四：不克訟，復即命渝，安貞吉。

Translation：One is unequal to the contention. He returns to (the study of Heaven's) ordinances, changes (his wish to contend), and rests in being steadfast and principled. Good fortune follows.

Line 5 H06-L5

Original：九五：訟，元吉。

Translation：One is contending; and with great good fortune.

Line 6 H06-L6

Original：上九：或錫之鞶帶，終朝三褫之。

Translation：Its subject may have the leathern belt conferred on him (by the sovereign), and thrice it will be taken from him in a morning.
