咸
Influence
H31 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision.

LineOriginalTranslation
JudgmentH31-JUDGMENT咸：亨。利貞。取女吉。Influence brings success. Steadfastness is favorable. Taking a young woman as wife brings good fortune.
Line 1H31-L1初六：咸其拇。One moving his great toes.
Line 2H31-L2六二：咸其腓，凶，居吉。One moving the calves of his leg. Misfortune follows. If he remains (quiet in his place), good fortune follows.
Line 3H31-L3九三：咸其股，執其隨，往吝。One moving his thighs, and keeping close hold of those whom he follows. Going forward (in this way) will cause regret.
Line 4H31-L4九四：貞吉悔亡，憧憧往來，朋從爾思。That steadfastness which will lead to good fortune, and prevent all reason for regret. If its subject be unsettled in his movements, (only) his friends will follow his purpose.
Line 5H31-L5九五：咸其脢，无悔。One moving the flesh along the spine above the heart. There will be no regret.
Line 6H31-L6上六：咸其輔，頰，舌。The influence shows in the jaws, cheeks, and tongue.
