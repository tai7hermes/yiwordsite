咸 Influence

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision. · hexagram_id: H31 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Judgment H31-JUDGMENT

Original：咸：亨。利貞。取女吉。

Translation：Influence brings success. Steadfastness is favorable. Taking a young woman as wife brings good fortune.

Line 1 H31-L1

Original：初六：咸其拇。

Translation：One moving his great toes.

Line 2 H31-L2

Original：六二：咸其腓，凶，居吉。

Translation：One moving the calves of his leg. Misfortune follows. If he remains (quiet in his place), good fortune follows.

Line 3 H31-L3

Original：九三：咸其股，執其隨，往吝。

Translation：One moving his thighs, and keeping close hold of those whom he follows. Going forward (in this way) will cause regret.

Line 4 H31-L4

Original：九四：貞吉悔亡，憧憧往來，朋從爾思。

Translation：That steadfastness which will lead to good fortune, and prevent all reason for regret. If its subject be unsettled in his movements, (only) his friends will follow his purpose.

Line 5 H31-L5

Original：九五：咸其脢，无悔。

Translation：One moving the flesh along the spine above the heart. There will be no regret.

Line 6 H31-L6

Original：上六：咸其輔，頰，舌。

Translation：The influence shows in the jaws, cheeks, and tongue.
