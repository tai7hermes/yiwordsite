Judgment H37-JUDGMENT

Original：家人：利女貞。

Translation：The Household. The perseverance of the woman is favorable.

Line 1 H37-L1

Original：初九：閑有家，悔亡。

Translation：Set clear limits within the household. Regret disappears.

Line 2 H37-L2

Original：六二：无攸遂，在中饋，貞吉。

Translation：One is taking nothing on herself, but in her central place attending to the preparation of the food. Through her steadfastness good fortune follows.

Line 3 H37-L3

Original：九三：家人嗃嗃，悔厲吉；婦子嘻嘻，終吝。

Translation：Strict discipline in the family may bring regret and danger, yet it ends well. If wife and children only laugh and chatter, humiliation follows in the end.

Line 4 H37-L4

Original：六四：富家，大吉。

Translation：She enriches the household. Great good fortune.

Line 5 H37-L5

Original：九五：王假有家，勿恤。吉。

Translation：The influence of the king extending to his family. There need be no anxiety; good fortune follows.

Line 6 H37-L6

Original：上九：有孚威如，終吉。

Translation：Sincerity joined with dignity brings good fortune in the end.
