JudgmentH37-JUDGMENT家人：利女貞。The Household. The perseverance of the woman is favorable.
Line 1#217H37-L1初九：閑有家，悔亡。Set clear limits within the household. Regret disappears.
Line 2#218H37-L2六二：无攸遂，在中饋，貞吉。One is taking nothing on herself, but in her central place attending to the preparation of the food. Through her steadfastness good fortune follows.
Line 3#219H37-L3九三：家人嗃嗃，悔厲吉；婦子嘻嘻，終吝。Strict discipline in the family may bring regret and danger, yet it ends well. If wife and children only laugh and chatter, humiliation follows in the end.
Line 4#220H37-L4六四：富家，大吉。She enriches the household. Great good fortune.
Line 5#221H37-L5九五：王假有家，勿恤。吉。The influence of the king extending to his family. There need be no anxiety; good fortune follows.
Line 6#222H37-L6上九：有孚威如，終吉。Sincerity joined with dignity brings good fortune in the end.
