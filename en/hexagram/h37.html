Judgment
H37-JUDGMENT		 家人：利女貞。 The Household. The perseverance of the woman is favorable.
Line 1
H37-L1		 初九：閑有家，悔亡。 Set clear limits within the household. Regret disappears.
Line 2
H37-L2		 六二：无攸遂，在中饋，貞吉。 One is taking nothing on herself, but in her central place attending to the preparation of the food. Through her steadfastness good fortune follows.
Line 3
H37-L3		 九三：家人嗃嗃，悔厲吉；婦子嘻嘻，終吝。 Strict discipline in the family may bring regret and danger, yet it ends well. If wife and children only laugh and chatter, humiliation follows in the end.
Line 4
H37-L4		 六四：富家，大吉。 She enriches the household. Great good fortune.
Line 5
H37-L5		 九五：王假有家，勿恤。吉。 The influence of the king extending to his family. There need be no anxiety; good fortune follows.
Line 6
H37-L6		 上九：有孚威如，終吉。 Sincerity joined with dignity brings good fortune in the end.
