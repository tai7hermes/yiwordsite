遯 Retreat

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision. · hexagram_id: H33 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Judgment H33-JUDGMENT

Original：遯：亨。小利貞。

Translation：Retreat brings success. In small matters, perseverance is favorable.

Line 1 H33-L1

Original：初六：遯尾，厲，勿用有攸往。

Translation：Retreat at the tail is dangerous. Do not undertake a journey.

Line 2 H33-L2

Original：六二：執之用黃牛之革，莫之勝說。

Translation：One is holding (his purpose) fast as if by a (thong made from the) hide of a yellow ox, which cannot be broken.

Line 3 H33-L3

Original：九三：系遯，有疾厲，畜臣妾吉。

Translation：One retiring but bound, — to his distress and peril. (If he were to deal with his binders as in) nourishing a servant or concubine, it would be fortunate for him.

Line 4 H33-L4

Original：九四：好遯君子吉，小人否。

Translation：One is retiring notwithstanding his likings. In a noble person this will lead to good fortune; a small man cannot attain to this.

Line 5 H33-L5

Original：九五：嘉遯，貞吉。

Translation：One is retiring in an admirable way. With steadfastness good fortune follows.

Line 6 H33-L6

Original：上九：肥遯，无不利。

Translation：A generous and unburdened retreat. Everything is favorable.
