遯
Retreat
H33 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision.

LineOriginalTranslation
JudgmentH33-JUDGMENT遯：亨。小利貞。Retreat brings success. In small matters, perseverance is favorable.
Line 1H33-L1初六：遯尾，厲，勿用有攸往。Retreat at the tail is dangerous. Do not undertake a journey.
Line 2H33-L2六二：執之用黃牛之革，莫之勝說。One is holding (his purpose) fast as if by a (thong made from the) hide of a yellow ox, which cannot be broken.
Line 3H33-L3九三：系遯，有疾厲，畜臣妾吉。One retiring but bound, — to his distress and peril. (If he were to deal with his binders as in) nourishing a servant or concubine, it would be fortunate for him.
Line 4H33-L4九四：好遯君子吉，小人否。One is retiring notwithstanding his likings. In a noble person this will lead to good fortune; a small man cannot attain to this.
Line 5H33-L5九五：嘉遯，貞吉。One is retiring in an admirable way. With steadfastness good fortune follows.
Line 6H33-L6上九：肥遯，无不利。A generous and unburdened retreat. Everything is favorable.
