隨 Following

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision. · hexagram_id: H17 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Judgment H17-JUDGMENT

Original：隨：元亨。利貞。无咎。

Translation：Following brings great progress. Steadfastness is favorable, and there is no blame.

Line 1 H17-L1

Original：初九：官有渝，貞吉。出門交有功。

Translation：Us one changing the object of his pursuit; but if he is steadfast and principled, there will be good fortune. Going beyond (his own) gate to find associates, he will achieve merit.

Line 2 H17-L2

Original：六二：系小子，失丈夫。

Translation：Us one who cleaves to the little boy, and lets go the man of age and experience.

Line 3 H17-L3

Original：六三：系丈夫，失小子。隨，有求得利，居貞。

Translation：Us one who cleaves to the man of age and experience, and lets go the little boy. Such following will get what it seeks; but it is favorable to adhere to what is steadfast and principled.

Line 4 H17-L4

Original：九四：隨有獲，貞凶。有孚在道，以明，何咎。

Translation：Following brings gains, but rigid persistence brings misfortune. If sincerity keeps you on a clear and proper path, there is no blame.

Line 5 H17-L5

Original：九五：孚于嘉，吉。

Translation：Us (the ruler) sincere in (fostering all) that is excellent. Good fortune follows.

Line 6 H17-L6

Original：上六：拘系之，乃從維之。王用亨于西山。

Translation：Sincerity is held and bound firmly. The king presents an offering on the western mountain.
