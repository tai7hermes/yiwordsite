隨
Following

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision.

LineOriginalTranslation
JudgmentH17-JUDGMENT隨：元亨。利貞。无咎。Following brings great progress. Steadfastness is favorable, and there is no blame.
Line 1H17-L1初九：官有渝，貞吉。出門交有功。Us one changing the object of his pursuit; but if he is steadfast and principled, there will be good fortune. Going beyond (his own) gate to find associates, he will achieve merit.
Line 2H17-L2六二：系小子，失丈夫。Us one who cleaves to the little boy, and lets go the man of age and experience.
Line 3H17-L3六三：系丈夫，失小子。隨，有求得利，居貞。Us one who cleaves to the man of age and experience, and lets go the little boy. Such following will get what it seeks; but it is favorable to adhere to what is steadfast and principled.
Line 4H17-L4九四：隨有獲，貞凶。有孚在道，以明，何咎。Following brings gains, but rigid persistence brings misfortune. If sincerity keeps you on a clear and proper path, there is no blame.
Line 5H17-L5九五：孚于嘉，吉。Us (the ruler) sincere in (fostering all) that is excellent. Good fortune follows.
Line 6H17-L6上六：拘系之，乃從維之。王用亨于西山。Sincerity is held and bound firmly. The king presents an offering on the western mountain.
