Apart Splitting

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision. · hexagram_id: H23 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Judgment H23-JUDGMENT

Original：剝：不利。有攸往。

Translation：Splitting apart. It is not favorable to go forward.

Line 1 H23-L1

Original：初六：剝牀以足，蔑貞凶。

Translation：One overturning the couch by injuring its legs. (The injury will go on to) the destruction of (all) steadfastness, and misfortune follows.

Line 2 H23-L2

Original：六二：剝牀以辨，蔑貞凶。

Translation：One overthrowing the couch by injuring its frame. (The injury will go on to) the destruction of (all) steadfastness, and misfortune follows.

Line 3 H23-L3

Original：六三：剝之，无咎。

Translation：One is among the overthrowers; but there will be no blame.

Line 4 H23-L4

Original：六四：剝牀以膚，凶。

Translation：One is having overthrown the couch, and (going to injure) the skin (of him who lies on it). Misfortune follows.

Line 5 H23-L5

Original：六五：貫魚，以宮人寵，无不利。

Translation：(its subject leading on the others like) a string of fishes, and (obtaining for them) the favor that lights on the inmates of the palace. There will be advantage in every way.

Line 6 H23-L6

Original：上九：碩果不食，君子得輿，小人剝廬。

Translation：One is (as) a great fruit which has not been eaten. The superior man finds (the people again) as a chariot carrying him. The lesser people (by their course) overthrow their own dwellings.
