Splitting

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision.

LineOriginalTranslation
JudgmentH23-JUDGMENT剝：不利。有攸往。Splitting apart. It is not favorable to go forward.
Line 1H23-L1初六：剝牀以足，蔑貞凶。One overturning the couch by injuring its legs. (The injury will go on to) the destruction of (all) steadfastness, and misfortune follows.
Line 2H23-L2六二：剝牀以辨，蔑貞凶。One overthrowing the couch by injuring its frame. (The injury will go on to) the destruction of (all) steadfastness, and misfortune follows.
Line 3H23-L3六三：剝之，无咎。One is among the overthrowers; but there will be no blame.
Line 4H23-L4六四：剝牀以膚，凶。One is having overthrown the couch, and (going to injure) the skin (of him who lies on it). Misfortune follows.
Line 5H23-L5六五：貫魚，以宮人寵，无不利。(its subject leading on the others like) a string of fishes, and (obtaining for them) the favor that lights on the inmates of the palace. There will be advantage in every way.
Line 6H23-L6上九：碩果不食，君子得輿，小人剝廬。One is (as) a great fruit which has not been eaten. The superior man finds (the people again) as a chariot carrying him. The lesser people (by their course) overthrow their own dwellings.
