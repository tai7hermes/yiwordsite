Wanderer The

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision. · hexagram_id: H56 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Judgment H56-JUDGMENT

Original：旅：小亨，旅貞吉。

Translation：The Traveler. Small success. The traveler’s perseverance brings good fortune.

Line 1 H56-L1

Original：初六：旅瑣瑣，斯其所取災。

Translation：The traveler busies himself with petty matters and as a result brings disaster upon himself.

Line 2 H56-L2

Original：六二：旅即次，懷其資，得童僕貞。

Translation：The stranger, occupying his lodging-house, carrying with him his means of livelihood, and provided with good and trusty servants.

Line 3 H56-L3

Original：九三：旅焚其次，喪其童僕，貞厲。

Translation：The stranger, burning his lodging-house, and having lost his servants. However steadfast and principled he (try to) be, he will be in peril.

Line 4 H56-L4

Original：九四：旅于處，得其資斧，我心不快。

Translation：The traveller in a resting-place, having (also) the means of livelihood and the axe, (but still saying)“ am not at ease in my mind.'

Line 5 H56-L5

Original：六五：射雉一矢亡，終以譽命。

Translation：One is shooting a pheasant. He will lose his arrow, but in the end he will obtain praise and a (high) charge.

Line 6 H56-L6

Original：上九：鳥焚其巢，旅人先笑后號咷。喪牛于易，凶。

Translation：A bird burns its nest. The traveler first laughs, then cries. He loses his ox through carelessness. Misfortune.
