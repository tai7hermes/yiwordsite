Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision.

LineOriginalTranslation
JudgmentH56-JUDGMENT旅：小亨，旅貞吉。The Traveler. Small success. The traveler’s perseverance brings good fortune.
Line 1H56-L1初六：旅瑣瑣，斯其所取災。The traveler busies himself with petty matters and as a result brings disaster upon himself.
Line 2H56-L2六二：旅即次，懷其資，得童僕貞。The stranger, occupying his lodging-house, carrying with him his means of livelihood, and provided with good and trusty servants.
Line 3H56-L3九三：旅焚其次，喪其童僕，貞厲。The stranger, burning his lodging-house, and having lost his servants. However steadfast and principled he (try to) be, he will be in peril.
Line 4H56-L4九四：旅于處，得其資斧，我心不快。The traveller in a resting-place, having (also) the means of livelihood and the axe, (but still saying)“ am not at ease in my mind.'
Line 5H56-L5六五：射雉一矢亡，終以譽命。One is shooting a pheasant. He will lose his arrow, but in the end he will obtain praise and a (high) charge.
Line 6H56-L6上九：鳥焚其巢，旅人先笑后號咷。喪牛于易，凶。A bird burns its nest. The traveler first laughs, then cries. He loses his ox through carelessness. Misfortune.
