渙 Dispersion

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision. · hexagram_id: H59 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Judgment H59-JUDGMENT

Original：渙：亨。王假有廟，利涉大川，利貞。

Translation：Dispersion brings success. The king approaches his ancestral temple. It is favorable to cross the great river; perseverance is favorable.

Line 1 H59-L1

Original：初六：用拯馬壯，吉。

Translation：One is engaged in rescuing (from the impending evil) and having (the assistance of) a strong horse. Good fortune follows.

Line 2 H59-L2

Original：九二：渙奔其机，悔亡。

Translation：One is, amid the dispersion, hurrying to his contrivance (for security). All reason for regret will disappear.

Line 3 H59-L3

Original：六三：渙其躬，无悔。

Translation：One is discarding any regard to his own person. There will be no regret.

Line 4 H59-L4

Original：六四：渙其群，元吉。渙有丘，匪夷所思。

Translation：One is scattering the (different) parties (in the state); which leads to great good fortune. From the dispersion (he collects again good men standing out, a crowd) like a mound, which is what ordinary men would not have thought of.

Line 5 H59-L5

Original：九五：渙汗其大號，渙王居，无咎。

Translation：Amid dispersal, great proclamations issue like sweat; the king disperses his treasury. No blame.

Line 6 H59-L6

Original：上九：渙其血，去逖出，无咎。

Translation：Dispersing blood: depart and keep at a distance. No blame.
