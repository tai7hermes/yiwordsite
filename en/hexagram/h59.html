渙
Dispersion
H59 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision.

LineOriginalTranslation
JudgmentH59-JUDGMENT渙：亨。王假有廟，利涉大川，利貞。Dispersion brings success. The king approaches his ancestral temple. It is favorable to cross the great river; perseverance is favorable.
Line 1H59-L1初六：用拯馬壯，吉。One is engaged in rescuing (from the impending evil) and having (the assistance of) a strong horse. Good fortune follows.
Line 2H59-L2九二：渙奔其机，悔亡。One is, amid the dispersion, hurrying to his contrivance (for security). All reason for regret will disappear.
Line 3H59-L3六三：渙其躬，无悔。One is discarding any regard to his own person. There will be no regret.
Line 4H59-L4六四：渙其群，元吉。渙有丘，匪夷所思。One is scattering the (different) parties (in the state); which leads to great good fortune. From the dispersion (he collects again good men standing out, a crowd) like a mound, which is what ordinary men would not have thought of.
Line 5H59-L5九五：渙汗其大號，渙王居，无咎。Amid dispersal, great proclamations issue like sweat; the king disperses his treasury. No blame.
Line 6H59-L6上九：渙其血，去逖出，无咎。Dispersing blood: depart and keep at a distance. No blame.
