賁 Grace

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision. · hexagram_id: H22 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Judgment H22-JUDGMENT

Original：賁：亨。小利有攸往。

Translation：Grace brings success. In small matters, it is favorable to have somewhere to go.

Line 1 H22-L1

Original：初九：賁其趾，舍車而徒。

Translation：One adorning (the way of) his feet. He can discard a carriage and walk on foot.

Line 2 H22-L2

Original：六二：賁其須。

Translation：One adorning his beard.

Line 3 H22-L3

Original：九三：賁如濡如，永貞吉。

Translation：One is with the appearance of being adorned and bedewed (with rich favors). But he should ever maintain his steadfastness, and good fortune follows.

Line 4 H22-L4

Original：六四：賁如皤如，白馬翰如，匪寇婚媾。

Translation：Plain white adornment. A white horse approaches as if it had wings. The visitor is not a robber but a marriage seeker.

Line 5 H22-L5

Original：六五：賁於丘園，束帛戔戔，吝，終吉。

Translation：One is adorned by (the occupants of) the heights and gardens. He bears his roll of silk, small and slight. He may appear stingy; but good fortune follows in the end.

Line 6 H22-L6

Original：上九：白賁，无咎。

Translation：One with white as his (only) ornament. There is no blame.
