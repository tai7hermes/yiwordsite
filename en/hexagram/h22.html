LineOriginalTranslation
JudgmentH22-JUDGMENT賁：亨。小利有攸往。Grace brings success. In small matters, it is favorable to have somewhere to go.
Line 1H22-L1初九：賁其趾，舍車而徒。One adorning (the way of) his feet. He can discard a carriage and walk on foot.
Line 2H22-L2六二：賁其須。One adorning his beard.
Line 3H22-L3九三：賁如濡如，永貞吉。One is with the appearance of being adorned and bedewed (with rich favors). But he should ever maintain his steadfastness, and good fortune follows.
Line 4H22-L4六四：賁如皤如，白馬翰如，匪寇婚媾。Plain white adornment. A white horse approaches as if it had wings. The visitor is not a robber but a marriage seeker.
Line 5H22-L5六五：賁於丘園，束帛戔戔，吝，終吉。One is adorned by (the occupants of) the heights and gardens. He bears his roll of silk, small and slight. He may appear stingy; but good fortune follows in the end.
Line 6H22-L6上九：白賁，无咎。One with white as his (only) ornament. There is no blame.
