Judgment H03-JUDGMENT

Original：屯：元亨，利貞。勿用有攸往，利建侯。

Translation：A promising beginning can lead to progress. Stay steadfast. Do not rush forward. It is favorable to appoint capable helpers.

Line 1 H03-L1

Original：初九：磐桓，利居貞，利建侯。

Translation：The difficulty (its subject has) in advancing. It will be favorable for him to abide steadfast and principled; favorable (also) to be made a feudal ruler.

Line 2 H03-L2

Original：六二：屯如邅如，乘馬班如，匪寇婚媾，女子貞不字，十年乃字。

Translation：Progress is difficult, and the horses turn back. The visitor is not a robber but a marriage seeker. The young woman remains steadfast and does not marry. After ten years, she marries.

Line 3 H03-L3

Original：六三：即鹿无虞，惟入于林中，君子幾不如舍，往吝。

Translation：Chasing deer without a guide leads only into the forest. The noble person sees the danger and stops. Going on would bring humiliation.

Line 4 H03-L4

Original：六四：乘馬班如，求婚媾，往，吉无不利。

Translation：(its subject as a lady), the horses of whose chariot appear in retreat. She seeks, however, (the help of) him who seeks her to be his wife. Advance will be fortunate; all will turn out favorablely.

Line 5 H03-L5

Original：九五：屯其膏；小貞吉，大貞凶。

Translation：The difficulties in the way of (its subject's) dispensing the rich favors that might be expected from him. With steadfast conduct good fortune follows in small things; (even) with them in great things misfortune follows.

Line 6 H03-L6

Original：上六：乘馬班如，泣血漣如。

Translation：One is with the horses of his chariot obliged to retreat, and weeping tears of blood in streams.
