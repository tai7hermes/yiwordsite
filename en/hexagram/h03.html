JudgmentH03-JUDGMENT屯：元亨，利貞。勿用有攸往，利建侯。A promising beginning can lead to progress. Stay steadfast. Do not rush forward. It is favorable to appoint capable helpers.
Line 1H03-L1初九：磐桓，利居貞，利建侯。The difficulty (its subject has) in advancing. It will be favorable for him to abide steadfast and principled; favorable (also) to be made a feudal ruler.
Line 2H03-L2六二：屯如邅如，乘馬班如，匪寇婚媾，女子貞不字，十年乃字。Progress is difficult, and the horses turn back. The visitor is not a robber but a marriage seeker. The young woman remains steadfast and does not marry. After ten years, she marries.
Line 3H03-L3六三：即鹿无虞，惟入于林中，君子幾不如舍，往吝。Chasing deer without a guide leads only into the forest. The noble person sees the danger and stops. Going on would bring humiliation.
Line 4H03-L4六四：乘馬班如，求婚媾，往，吉无不利。(its subject as a lady), the horses of whose chariot appear in retreat. She seeks, however, (the help of) him who seeks her to be his wife. Advance will be fortunate; all will turn out favorablely.
Line 5H03-L5九五：屯其膏；小貞吉，大貞凶。The difficulties in the way of (its subject's) dispensing the rich favors that might be expected from him. With steadfast conduct good fortune follows in small things; (even) with them in great things misfortune follows.
Line 6H03-L6上六：乘馬班如，泣血漣如。One is with the horses of his chariot obliged to retreat, and weeping tears of blood in streams.
