臨
Approach
H19 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision.

LineOriginalTranslation
JudgmentH19-JUDGMENT臨：元亨。利貞。至于八月有凶。Approach brings great progress. Steadfastness is favorable. By the eighth month, misfortune may come.
Line 1#109H19-L1初九：咸臨，貞吉。One is advancing in company (with the subject of the second line). Through his steadfastness good fortune follows.
Line 2#110H19-L2九二：咸臨，吉无不利。Approaching together brings good fortune. Everything is favorable.
Line 3#111H19-L3六三：甘臨，无攸利。既憂之，无咎。One well pleased (indeed) to advance, (but whose action) will be in no way favorable. If he iscome anxious about it (however), there will be no blame.
Line 4#112H19-L4六四：至臨，无咎。One advancing in the highest mode. There is no blame.
Line 5#113H19-L5六五：知臨，大君之宜，吉。The advance of wisdom, such as befits the great ruler. Good fortune follows.
Line 6#114H19-L6上六：敦臨，吉无咎。A generous and honest approach brings good fortune and no blame.
