臨 Approach

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision. · hexagram_id: H19 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Judgment H19-JUDGMENT

Original：臨：元亨。利貞。至于八月有凶。

Translation：Approach brings great progress. Steadfastness is favorable. By the eighth month, misfortune may come.

Line 1 H19-L1

Original：初九：咸臨，貞吉。

Translation：One is advancing in company (with the subject of the second line). Through his steadfastness good fortune follows.

Line 2 H19-L2

Original：九二：咸臨，吉无不利。

Translation：Approaching together brings good fortune. Everything is favorable.

Line 3 H19-L3

Original：六三：甘臨，无攸利。既憂之，无咎。

Translation：One well pleased (indeed) to advance, (but whose action) will be in no way favorable. If he iscome anxious about it (however), there will be no blame.

Line 4 H19-L4

Original：六四：至臨，无咎。

Translation：One advancing in the highest mode. There is no blame.

Line 5 H19-L5

Original：六五：知臨，大君之宜，吉。

Translation：The advance of wisdom, such as befits the great ruler. Good fortune follows.

Line 6 H19-L6

Original：上六：敦臨，吉无咎。

Translation：A generous and honest approach brings good fortune and no blame.
