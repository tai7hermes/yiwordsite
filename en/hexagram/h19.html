臨 Approach

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision. · hexagram_id: H19 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

OriginalOriginalTranslation
Judgment
H19-JUDGMENT		 臨：元亨。利貞。至于八月有凶。 Approach brings great progress. Steadfastness is favorable. By the eighth month, misfortune may come.
Line 1
H19-L1		 初九：咸臨，貞吉。 One is advancing in company (with the subject of the second line). Through his steadfastness good fortune follows.
Line 2
H19-L2		 九二：咸臨，吉无不利。 Approaching together brings good fortune. Everything is favorable.
Line 3
H19-L3		 六三：甘臨，无攸利。既憂之，无咎。 One well pleased (indeed) to advance, (but whose action) will be in no way favorable. If he iscome anxious about it (however), there will be no blame.
Line 4
H19-L4		 六四：至臨，无咎。 One advancing in the highest mode. There is no blame.
Line 5
H19-L5		 六五：知臨，大君之宜，吉。 The advance of wisdom, such as befits the great ruler. Good fortune follows.
Line 6
H19-L6		 上六：敦臨，吉无咎。 A generous and honest approach brings good fortune and no blame.
