節
Limitation
H60 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision.

LineOriginalTranslation
JudgmentH60-JUDGMENT節：亨。苦節不可貞。Limitation brings success. Bitter limitation cannot be maintained.
Line 1#355H60-L1初九：不出戶庭，无咎。Not going out beyond the inner courtyard. No blame.
Line 2#356H60-L2九二：不出門庭，凶。One is not quitting the courtyard inside his gate. Misfortune follows.
Line 3#357H60-L3六三：不節若，則嗟若，无咎。One is with no appearance of observing the (proper) regulations, in which case we will see him lamenting. But there will be no one to blame (but himself).
Line 4#358H60-L4六四：安節，亨。One is quietly and naturally (attentive to all) regulations. There will be progress and success.
Line 5#359H60-L5九五：甘節，吉；往有尚。One is sweetly and acceptably enacting his regulations. Good fortune follows. The onward progress with them will afford ground for admiration.
Line 6#360H60-L6上六：苦節，貞凶，悔亡。Bitter limitation: perseverance brings misfortune. Regret disappears.
