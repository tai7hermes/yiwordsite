節 Limitation

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision. · hexagram_id: H60 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Judgment H60-JUDGMENT

Original：節：亨。苦節不可貞。

Translation：Limitation brings success. Bitter limitation cannot be maintained.

Line 1 H60-L1

Original：初九：不出戶庭，无咎。

Translation：Not going out beyond the inner courtyard. No blame.

Line 2 H60-L2

Original：九二：不出門庭，凶。

Translation：One is not quitting the courtyard inside his gate. Misfortune follows.

Line 3 H60-L3

Original：六三：不節若，則嗟若，无咎。

Translation：One is with no appearance of observing the (proper) regulations, in which case we will see him lamenting. But there will be no one to blame (but himself).

Line 4 H60-L4

Original：六四：安節，亨。

Translation：One is quietly and naturally (attentive to all) regulations. There will be progress and success.

Line 5 H60-L5

Original：九五：甘節，吉；往有尚。

Translation：One is sweetly and acceptably enacting his regulations. Good fortune follows. The onward progress with them will afford ground for admiration.

Line 6 H60-L6

Original：上六：苦節，貞凶，悔亡。

Translation：Bitter limitation: perseverance brings misfortune. Regret disappears.
