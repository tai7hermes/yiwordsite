解 Deliverance

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision. · hexagram_id: H40 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Judgment H40-JUDGMENT

Original：解：利西南，无所往，其來復吉。有攸往，夙吉。

Translation：Release. The southwest is favorable. If there is nowhere to go, returning brings good fortune; if there is somewhere to go, setting out early brings good fortune.

Line 1 H40-L1

Original：初六：无咎。

Translation：No blame.

Line 2 H40-L2

Original：九二：田獲三狐，得黃矢，貞吉。

Translation：One is catch in hunting, three foxes, and obtain the yellow ( = golden) arrows. With steadfastness good fortune follows.

Line 3 H40-L3

Original：六三：負且乘，致寇至，貞吝。

Translation：A porter carries a burden while riding in a carriage, inviting robbers to come. Perseverance brings humiliation.

Line 4 H40-L4

Original：九四：解而拇，朋至斯孚。

Translation：Free yourself from the entanglement at your great toe; then a companion comes, and trust is possible.

Line 5 H40-L5

Original：六五：君子維有解，吉；有孚于小人。

Translation：The noble person alone can bring release. Good fortune; sincerity is as a result shown to lesser people.

Line 6 H40-L6

Original：上六：公用射隼，于高墉之上，獲之，无不利。

Translation：The prince shoots a hawk from the top of a high wall and captures it. Nothing is unfavorable.
