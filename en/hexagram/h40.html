Deliverance
H40 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision.

LineOriginalTranslation
JudgmentH40-JUDGMENT解：利西南，无所往，其來復吉。有攸往，夙吉。Release. The southwest is favorable. If there is nowhere to go, returning brings good fortune; if there is somewhere to go, setting out early brings good fortune.
Line 1H40-L1初六：无咎。No blame.
Line 2H40-L2九二：田獲三狐，得黃矢，貞吉。One is catch in hunting, three foxes, and obtain the yellow ( = golden) arrows. With steadfastness good fortune follows.
Line 3H40-L3六三：負且乘，致寇至，貞吝。A porter carries a burden while riding in a carriage, inviting robbers to come. Perseverance brings humiliation.
Line 4H40-L4九四：解而拇，朋至斯孚。Free yourself from the entanglement at your great toe; then a companion comes, and trust is possible.
Line 5H40-L5六五：君子維有解，吉；有孚于小人。The noble person alone can bring release. Good fortune; sincerity is as a result shown to lesser people.
Line 6H40-L6上六：公用射隼，于高墉之上，獲之，无不利。The prince shoots a hawk from the top of a high wall and captures it. Nothing is unfavorable.
