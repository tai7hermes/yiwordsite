JudgmentH62-JUDGMENT小過：亨。利貞。可小事，不可大事。飛鳥遺之音，不宜上宜下，大吉。Small Exceeding brings success; perseverance is favorable. Small matters may be done, great matters may not. The flying bird leaves a message: it is fitting to descend, not ascend. Great good fortune.
Line 1H62-L1初六：飛鳥以凶。Suggests (the idea of) a bird flying, (and ascending) till the issue is evil.
Line 2H62-L2六二：過其祖，遇其妣；不及其君，遇其臣；无咎。Passing the grandfather, one meets the grandmother; not reaching the ruler, one meets the minister. No blame.
Line 3H62-L3九三：弗過防之，從或戕之，凶。One is taking no extraordinary precautions against danger; and some in consequence finding opportunity to assail and injure him. Misfortune follows.
Line 4H62-L4九四：无咎，弗過遇之。往厲必戒，勿用永貞。One is falling into no blame, but meeting (the exigency of his situation), without exceeding (in his natural course). If he goes forward, there will be peril, and he must be cautious. There is no occasion to be using firmness perpetually.
Line 5H62-L5六五：密云不雨，自我西郊，公弋取彼在穴。(suggests the idea) of dense clouds, but no rain, coming from our borders in the west. It also (shows) the prince shooting his arrow, and taking the bird in a cave.
Line 6H62-L6上六：弗遇過之，飛鳥離之，凶，是謂災眚。Not meeting but passing beyond; the flying bird is caught in the net. Misfortune—this is calamity and harm.
