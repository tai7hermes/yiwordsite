Judgment H53-JUDGMENT

Original：漸：女歸吉，利貞。

Translation：Gradual Progress. The woman’s marriage brings good fortune. Perseverance is favorable.

Line 1 H53-L1

Original：初六：鴻漸于干，小子厲，有言，无咎。

Translation：The wild goose gradually approaches the shore. The young son is in danger and there is talk, but no blame.

Line 2 H53-L2

Original：六二：鴻漸于磐，飲食衎衎，吉。

Translation：The geese gradually approaching the large rocks, where they eat and drink joyfully and at ease. Good fortune follows.

Line 3 H53-L3

Original：九三：鴻漸于陸，夫征不復，婦孕不育，凶；利禦寇。

Translation：The wild goose reaches the dry plateau. The husband goes out and does not return; the wife is pregnant but does not raise the child. Misfortune. It is favorable to resist robbers.

Line 4 H53-L4

Original：六四：鴻漸于木，或得其桷，无咎。

Translation：The geese gradually advanced to the trees. They may light on the flat branches. There is no blame.

Line 5 H53-L5

Original：九五：鴻漸于陵，婦三歲不孕，終莫之勝，吉。

Translation：The geese gradually advanced to the high mound. (It suggests the idea of) a wife who for three years does not become pregnant; but in the end the natural issue cannot be prevented. Good fortune follows.

Line 6 H53-L6

Original：上九：鴻漸于陸，其羽可用為儀，吉。

Translation：The geese gradually advanced to the large heights (beyond). Their feathers can be used as ornaments. Good fortune follows.
