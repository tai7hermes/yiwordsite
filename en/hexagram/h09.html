Judgment H09-JUDGMENT

Original：小畜：亨。密雲不雨，自我西郊。

Translation：Small forces can restrain and shape events, bringing progress. Dense clouds gather, but no rain falls from our western region.

Line 1 H09-L1

Original：初九：復自道，何其咎，吉。

Translation：One is returning and pursuing his own course. What mistake should he fall into? Good fortune follows.

Line 2 H09-L2

Original：九二：牽復，吉。

Translation：One is drawn back to the proper path. Good fortune.

Line 3 H09-L3

Original：九三：輿說輻，夫妻反目。

Translation：A carriage, the strap beneath which has been removed, or of a husband and wife looking on each other with averted eyes.

Line 4 H09-L4

Original：六四：有孚，血去惕出，无咎。

Translation：One is possessed of sincerity. The danger of bloodshed is as a result averted, and his (ground for) apprehension dismissed. There is no blame.

Line 5 H09-L5

Original：九五：有孚攣如，富以其鄰。

Translation：One is possessed of sincerity, and drawing others to unite with him. Rich in resources, he employs his neighbors (in the same cause with himself).

Line 6 H09-L6

Original：上九：既雨既處，尚德載，婦貞厲，月幾望，君子征凶。

Translation：The rain has come and the situation has settled. The accumulated virtue should be respected. A wife who persists faces danger; the moon is almost full. If the noble person advances now, misfortune follows.
