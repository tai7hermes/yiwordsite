JudgmentH09-JUDGMENT小畜：亨。密雲不雨，自我西郊。Small forces can restrain and shape events, bringing progress. Dense clouds gather, but no rain falls from our western region.
Line 1H09-L1初九：復自道，何其咎，吉。One is returning and pursuing his own course. What mistake should he fall into? Good fortune follows.
Line 2H09-L2九二：牽復，吉。One is drawn back to the proper path. Good fortune.
Line 3H09-L3九三：輿說輻，夫妻反目。A carriage, the strap beneath which has been removed, or of a husband and wife looking on each other with averted eyes.
Line 4H09-L4六四：有孚，血去惕出，无咎。One is possessed of sincerity. The danger of bloodshed is as a result averted, and his (ground for) apprehension dismissed. There is no blame.
Line 5H09-L5九五：有孚攣如，富以其鄰。One is possessed of sincerity, and drawing others to unite with him. Rich in resources, he employs his neighbors (in the same cause with himself).
Line 6H09-L6上九：既雨既處，尚德載，婦貞厲，月幾望，君子征凶。The rain has come and the situation has settled. The accumulated virtue should be respected. A wife who persists faces danger; the moon is almost full. If the noble person advances now, misfortune follows.
