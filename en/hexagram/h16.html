豫 Enthusiasm

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision. · hexagram_id: H16 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Judgment H16-JUDGMENT

Original：豫：利建侯行師。

Translation：Enthusiasm. It is favorable to appoint helpers and mobilize the army.

Line 1 H16-L1

Original：初六：鳴豫，凶。

Translation：One is proclaiming his pleasure and satisfaction. Misfortune follows.

Line 2 H16-L2

Original：六二：介于石，不終日，貞吉。

Translation：One who is firm as a rock. (He sees a thing) without waiting till it has come to pass; with his steadfastness good fortune follows.

Line 3 H16-L3

Original：六三：盱豫，悔。遲有悔。

Translation：One looking up (for favors), while he indulges the feeling of pleasure and satisfaction. If he would understand!—If he is late in doing so, there will indeed be reason for regret.

Line 4 H16-L4

Original：九四：由豫，大有得。勿疑。朋盍簪。

Translation：Him from whom the harmony and satisfaction come. Great is the success which he obtains. He should not allow suspicions to enter his mind, and thus friends will gather around him.

Line 5 H16-L5

Original：六五：貞疾，恆不死。

Translation：One with a chronic complaint, but who lives on without dying.

Line 6 H16-L6

Original：上六：冥豫，成有渝，无咎。

Translation：One is with darkened mind devoted to the pleasure and satisfaction (of the time); but if he change his course even when (it may be considered as) completed, there will be no blame.
