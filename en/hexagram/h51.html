震 Shock

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision. · hexagram_id: H51 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Judgment H51-JUDGMENT

Original：震：亨。震來虩虩，笑言啞啞。震驚百里，不喪匕鬯。

Translation：Thunder brings success. Shock comes with fear, then laughter and conversation. It terrifies for a hundred miles, yet the sacrificial spoon and wine are not lost.

Line 1 H51-L1

Original：初九：震來虩虩，后笑言啞啞，吉。

Translation：One is, when the movement approaches, looking out and around with apprehension, and afterwards smiling and talking cheerfully. Good fortune follows.

Line 2 H51-L2

Original：六二：震來厲，億喪貝，躋于九陵，勿逐，七日得。

Translation：Thunder comes with danger. Let go of your valuables and climb to high ground. Do not chase what was lost; after seven days it will return.

Line 3 H51-L3

Original：六三：震蘇蘇，震行无眚。

Translation：Thunder causes agitation. Let the shock move one to action, and there is no blame.

Line 4 H51-L4

Original：九四：震遂泥。

Translation：One is, amid the startling movements, supinely sinking (deeper) in the mud.

Line 5 H51-L5

Original：六五：震往來厲，億无喪，有事。

Translation：One is going and coming amidst the startling movements (of the time), and always in peril; but perhaps he will not incur loss, and find business (which he can accomplish).

Line 6 H51-L6

Original：上六：震索索，視矍矍，征凶。震不于其躬，于其鄰，无咎。婚媾有言。

Translation：Thunder brings trembling and frightened glances. Going forward brings misfortune. If the shock has not reached oneself but only a neighbor, there is no blame; marriage brings criticism.
