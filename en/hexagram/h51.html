震
Shock
H51 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision.

LineOriginalTranslation
JudgmentH51-JUDGMENT震：亨。震來虩虩，笑言啞啞。震驚百里，不喪匕鬯。Thunder brings success. Shock comes with fear, then laughter and conversation. It terrifies for a hundred miles, yet the sacrificial spoon and wine are not lost.
Line 1H51-L1初九：震來虩虩，后笑言啞啞，吉。One is, when the movement approaches, looking out and around with apprehension, and afterwards smiling and talking cheerfully. Good fortune follows.
Line 2H51-L2六二：震來厲，億喪貝，躋于九陵，勿逐，七日得。Thunder comes with danger. Let go of your valuables and climb to high ground. Do not chase what was lost; after seven days it will return.
Line 3H51-L3六三：震蘇蘇，震行无眚。Thunder causes agitation. Let the shock move one to action, and there is no blame.
Line 4H51-L4九四：震遂泥。One is, amid the startling movements, supinely sinking (deeper) in the mud.
Line 5H51-L5六五：震往來厲，億无喪，有事。One is going and coming amidst the startling movements (of the time), and always in peril; but perhaps he will not incur loss, and find business (which he can accomplish).
Line 6H51-L6上六：震索索，視矍矍，征凶。震不于其躬，于其鄰，无咎。婚媾有言。Thunder brings trembling and frightened glances. Going forward brings misfortune. If the shock has not reached oneself but only a neighbor, there is no blame; marriage brings criticism.
