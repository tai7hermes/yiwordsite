Judgment H02-JUDGMENT

Original：坤：元亨。利牝馬之貞。君子有攸往，先迷後得主。利西南得朋，東北喪朋。安貞，吉。

Translation：A receptive and supportive approach brings progress. Stay as steady as a mare. If the noble person takes the lead, they may lose their way; by following what is right, they find guidance. Friends are found in the southwest and lost in the northeast. Calm steadfastness brings good fortune.

Line 1 H02-L1

Original：初六：履霜，堅冰至。

Translation：Frost underfoot signals that solid ice is approaching.

Line 2 H02-L2

Original：六二：直方大，不習无不利。

Translation：Straight, square, and broad. Without forcing the result, everything is favorable.

Line 3 H02-L3

Original：六三：含章，可貞。或從王事，无成有終。

Translation：Keep your abilities contained and remain steadfast. If you serve the king, do not claim the achievement, but bring the work to completion.

Line 4 H02-L4

Original：六四：括囊，无咎无譽。

Translation：A tied sack. There is neither blame nor praise.

Line 5 H02-L5

Original：六五：黃裳，元吉。

Translation：A yellow lower garment symbolizes balance and proper conduct. Great good fortune.

Line 6 H02-L6

Original：上六：龍戰于野，其血玄黃。

Translation：Dragons fight in the open country. Their blood is dark and yellow.

Use of Six H02-USE

Original：用六：利永貞。

Translation：When all sixes are used, lasting steadfastness is favorable.
