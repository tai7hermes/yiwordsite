JudgmentH02-JUDGMENT坤：元亨。利牝馬之貞。君子有攸往，先迷後得主。利西南得朋，東北喪朋。安貞，吉。A receptive and supportive approach brings progress. Stay as steady as a mare. If the noble person takes the lead, they may lose their way; by following what is right, they find guidance. Friends are found in the southwest and lost in the northeast. Calm steadfastness brings good fortune.
Line 1H02-L1初六：履霜，堅冰至。Frost underfoot signals that solid ice is approaching.
Line 2H02-L2六二：直方大，不習无不利。Straight, square, and broad. Without forcing the result, everything is favorable.
Line 3H02-L3六三：含章，可貞。或從王事，无成有終。Keep your abilities contained and remain steadfast. If you serve the king, do not claim the achievement, but bring the work to completion.
Line 4H02-L4六四：括囊，无咎无譽。A tied sack. There is neither blame nor praise.
Line 5H02-L5六五：黃裳，元吉。A yellow lower garment symbolizes balance and proper conduct. Great good fortune.
Line 6H02-L6上六：龍戰于野，其血玄黃。Dragons fight in the open country. Their blood is dark and yellow.
Use of SixH02-USE用六：利永貞。When all sixes are used, lasting steadfastness is favorable.
