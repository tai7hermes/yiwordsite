LineOriginalTranslation
JudgmentH32-JUDGMENT恆：亨，无咎。利貞，利有攸往。Constancy brings success and no blame. Perseverance is favorable; it is favorable to have somewhere to go.
Line 1H32-L1初六：浚恆，貞凶，无攸利。Seeking deep constancy at the outset: perseverance brings misfortune. Nothing is favorable.
Line 2H32-L2九二：悔亡。All reason for regret disappearing.
Line 3H32-L3九三：不恆其德，或承之羞，貞吝。One who does not continuously maintain his virtue. There are those who will impute this to him as a disgrace. However firm he may be, there will be ground for regret.
Line 4H32-L4九四：田无禽。A field where there is no game.
Line 5H32-L5六五：恆其德，貞，婦人吉，夫子凶。One is continuously maintaining the virtue indicated by it. In a wife this will be fortunate; in a husband, evil.
Line 6H32-L6上六：振恆，凶。Restless striving for constancy brings misfortune.
