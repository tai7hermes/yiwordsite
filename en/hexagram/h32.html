Judgment H32-JUDGMENT

Original：恆：亨，无咎。利貞，利有攸往。

Translation：Constancy brings success and no blame. Perseverance is favorable; it is favorable to have somewhere to go.

Line 1 H32-L1

Original：初六：浚恆，貞凶，无攸利。

Translation：Seeking deep constancy at the outset: perseverance brings misfortune. Nothing is favorable.

Line 2 H32-L2

Original：九二：悔亡。

Translation：All reason for regret disappearing.

Line 3 H32-L3

Original：九三：不恆其德，或承之羞，貞吝。

Translation：One who does not continuously maintain his virtue. There are those who will impute this to him as a disgrace. However firm he may be, there will be ground for regret.

Line 4 H32-L4

Original：九四：田无禽。

Translation：A field where there is no game.

Line 5 H32-L5

Original：六五：恆其德，貞，婦人吉，夫子凶。

Translation：One is continuously maintaining the virtue indicated by it. In a wife this will be fortunate; in a husband, evil.

Line 6 H32-L6

Original：上六：振恆，凶。

Translation：Restless striving for constancy brings misfortune.
