Together Gathering

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision. · hexagram_id: H45 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Judgment H45-JUDGMENT

Original：萃：亨。王假有廟，利見大人，亨。利貞。用大牲吉，利有攸往。

Translation：Gathering brings success. The king approaches his ancestral temple. It is favorable to see the great person and to persevere. Offering a great sacrifice brings good fortune; it is favorable to go forward.

Line 1 H45-L1

Original：初六：有孚不終，乃亂乃萃，若號一握為笑，勿恤，往无咎。

Translation：Sincere, but not to the end: first disorder, then gathering. If one calls out, a clasp of hands turns to laughter. Do not worry; going forward brings no blame.

Line 2 H45-L2

Original：六二：引吉，无咎，孚乃利用禴。

Translation：Allow yourself to be guided forward. Good fortune and no blame. With sincerity, even a small seasonal offering is accepted.

Line 3 H45-L3

Original：六三：萃如，嗟如，无攸利，往无咎，小吝。

Translation：One is striving after union and seeming to sigh, yet nowhere finding any advantage. If he goes forward, he will not err, though there may be some small cause for regret.

Line 4 H45-L4

Original：九四：大吉，无咎。

Translation：One is in such a state that, if he is greatly fortunate, he will receive no blame.

Line 5 H45-L5

Original：九五：萃有位，无咎。匪孚，元永貞，悔亡。

Translation：Gathering around a person in authority brings no blame. If trust is lacking, maintain great and lasting steadfastness; regret then disappears.

Line 6 H45-L6

Original：上六：齎咨涕洟，无咎。

Translation：Sighing and weeping. No blame.
