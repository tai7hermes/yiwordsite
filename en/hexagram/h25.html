无妄 Innocence

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision. · hexagram_id: H25 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Judgment H25-JUDGMENT

Original：无妄：元亨。利貞。其匪正有眚，不利有攸往。

Translation：Innocence brings great progress. Steadfastness is favorable. If a person is not acting rightly, misfortune follows, and no undertaking is favorable.

Line 1 H25-L1

Original：初九：无妄，往吉。

Translation：One is free from all insincerity. His advance will be accompanied with good fortune.

Line 2 H25-L2

Original：六二：不耕穫，不菑畬，則利有攸往。

Translation：Reaping without first plowing, and using a field without first clearing it. It is favorable to go forward without calculating personal gain.

Line 3 H25-L3

Original：六三：无妄之災，或系之牛，行人之得，邑人之災。

Translation：Unexpected trouble: a tethered ox is taken by a passerby, while the nearby people are wrongly blamed for the loss.

Line 4 H25-L4

Original：九四：可貞，无咎。

Translation：(a case) in which, if its subject can remain steadfast and principled, there will be no blame.

Line 5 H25-L5

Original：九五：无妄之疾，勿藥有喜。

Translation：An undeserved illness. Do not use medicine; recovery will bring joy.

Line 6 H25-L6

Original：上九：无妄，行有眚，无攸利。

Translation：One is free from insincerity, yet sure to fall into error, if he takes action. (His action) will not be favorable in any way.
