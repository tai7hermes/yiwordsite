无妄
Innocence
H25 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision.

LineOriginalTranslation
JudgmentH25-JUDGMENT无妄：元亨。利貞。其匪正有眚，不利有攸往。Innocence brings great progress. Steadfastness is favorable. If a person is not acting rightly, misfortune follows, and no undertaking is favorable.
Line 1H25-L1初九：无妄，往吉。One is free from all insincerity. His advance will be accompanied with good fortune.
Line 2H25-L2六二：不耕穫，不菑畬，則利有攸往。Reaping without first plowing, and using a field without first clearing it. It is favorable to go forward without calculating personal gain.
Line 3H25-L3六三：无妄之災，或系之牛，行人之得，邑人之災。Unexpected trouble: a tethered ox is taken by a passerby, while the nearby people are wrongly blamed for the loss.
Line 4H25-L4九四：可貞，无咎。(a case) in which, if its subject can remain steadfast and principled, there will be no blame.
Line 5H25-L5九五：无妄之疾，勿藥有喜。An undeserved illness. Do not use medicine; recovery will bring joy.
Line 6H25-L6上九：无妄，行有眚，无攸利。One is free from insincerity, yet sure to fall into error, if he takes action. (His action) will not be favorable in any way.
