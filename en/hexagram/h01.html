JudgmentH01-JUDGMENT乾：元亨。利貞。A strong beginning and smooth progress. It is favorable to remain steadfast and principled.
Line 1#001H01-L1初九：潛龍勿用。The dragon remains hidden. The time is not yet right for action.
Line 2#002H01-L2九二：見龍在田，利見大人。The dragon appears in the field. It is favorable to meet a great person.
Line 3#003H01-L3九三：君子終日乾乾，夕惕若；厲，无咎。The noble person works diligently all day and remains alert at night. The situation is dangerous, but careful conduct prevents blame.
Line 4#004H01-L4九四：或躍在淵，无咎。The dragon may leap upward or remain in the depths. Respond to the timing, and there is no blame.
Line 5#005H01-L5九五：飛龍在天，利見大人。The dragon flies in the sky. It is favorable to meet a great person.
Line 6#006H01-L6上九：亢龍，有悔。The dragon rises too high. Regret will follow.
Use of NineH01-USE用九：見羣龍无首，吉。When the group of dragons appears without anyone demanding to lead, there is good fortune.
