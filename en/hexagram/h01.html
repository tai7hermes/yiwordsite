Judgment H01-JUDGMENT

Original：乾：元亨。利貞。

Translation：A strong beginning and smooth progress. It is favorable to remain steadfast and principled.

Line 1 H01-L1

Original：初九：潛龍勿用。

Translation：The dragon remains hidden. The time is not yet right for action.

Line 2 H01-L2

Original：九二：見龍在田，利見大人。

Translation：The dragon appears in the field. It is favorable to meet a great person.

Line 3 H01-L3

Original：九三：君子終日乾乾，夕惕若；厲，无咎。

Translation：The noble person works diligently all day and remains alert at night. The situation is dangerous, but careful conduct prevents blame.

Line 4 H01-L4

Original：九四：或躍在淵，无咎。

Translation：The dragon may leap upward or remain in the depths. Respond to the timing, and there is no blame.

Line 5 H01-L5

Original：九五：飛龍在天，利見大人。

Translation：The dragon flies in the sky. It is favorable to meet a great person.

Line 6 H01-L6

Original：上九：亢龍，有悔。

Translation：The dragon rises too high. Regret will follow.

Use of Nine H01-USE

Original：用九：見羣龍无首，吉。

Translation：When the group of dragons appears without anyone demanding to lead, there is good fortune.
