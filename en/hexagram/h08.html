Together Holding

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision. · hexagram_id: H08 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Judgment H08-JUDGMENT

Original：比：吉。原筮元永貞，无咎。不寧方來，後夫凶。

Translation：Joining with others brings good fortune. Examine whether your character is broad, constant, and steadfast. If so, there is no blame. Those who are unsettled will come, but latecomers meet misfortune.

Line 1 H08-L1

Original：初六：有孚，比之，无咎。有孚盈缶，終來有它，吉。

Translation：One is seeking by his sincerity to win the attachment of his object. There is no blame. Let (the breast) be full of sincerity as an earthenware vessel is of its contents, and it will in the end bring other advantages.

Line 2 H08-L2

Original：六二：比之自內，貞吉。

Translation：The wish to unite with others comes from within. Steadfastness brings good fortune.

Line 3 H08-L3

Original：六三：比之匪人。

Translation：One seeking for union with such as ought not to be associated with.

Line 4 H08-L4

Original：六四：外比之，貞吉。

Translation：One seeking for union with the one beyond himself. With steadfastness good fortune follows.

Line 5 H08-L5

Original：九五：顯比。王用三驅，失前禽，邑人不誡，吉。

Translation：The king hunts from three sides and leaves an open path for the game ahead. The people do not warn one another. Good fortune.

Line 6 H08-L6

Original：上六：比之无首，凶。

Translation：Seeking union without a proper beginning ends in misfortune.
