夬 Breakthrough

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision. · hexagram_id: H43 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Judgment H43-JUDGMENT

Original：夬：揚于王庭，孚號，有厲，告自邑，不利即戎，利有攸往。

Translation：Breakthrough must be announced at the royal court. Sincerely proclaim the danger from one’s own city. Do not resort to arms; it is favorable to go forward.

Line 1 H43-L1

Original：初九：壯于前趾，往不勝為咎。

Translation：One is in (the pride of) strength advancing with his toes. He goes forward, but will not succeed. There is blame.

Line 2 H43-L2

Original：九二：惕號，莫夜有戎，勿恤。

Translation：One is full of apprehension and appealing (for sympathy and help). Late at night hostile measures may be (taken against him), but he need not be anxious about them.

Line 3 H43-L3

Original：九三：壯于頄，有凶。君子夬夬，獨行遇雨，若濡有慍，无咎。

Translation：Resoluteness in the cheekbones brings misfortune. The noble person decides to walk alone, is soaked by rain, and is resented; no blame.

Line 4 H43-L4

Original：九四：臀无膚，其行次且。牽羊悔亡，聞言不信。

Translation：The skin is gone from the buttocks, and walking is difficult. Let yourself be led like a sheep and regret disappears, but these words are not believed.

Line 5 H43-L5

Original：九五：莧陸夬夬，中行无咎。

Translation：Wild purslane must be uprooted with firm resolve. Walk the middle path, and there is no blame.

Line 6 H43-L6

Original：上六：无號，終有凶。

Translation：There is no cry for help. Misfortune in the end.
