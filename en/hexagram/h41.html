Decrease
H41 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision.

LineOriginalTranslation
JudgmentH41-JUDGMENT損：有孚，元吉。无咎，可貞，利有攸往。曷之用？二簋可用享。Decrease joined with sincerity brings great good fortune and no blame. Perseverance is possible; it is favorable to go somewhere. Two simple bowls may be used for the offering.
Line 1#241H41-L1初九：已事遄往，无咎，酌損之。One is suspending his own affairs, and hurrying away (to help the subject of the fourth line). He will commit no blame, but he should consider how far he should contribute of what is his (for the other).
Line 2#242H41-L2九二：利貞，征凶，弗損益之。That it will be favorable for its subject to maintain a steadfastness, and that action on his part will be evil. He can give increase (to his correlate) without taking from himself.
Line 3#243H41-L3六三：三人行，則損一人；一人行，則得其友。Of three men walking together, the number is diminished by one; and how one, walking, finds his friend.
Line 4#244H41-L4六四：損其疾，使遄有喜，无咎。One is diminishing the ailment under which he labours by making (the subject of the first line) hasten (to his help), and make him glad. There is no blame.
Line 5#245H41-L5六五：或益之，十朋之龜弗克違，元吉。Someone increases him by ten pairs of tortoise shells; this cannot be opposed. Great good fortune.
Line 6#246H41-L6上九：弗損益之，无咎，貞吉，利有攸往，得臣无家。Increasing others without diminishing oneself: no blame. Perseverance brings good fortune. It is favorable to go somewhere; one gains servants but no separate household.
