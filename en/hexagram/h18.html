Repairing

LineOriginalTranslation
JudgmentH18-JUDGMENT蠱：元亨。利涉大川。先甲三日，後甲三日。Repairing what has decayed brings great progress. It is favorable to cross the great river. Prepare for three days before beginning and remain attentive for three days afterward.
Line 1#103H18-L1初六：幹父之蠱，有子考，无咎，厲終吉。(a son) dealing with the troubles caused by his father. If he is an (able) son, the father will escape the blame of having erred. The position is perilous, but good fortune follows in the end.
Line 2#104H18-L2九二：幹母之蠱，不可貞。(a son) dealing with the troubles caused by his mother. He should not (carry) his steadfastness (to the utmost).
Line 3#105H18-L3九三：幹父之蠱，小有悔，无大咎。(a son) dealing with the troubles caused by his father. There may be some small reason for regret, but there will not be any great error.
Line 4#106H18-L4六四：裕父之蠱，往見吝。(a son) viewing indulgently the troubles caused by his father. If he goes forward, he will find cause to regret it.
Line 5#107H18-L5六五：幹父之蠱，用譽。(a son) dealing with the troubles caused by his father. He obtains the praise of using (the fit instrument for his work).
Line 6#108H18-L6上九：不事王侯，高尚其事。Us one who does not serve either king or feudal lord, but in a lofty spirit prefers (to attend to) his own affairs.
