Gentle
The
H57 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision.

LineOriginalTranslation
JudgmentH57-JUDGMENT巽：小亨。利有攸往。利見大人。Gentle Penetration. Small success. It is favorable to have somewhere to go and to see the great person.
Line 1H57-L1初六：進退，利武人之貞。One is (now) advancing, (now) receding. It would be favorable for him to have the steadfastness of a brave soldier.
Line 2H57-L2九二：巽在牀下，用史巫紛若，吉无咎。The representative of Sun beneath a couch, and employing diviners and exorcists in a way bordering on confusion. There will be crood fortune and no blame.
Line 3H57-L3九三：頻巽，吝。One is penetrating (only) by violent and repeated efforts. There will be regret.
Line 4H57-L4六四：悔亡，田獲三品。All reason for regret (in its subject) passed away. He takes game for its threefold use in his hunting.
Line 5H57-L5九五：貞吉悔亡，无不利。无初有終，先庚三日，后庚三日，吉。Perseverance brings good fortune and regret disappears. Nothing is unfavorable. No beginning, but an end; three days before the change and three days after it. Good fortune.
Line 6H57-L6上九：巽在牀下，喪其資斧，貞凶。Penetrating beneath the bed, one loses property and the axe. Perseverance brings misfortune.
