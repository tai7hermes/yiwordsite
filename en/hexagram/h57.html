Judgment
H57-JUDGMENT		 巽：小亨。利有攸往。利見大人。 Gentle Penetration. Small success. It is favorable to have somewhere to go and to see the great person.
Line 1
H57-L1		 初六：進退，利武人之貞。 One is (now) advancing, (now) receding. It would be favorable for him to have the steadfastness of a brave soldier.
Line 2
H57-L2		 九二：巽在牀下，用史巫紛若，吉无咎。 The representative of Sun beneath a couch, and employing diviners and exorcists in a way bordering on confusion. There will be crood fortune and no blame.
Line 3
H57-L3		 九三：頻巽，吝。 One is penetrating (only) by violent and repeated efforts. There will be regret.
Line 4
H57-L4		 六四：悔亡，田獲三品。 All reason for regret (in its subject) passed away. He takes game for its threefold use in his hunting.
Line 5
H57-L5		 九五：貞吉悔亡，无不利。无初有終，先庚三日，后庚三日，吉。 Perseverance brings good fortune and regret disappears. Nothing is unfavorable. No beginning, but an end; three days before the change and three days after it. Good fortune.
Line 6
H57-L6		 上九：巽在牀下，喪其資斧，貞凶。 Penetrating beneath the bed, one loses property and the axe. Perseverance brings misfortune.
