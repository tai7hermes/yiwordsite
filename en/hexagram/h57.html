Gentle The

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision. · hexagram_id: H57 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Judgment H57-JUDGMENT

Original：巽：小亨。利有攸往。利見大人。

Translation：Gentle Penetration. Small success. It is favorable to have somewhere to go and to see the great person.

Line 1 H57-L1

Original：初六：進退，利武人之貞。

Translation：One is (now) advancing, (now) receding. It would be favorable for him to have the steadfastness of a brave soldier.

Line 2 H57-L2

Original：九二：巽在牀下，用史巫紛若，吉无咎。

Translation：The representative of Sun beneath a couch, and employing diviners and exorcists in a way bordering on confusion. There will be crood fortune and no blame.

Line 3 H57-L3

Original：九三：頻巽，吝。

Translation：One is penetrating (only) by violent and repeated efforts. There will be regret.

Line 4 H57-L4

Original：六四：悔亡，田獲三品。

Translation：All reason for regret (in its subject) passed away. He takes game for its threefold use in his hunting.

Line 5 H57-L5

Original：九五：貞吉悔亡，无不利。无初有終，先庚三日，后庚三日，吉。

Translation：Perseverance brings good fortune and regret disappears. Nothing is unfavorable. No beginning, but an end; three days before the change and three days after it. Good fortune.

Line 6 H57-L6

Original：上九：巽在牀下，喪其資斧，貞凶。

Translation：Penetrating beneath the bed, one loses property and the axe. Perseverance brings misfortune.
