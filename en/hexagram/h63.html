JudgmentH63-JUDGMENT既濟：亨小。利貞。初吉終亂。After Completion. Success in small matters; perseverance is favorable. Good fortune at the beginning, disorder at the end.
Line 1H63-L1初九：曳其輪，濡其尾，无咎。The wheels are pulled back, and the fox’s tail becomes wet. There is no blame.
Line 2H63-L2六二：婦喪其茀，勿逐，七日得。A wife who has lost her (carriage-)screen. There is no occasion to go in pursuit of it. In seven days she will find it.
Line 3H63-L3九三：高宗伐鬼方，三年克之，小人勿用。Gao Zong attacked the Demon Region and conquered it after three years. Lesser people must not be employed.
Line 4H63-L4六四：繻有衣袽，終日戒。One is with rags provided against any leak (in his boat), and on his guard all day long.
Line 5H63-L5九五：東鄰殺牛，不如西鄰之禴祭，實受其福。One is (as) the neighbor in the east who slaughters an ox (for his sacrifice); but this is not equal to the (small) spring sacrifice of the neighbor in the west, whose sincerity receives the blessing.
Line 6H63-L6上六：濡其首，厲。The head is immersed. Danger.
