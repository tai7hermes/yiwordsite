Judgment H63-JUDGMENT

Original：既濟：亨小。利貞。初吉終亂。

Translation：After Completion. Success in small matters; perseverance is favorable. Good fortune at the beginning, disorder at the end.

Line 1 H63-L1

Original：初九：曳其輪，濡其尾，无咎。

Translation：The wheels are pulled back, and the fox’s tail becomes wet. There is no blame.

Line 2 H63-L2

Original：六二：婦喪其茀，勿逐，七日得。

Translation：A wife who has lost her (carriage-)screen. There is no occasion to go in pursuit of it. In seven days she will find it.

Line 3 H63-L3

Original：九三：高宗伐鬼方，三年克之，小人勿用。

Translation：Gao Zong attacked the Demon Region and conquered it after three years. Lesser people must not be employed.

Line 4 H63-L4

Original：六四：繻有衣袽，終日戒。

Translation：One is with rags provided against any leak (in his boat), and on his guard all day long.

Line 5 H63-L5

Original：九五：東鄰殺牛，不如西鄰之禴祭，實受其福。

Translation：One is (as) the neighbor in the east who slaughters an ox (for his sacrifice); but this is not equal to the (small) spring sacrifice of the neighbor in the west, whose sincerity receives the blessing.

Line 6 H63-L6

Original：上六：濡其首，厲。

Translation：The head is immersed. Danger.
