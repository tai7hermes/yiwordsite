OriginalOriginalTranslation
Judgment
H34-JUDGMENT		 大壯：利貞。 Great power. Perseverance is favorable.
Line 1
H34-L1		 初九：壯于趾，征凶，有孚。 One is manifesting his strength in his toes. But advance will lead to evil, — most certainly.
Line 2
H34-L2		 九二：貞吉。 That with steadfastness good fortune follows.
Line 3
H34-L3		 九三：小人用壯，君子用罔，貞厲。羝羊觸藩，羸其角。 A lesser person uses raw force; a noble person does not. Persistence is dangerous. A ram butts a fence and traps its horns.
Line 4
H34-L4		 九四：貞吉悔亡，藩決不羸，壯于大輿之輹。 Steadfastness brings good fortune, and regret disappears. The fence opens and the horns are freed. Strength is like the spokes of a large wagon.
Line 5
H34-L5		 六五：喪羊于易，无悔。 One who loses his ram(-like strength) in the ease of his position. (But) there will be no reason for regret.
Line 6
H34-L6		 上六：羝羊觸藩，不能退，不能遂，无攸利，艱則吉。 The ram butts the fence and can neither retreat nor advance. Nothing is favorable. Recognizing the difficulty brings good fortune.
