Judgment H34-JUDGMENT

Original：大壯：利貞。

Translation：Great power. Perseverance is favorable.

Line 1 H34-L1

Original：初九：壯于趾，征凶，有孚。

Translation：One is manifesting his strength in his toes. But advance will lead to evil, — most certainly.

Line 2 H34-L2

Original：九二：貞吉。

Translation：That with steadfastness good fortune follows.

Line 3 H34-L3

Original：九三：小人用壯，君子用罔，貞厲。羝羊觸藩，羸其角。

Translation：A lesser person uses raw force; a noble person does not. Persistence is dangerous. A ram butts a fence and traps its horns.

Line 4 H34-L4

Original：九四：貞吉悔亡，藩決不羸，壯于大輿之輹。

Translation：Steadfastness brings good fortune, and regret disappears. The fence opens and the horns are freed. Strength is like the spokes of a large wagon.

Line 5 H34-L5

Original：六五：喪羊于易，无悔。

Translation：One who loses his ram(-like strength) in the ease of his position. (But) there will be no reason for regret.

Line 6 H34-L6

Original：上六：羝羊觸藩，不能退，不能遂，无攸利，艱則吉。

Translation：The ram butts the fence and can neither retreat nor advance. Nothing is favorable. Recognizing the difficulty brings good fortune.
