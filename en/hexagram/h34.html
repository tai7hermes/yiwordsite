LineOriginalTranslation
JudgmentH34-JUDGMENT大壯：利貞。Great power. Perseverance is favorable.
Line 1#199H34-L1初九：壯于趾，征凶，有孚。One is manifesting his strength in his toes. But advance will lead to evil, — most certainly.
Line 2#200H34-L2九二：貞吉。That with steadfastness good fortune follows.
Line 3#201H34-L3九三：小人用壯，君子用罔，貞厲。羝羊觸藩，羸其角。A lesser person uses raw force; a noble person does not. Persistence is dangerous. A ram butts a fence and traps its horns.
Line 4#202H34-L4九四：貞吉悔亡，藩決不羸，壯于大輿之輹。Steadfastness brings good fortune, and regret disappears. The fence opens and the horns are freed. Strength is like the spokes of a large wagon.
Line 5#203H34-L5六五：喪羊于易，无悔。One who loses his ram(-like strength) in the ease of his position. (But) there will be no reason for regret.
Line 6#204H34-L6上六：羝羊觸藩，不能退，不能遂，无攸利，艱則吉。The ram butts the fence and can neither retreat nor advance. Nothing is favorable. Recognizing the difficulty brings good fortune.
