晉 Progress

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision. · hexagram_id: H35 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Judgment H35-JUDGMENT

Original：晉：康侯用錫馬蕃庶，晝日三接。

Translation：Progress: the secure prince is granted many horses and is received in audience three times in one day.

Line 1 H35-L1

Original：初六：晉如，摧如，貞吉。罔孚，裕无咎。

Translation：One wishing to advance, and (at the same time) kept back. He should be steadfast and principled, and good fortune follows. If trust be not reposed in him, he should maintain a large and generous mind, and there will be no blame.

Line 2 H35-L2

Original：六二：晉如，愁如，貞吉。受茲介福，于其王母。

Translation：One is with the appearance of advancing, and yet of being sorrowful. If he is steadfast and principled, good fortune follows. He will receive this great blessing from his grandmother.

Line 3 H35-L3

Original：六三：眾允，悔亡。

Translation：One is trusted by all (around him). All reason for regret will disappear.

Line 4 H35-L4

Original：九四：晉如鼫鼠，貞厲。

Translation：One is with the appearance of advancing, but like a marmot. However steadfast and principled he maybe, the position is one of peril.

Line 5 H35-L5

Original：六五：悔亡，失得勿恤，往吉无不利。

Translation：All reason for regret disappears (from its subject). (But) he should not concern himself about whether he will fail or succeed. To advance will be fortunate, and in every way favorable.

Line 6 H35-L6

Original：上九：晉其角，維用伐邑，厲吉无咎，貞吝。

Translation：Advancing with the horns may be used only to discipline one’s own city. Danger, yet good fortune and no blame; perseverance brings regret.
