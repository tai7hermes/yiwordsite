Judgment H44-JUDGMENT

Original：姤：女壯，勿用取女。

Translation：Coming to Meet. The woman is powerful; do not take such a woman as wife.

Line 1 H44-L1

Original：初六：系于金柅，貞吉，有攸往，見凶，羸豕孚踟躅。

Translation：Hold it fast with a metal brake. Steadfastness brings good fortune. Moving forward brings misfortune. The restless lean pig is certain to struggle.

Line 2 H44-L2

Original：九二：包有魚，无咎，不利賓。

Translation：One is with a wallet of fish. There is no blame. But it will not be well to let (the subject of the first line) go forward to the guests.

Line 3 H44-L3

Original：九三：臀无膚，其行次且，厲，无大咎。

Translation：One from whose buttocks the skin has been stripped so that he walks with difficulty. The position is perilous, but there will be no great error.

Line 4 H44-L4

Original：九四：包无魚，起凶。

Translation：One is with his wallet, but no fish in it. This will give rise to evil.

Line 5 H44-L5

Original：九五：以杞包瓜，含章，有隕自天。

Translation：A medlar tree overspreading the gourd (beneath it). If he keeps his brilliant qualities concealed, (a good issue) will descend (as) from Heaven.

Line 6 H44-L6

Original：上九：姤其角，吝，无咎。

Translation：Meeting at the horns. Humiliation, but no blame.
