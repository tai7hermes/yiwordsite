JudgmentH44-JUDGMENT姤：女壯，勿用取女。Coming to Meet. The woman is powerful; do not take such a woman as wife.
Line 1H44-L1初六：系于金柅，貞吉，有攸往，見凶，羸豕孚踟躅。Hold it fast with a metal brake. Steadfastness brings good fortune. Moving forward brings misfortune. The restless lean pig is certain to struggle.
Line 2H44-L2九二：包有魚，无咎，不利賓。One is with a wallet of fish. There is no blame. But it will not be well to let (the subject of the first line) go forward to the guests.
Line 3H44-L3九三：臀无膚，其行次且，厲，无大咎。One from whose buttocks the skin has been stripped so that he walks with difficulty. The position is perilous, but there will be no great error.
Line 4H44-L4九四：包无魚，起凶。One is with his wallet, but no fish in it. This will give rise to evil.
Line 5H44-L5九五：以杞包瓜，含章，有隕自天。A medlar tree overspreading the gourd (beneath it). If he keeps his brilliant qualities concealed, (a good issue) will descend (as) from Heaven.
Line 6H44-L6上九：姤其角，吝，无咎。Meeting at the horns. Humiliation, but no blame.
