觀
Contemplation
H20 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision.

LineOriginalTranslation
JudgmentH20-JUDGMENT觀：盥而不荐，有孚顒若。Contemplation is like washing the hands before an offering has been presented. Sincerity and dignity inspire respect.
Line 1H20-L1初六：童觀，小人无咎，君子吝。The looking of a lad; not blamable in men of inferior rank, but matter for regret in noble people.
Line 2H20-L2六二：窺觀，利女貞。One peeping out from a door. It would be favorable if it were (merely) the steadfastness of a female.
Line 3H20-L3六三：觀我生，進退。One looking at (the course of) his own life, to advance or recede (accordingly).
Line 4H20-L4六四：觀國之光，利用賓于王。One contemplating the glory of the kingdom. It will be favorable for him, being such as he is, (to seek) to be a guest of the king.
Line 5H20-L5九五：觀我生，君子无咎。One is contemplating his own life(-course). A superior man, he will (thus) fall into no blame.
Line 6H20-L6上九：觀其生，君子无咎。One is contemplating his character to see if it be indeed that of a noble person. He will not fall into error.
