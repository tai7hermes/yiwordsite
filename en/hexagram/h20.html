觀 Contemplation

Original text pending collation; translations under continuous revision. · hexagram_id: H20 · text_version: zhouyi-en-1.0.0 · review_status: draft

Judgment H20-JUDGMENT

Original：觀：盥而不荐，有孚顒若。

Translation：Contemplation is like washing the hands before an offering has been presented. Sincerity and dignity inspire respect.

Line 1 H20-L1

Original：初六：童觀，小人无咎，君子吝。

Translation：The looking of a lad; not blamable in men of inferior rank, but matter for regret in noble people.

Line 2 H20-L2

Original：六二：窺觀，利女貞。

Translation：One peeping out from a door. It would be favorable if it were (merely) the steadfastness of a female.

Line 3 H20-L3

Original：六三：觀我生，進退。

Translation：One looking at (the course of) his own life, to advance or recede (accordingly).

Line 4 H20-L4

Original：六四：觀國之光，利用賓于王。

Translation：One contemplating the glory of the kingdom. It will be favorable for him, being such as he is, (to seek) to be a guest of the king.

Line 5 H20-L5

Original：九五：觀我生，君子无咎。

Translation：One is contemplating his own life(-course). A superior man, he will (thus) fall into no blame.

Line 6 H20-L6

Original：上九：觀其生，君子无咎。

Translation：One is contemplating his character to see if it be indeed that of a noble person. He will not fall into error.
