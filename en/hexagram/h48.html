Judgment H48-JUDGMENT

Original：井：改邑不改井，无喪无得，往來井井。汔至亦未繘井。羸其瓶，凶。

Translation：The Well. The town may be changed, but the well cannot. It neither decreases nor increases. People come and go and draw from it. If the rope nearly reaches the water but the bucket is broken, misfortune.

Line 1 H48-L1

Original：初六：井泥不食，舊井无禽。

Translation：A well so muddy that men will not drink of it; or an old well to which neither birds (nor other creatures) resort.

Line 2 H48-L2

Original：九二：井谷射鮒，瓮敝漏。

Translation：A well from which by a hole the water escapes and flows away to the shrimps (and such small creatures among the grass), or one the water of which leaks away from a broken basket.

Line 3 H48-L3

Original：九三：井渫不食，為我心惻，可用汲，王明，并受其福。

Translation：The well has been cleaned, but no one drinks from it. This causes sorrow, because its water can be used. If the king is wise, everyone shares the blessing.

Line 4 H48-L4

Original：六四：井甃，无咎。

Translation：A well, the lining of which is well laid. There is no blame.

Line 5 H48-L5

Original：九五：井冽，寒泉食。

Translation：A clear, limpid well, (the waters from) whose cold spring are (freely) drunk.

Line 6 H48-L6

Original：上六：井收勿幕，有孚元吉。

Translation：The well is drawn from and left uncovered. Sincerity brings great good fortune.
