Hexagram 29 Line Image 4XIANG-U-29-05陽卦多陰，陰卦多陽，其故何也？陽卦奇，陰卦偶。其德行何也？陽一君而二民，君子之道也；陰二君而一民，小人之道也。In everyday terms, the idea is this: yang trigrams contain more yin lines, and yin trigrams contain more yang lines. Why is this? A yang trigram is odd, while a yin trigram is even. What does this imply about their conduct? In a yang trigram there is one ruler and two subjects; the way of the noble person. In a yin trigram there are two rulers and one subject; the way of the lesser person.
Hexagram 29 Line Image 5XIANG-U-29-06易曰：「憧憧往來，朋從爾思。」子曰：「天下何思何慮！天下同歸而殊途，一致而百慮，天下何思何慮！日往則月來，月往則日來，日月相推而明生焉。寒往則暑來，暑往則寒來，寒暑相推而歲成焉。往者，屈也；來者，信也。屈信相感而利生焉。尺蠖之屈，以求信也。龍蛇之蟄，以存身也。精義入神，以致用也。利用安身，以崇德也。過此以往，未之或知也。窮神知化，德之盛也。」The Yi says, “Restlessly coming and going, your friends follow your thoughts.” The Master said: “What thought or anxiety is there under heaven? Different paths lead to the same destination, and a hundred deliberations arrive at one result. What thought or anxiety is there under heaven? The sun departs and the moon comes; the moon departs and the sun comes. By their mutual succession, light is produced. Cold departs and heat comes; heat departs and cold comes. By their mutual succession, the year is completed. What departs contracts, and what comes expands; through the mutual influence of contraction and expansion, benefit arises. The inchworm contracts in order to extend; dragons and snakes hibernate in order to preserve themselves. Refining principles until they enter the subtle and applying them in practice brings usefulness; making use beneficial and settling oneself securely exalts virtue. Beyond this, perhaps nothing can be known. To exhaust the spiritual and understand transformation is the fullness of virtue.”.
Hexagram 29 Line Image 6XIANG-U-29-07易曰：「困于石，據于蒺蔾，入于其宮，不見其妻，凶。」子曰：「非所困而困焉，名必辱；非所據而據焉，身必危；既辱且危，死期將至，妻其可得見耶！」The Yi says, “Oppressed by rocks, the person rests on thorns. The person enters their house and does not see their wife: misfortune.” The Master said: “If one is oppressed by what should not oppress them, their name will surely be disgraced. If the person relies on what the person should not rely on, their person will surely be endangered. Once disgraced and endangered, death is near; how could the person see their wife?”.
Hexagram 30 Great ImageXIANG-U-30-01易曰：「公用射隼于高墉之上，獲之，无不利。」子曰：「隼者，禽也；弓矢者，器也。射之者，人也。君子藏器於身，待時而動，何不利之有。動而不括，是以出而有獲，語成器而動者也。」The Yi says, “The duke shoots a falcon atop a high wall and obtains it; nothing is unfavorable.” The Master said: “The falcon is a bird; the bow and arrow are instruments; the shooter is a person. The noble person keeps the instrument concealed on their person and waits for the proper time to act. How could anything be unfavorable? Because the person acts without obstruction, the person goes forth and obtains their object. This speaks of preparing an instrument and then acting.”.
Hexagram 30 Line Image 1XIANG-U-30-02子曰：「小人不恥不仁，不畏不義，不見利不勸，不威不懲，小懲而大誡，此小人之福也。易曰：『履校滅趾，无咎。』此之謂也。善不積不足以成名，惡不積不足以滅身，小人以小善為无益而弗為也，以小惡為无傷而弗去也；故惡積而不可揜，罪大而不可解。易曰：『何校滅耳，凶。』」The Master said: “The lesser person is not ashamed of inhumanity and does not fear unrighteousness. Unless the person sees profit, the person is not encouraged; unless threatened, the person is not corrected. A small punishment that produces a great warning is a blessing for the lesser person. The Yi says, ‘Their feet are placed in the stocks and their toes are destroyed; no blame.’ This is what it means. If good deeds are not accumulated, they are insufficient to establish a reputation; if misfortune deeds are not accumulated, they are insufficient to destroy a person. The lesser person thinks a small good brings no benefit and does not do it; the person thinks a small misfortune causes no harm and does not abandon it. Therefore misfortune accumulates until it cannot be concealed, and guilt grows until it cannot be released. The Yi says, ‘The person wears a cangue and their ears are destroyed: misfortune.’”.
Hexagram 30 Line Image 2XIANG-U-30-03子曰：「危者，安其位者也。亡者，保其存者也。亂者，有其治者也。是故君子安而不忘危，存而不忘亡，治而不忘亂，是以身安而國家可保也。易曰：『其亡其亡，繫于苞桑。』」The Master said: “One who keeps danger in mind secures their position; one who keeps ruin in mind preserves what the person possesses; one who keeps disorder in mind maintains good order. Therefore, while safe, the noble person does not forget danger; while secure, the person does not forget ruin; while orderly, the person does not forget disorder. In this way their person is safe and their state and household can be preserved. The Yi says, ‘Will it perish? Will it perish? Bind it to a cluster of mulberry shoots.’”.
Hexagram 30 Line Image 3XIANG-U-30-04子曰：「德薄而位尊，知小而謀大，力小而任重，鮮不及矣。易曰：『鼎折足，覆公餗，其形渥，凶。』言不勝其任也。」The Master said: “When virtue is meager but position is honored, knowledge is slight but plans are great, strength is weak but responsibility is heavy, disaster is seldom avoided. The Yi says, ‘The legs of the cauldron break; the duke’s meal is overturned, and their body is soaked: misfortune.’ It means that the person is unequal to their charge.”.
Hexagram 30 Line Image 4XIANG-U-30-05子曰：「知幾其神乎！君子上交不諂，下交不瀆，其知幾乎！幾者，動之微，吉之先見者也。君子見幾而作，不俟終日。易曰：『介于石，不終日，貞吉。』介如石焉，寧用終日，斷可識矣。君子知微知彰，知柔知剛，萬夫之望。」The Master said: “Is not the person who knows the subtle beginnings almost spirit-like? In relations with superiors, the noble person does not flatter; in relations with inferiors, the person is not contemptuous. Does the person not understand subtle beginnings? A subtle beginning is the faint start of movement and the earliest appearance of good or bad fortune. The noble person sees it and acts without waiting for the day to end. The Yi says, ‘Firm as a rock, not waiting for the day to end; steadfastness brings good fortune.’ Firm as a rock; why wait for the day to end? The judgment can be known. The noble person knows the hidden and the manifest, the yielding and the firm; the person is the hope of the multitude.”.
Hexagram 30 Line Image 5XIANG-U-30-06子曰：「顏氏之子，其殆庶幾乎！有不善，未嘗不知；知之，未嘗復行也。易曰：『不遠復，无祇悔，元吉。』天地絪縕，萬物化醇；男女構精，萬物化生。易曰：『三人行，則損一人；一人行，則得其友。』言致一也。」The Master said: “The son of the Yan family came close indeed! Whenever the person did something wrong, the person always recognized it; once the person recognized it, the person never repeated it. The Yi says, ‘Returning from not far away, without reaching remorse: great good fortune.’ Heaven and earth mingle their vital forces, and all things are transformed and enriched. Male and female unite their essences, and the myriad creatures are transformed and born. The Yi says, ‘When three people travel, one is lost; when one person travels, the person finds a companion.’ This speaks of bringing things to unity.”.
Hexagram 30 Line Image 6XIANG-U-30-07子曰：「君子安其身而後動，易其心而後語，定其交而後求。君子脩此三者，故全也。危以動，則民不與也。懼以語，則民不應也。无交而求，則民不與也。莫之與，則傷之者至矣。易曰：『莫益之，或擊之。立心勿恒，凶。』」The Master said: “The noble person settles their own person before taking action, makes their mind easy before speaking, and establishes their relationships before making requests. By cultivating these three things, the noble person remains whole. If the person acts from danger, the people will not join them. If the person speaks from fear, the people will not respond. If the person asks without established relationships, the people will not support them. When no one supports them, those who harm them will arrive. The Yi says, ‘No one benefits them; someone may strike them. The person does not keep their heart constant: misfortune.’”.
