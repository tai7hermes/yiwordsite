Hexagram 01 Great ImageXIANG-U-01-01天行健，君子以自強不息。Heaven, in its motion, gives the idea of strength. The noble person, following this image, nerves themself to ceaseless activity.
Hexagram 01 Line Image 1XIANG-U-01-02潛龍勿用，陽在下也。The image gives this practical lesson: the dragon lies hid in the deep; it is not the time for active doing: this appears from the strong and undivided lines being in the lowest place.
Hexagram 01 Line Image 2XIANG-U-01-03見龍在田，德施普也。This line or image shows that the dragon appears in the field: the diffusion of virtuous influence has been wide.
Hexagram 01 Line Image 3XIANG-U-01-04終日乾乾，反復道也。The image gives this practical lesson: active and vigilant all the day: this refers to the treading of the proper path over and over again.
Hexagram 01 Line Image 4XIANG-U-01-05或躍在淵，進无咎也。The person seems to be leaping up, but is still in the deep: if the person advance, there is no blame.
Hexagram 01 Line Image 5XIANG-U-01-06飛龍在天，大人造也。The dragon is on the wing in the sky: the great person rouses themself to their work.
Hexagram 01 Line Image 6XIANG-U-01-07亢龍有悔，盈不可久也。The dragon exceeds the proper limits; this will bring cause for regret: a state of fulness, that is, should not be indulged in long.
Hexagram 01 Line Image 7XIANG-U-01-08用九，天德不可為首也。The image gives this practical lesson: the same undivided line is used in all the places of this hexagram, but the attribute of heaven thereby denoted should not always take the foremost place.
Hexagram 02 Great ImageXIANG-U-02-01地勢坤，君子以厚德載物。The capacity and sustaining power of the earth is what is denoted by Kun. The noble person, following this image, with their large virtue supports men and things.
Hexagram 02 Line Image 1XIANG-U-02-02履霜堅冰，陰始凝也。馴致其道，至堅冰也。The person is treading on hoarfrost; the strong ice will come by and by: the cold air has begun to take form. Allow it to go on quietly according to its nature, and the hoarfrost will come to strong ice.
Hexagram 02 Line Image 2XIANG-U-02-03六二之動，直以方也。不習无不利，地道光也。The movement shown by the second yielding line, is from the straight line to the square. Its operation, without repeated effort, in every way beneficial, shows the brilliant result of the way of earth.
Hexagram 02 Line Image 3XIANG-U-02-04含章可貞，以時發也。或從王事，知光大也。The person keeps their excellence under restraint, but firmly maintains it: at the proper time the person will manifest it. The person may have occasion to engage in the kings service: great is the glory of their wisdom.
Hexagram 02 Line Image 4XIANG-U-02-05括囊无咎，慎不害也。The image gives this practical lesson: a sack tied up; there is no blame: this shows how, through carefulness, no injury will be received.
Hexagram 02 Line Image 5XIANG-U-02-06黃裳元吉，文在中也。The yellow lower-garment; this will bring great good fortune: this follows from that ornamental colours being in the right and central place.
Hexagram 02 Line Image 6XIANG-U-02-07龍戰于野，其道窮也。The dragons fight in the wild: the onward course shown by Kun is pursued to extremity.
Hexagram 02 Line Image 7XIANG-U-02-08用六永貞，以大終也。The lines are all weak and divided, as appears from the use of the number six: but those who are therefore represented becoming perpetually steadfast and principled, there will thereby be a great consummation.
Hexagram 03 Great ImageXIANG-U-03-01雲雷，屯；君子以經綸。The trigram representing clouds and that representing thunder form Zhun. The noble person, following this image, adjusts their measures of government as in sorting the threads of the warp and woof.
Hexagram 03 Line Image 1XIANG-U-03-02雖磐桓，志行正也。以貴下賤，大得民也。Although there is a difficulty in advancing, the mind of the person represented by the line is set on doing what is correct. While noble, the person humbles themself to the mean, and clearly gains the people.
Hexagram 03 Line Image 2XIANG-U-03-03六二之難，乘剛也。十年乃字，反常也。The difficulty to the person represented by the second yielding line, arises from its place over the undivided line below it. The union and children after ten years shows things resuming their regular course.
Hexagram 03 Line Image 3XIANG-U-03-04即鹿无虞，以從禽也。君子舍之，往吝窮也。One pursues the deer without the guidance of the forester: the person does so in their eagerness to follow the game. The noble person gives up the chase, knowing that if the person go forward the person will regret it: the person would be reduced to extremity.
Hexagram 03 Line Image 4XIANG-U-03-05求而往，明也。This line or image shows that going forward after such a search for a helper shows intelligence.
Hexagram 03 Line Image 5XIANG-U-03-06屯其膏，施未光也。Difficulty is experienced by the person represented by the fifth line in bestowing their rich favours: the extent to which they reach will not yet be conspicuous.
Hexagram 03 Line Image 6XIANG-U-03-07泣血漣如，何可長也。The person weeps tears of blood in streams: how can the state therefore emblemed continue long?
Hexagram 04 Great ImageXIANG-U-04-01山下出泉，蒙；君子以果行育德。The trigram representing a mountain, and beneath it that for a spring issuing forth form Mang. The noble person, following this image, strives to be resolute in their conduct and nourishes their virtue.
Hexagram 04 Line Image 1XIANG-U-04-02利用刑人，以正法也。This line or image shows that it is favorable to use punishment: the object being to bring under the influence of correcting law.
Hexagram 04 Line Image 2XIANG-U-04-03子克家，剛柔接也。A son able to sustain the burden of their family: as appears from the reciprocation between this strong line and the weak fifth line.
Hexagram 04 Line Image 3XIANG-U-04-04勿用取女，行不順也。This line or image shows that a woman such as is here represented should not be taken in marriage: her conduct is not agreeable to what is right.
Hexagram 04 Line Image 4XIANG-U-04-05困蒙之吝，獨遠實也。The image gives this practical lesson: the regret arising from ignorance bound in chains is due to the special distance of the person represented by this line from the solidity shown in lines 2 and 6.
Hexagram 04 Line Image 5XIANG-U-04-06童蒙之吉，順以巽也。The good fortune belonging to the simple lad without experience comes from their docility going on to humility.
Hexagram 04 Line Image 6XIANG-U-04-07利禦寇，上下順也。The image gives this practical lesson: advantage will come from warding off injury: the person represented by this line above and the ignorant below, all do and are done to in accordance with their nature.
Hexagram 05 Great ImageXIANG-U-05-01雲上於天，需；君子以飲食宴樂。The trigram for clouds ascending over that for the sky forms Xu. The noble person, following this image, eats and drinks, feasts and enjoys themself as if there were nothing else to employ them.
Hexagram 05 Line Image 1XIANG-U-05-02需于郊，不犯難行也。利用恆，无咎，未失常也。The person is waiting in the distant border: the person makes no movement to encounter rashly the difficulties of the situation. It will be beneficial for them constantly to maintain the purpose therefore shown, in which case there is no blame: the person will not fail to pursue that regular course.
Hexagram 05 Line Image 2XIANG-U-05-03需于沙，衍在中也。雖小有言，以終吉也。The person is waiting on the sand: the person occupies their position in the centre with a generous forbearance. Though the person suffer the small injury of being spoken against, the person will bring things to a good issue.
Hexagram 05 Line Image 3XIANG-U-05-04需于泥，災在外也。自我致寇，敬慎不敗也。The person is waiting in the mud: serious trouble is close at hand, and as it were in the outer trigram. The person themself invites the approach of injury: if the person be reverent and careful, the person will not be worsted.
Hexagram 05 Line Image 4XIANG-U-05-05需于血，順以聽也。The person is waiting in the place of blood: the person accommodates themself to the circumstances of the time, and hearkens to its requirements.
Hexagram 05 Line Image 5XIANG-U-05-06酒食，貞吉，以中正也。The appliances of a feast, and the good fortune through being steadfast and right, are indicated, by the position in the central and correct place.
Hexagram 05 Line Image 6XIANG-U-05-07不速之客來，敬之終吉。雖不當位，未大失也。Guests come unurged to give their help, and if the person represented by the line receive them respectfully, this brings good fortune in the end: though the occupant and the place are not suited to each other, there has been no great failure in what has been done.
Hexagram 06 Great ImageXIANG-U-06-01天與水違行，訟；君子以作事謀始。The trigram representing heaven and that representing water, moving away from each other, form Song. The noble person, following this image, in the transaction of affairs takes good counsel about their first steps.
Hexagram 06 Line Image 1XIANG-U-06-02不永所事，訟不可長也。雖小有言，其辯明也。The person does not perpetuate the matter about which the contention is: contention should not be prolonged. Although the person may suffer the small injury of being spoken against, their argument is clear.
Hexagram 06 Line Image 2XIANG-U-06-03不克訟，歸逋竄也。自下訟上，患至掇也。The person is unequal to the contention; the person retires and keeps concealed, stealthily withdrawing from it: for them from their lower place to contend with the stronger one above, would be to invite serious trouble, as if the person brought it with their hand to themself.
Hexagram 06 Line Image 3XIANG-U-06-04食舊德，從上吉也。The person confines themself to the support assigned to them of old: therefore following those above them, the person will have good fortune.
Hexagram 06 Line Image 4XIANG-U-06-05復即命渝，安貞，不失也。The person returns to the study of Heavens ordinances, changes their wish to contend, and rests in being steadfast and right: the person does not fail in doing what is right.
Hexagram 06 Line Image 5XIANG-U-06-06訟，元吉，以中正也。The person contends; and with great fortune: this is shown by their holding the due mean and being in the correct place.
Hexagram 06 Line Image 6XIANG-U-06-07以訟受服，亦不足敬也。The person receives the robe through their contention: but still the person is not deserving of respect.
Hexagram 07 Great ImageXIANG-U-07-01地中有水，師；君子以容民畜衆。The trigram representing the earth and in the midst of it that representing water, form Shi. The noble person, following this image, nourishes and educates the people, and collects from among them the multitudes of the hosts.
Hexagram 07 Line Image 1XIANG-U-07-02師出以律；失律，凶也。The host goes forth according to the rules for such a movement: if those rules be not observed, this brings misfortune.
Hexagram 07 Line Image 2XIANG-U-07-03在師中吉，承天寵也。王三錫命，懷萬邦也。The person is in the midst of the host, and this brings good fortune: the person has received the favour of Heaven. The king has thrice conveyed to them the orders of their favour: the king cherishes the myriad regions in their heart.
Hexagram 07 Line Image 3XIANG-U-07-04師或輿尸，大无功也。The image gives this practical lesson: the host with the possibility of its having many idle leaders: great will be its want of success.
Hexagram 07 Line Image 4XIANG-U-07-05左次无咎，未失常也。This line or image shows that the host is in retreat; but there is no error: there has been no failure in the regular course.
Hexagram 07 Line Image 5XIANG-U-07-06長子帥師，以中行也。弟子輿尸，使不當也。The oldest son leads the host: its movements are directed by them in accordance with their position in the centre. Younger men idly occupy their positions: the employment of such men is improper.
Hexagram 07 Line Image 6XIANG-U-07-07大君有命，以正功也。小人勿用，必亂邦也。The great ruler delivers their charges: thereby the person rightly apportions merit. Small men should not be employed: they are sure to throw the states into confusion.
Hexagram 08 Great ImageXIANG-U-08-01地上有水，比；先王以建萬國，親諸侯。The trigram representing the earth, and over it that representing water, form Bi. The ancient kings, following this image, established the various states and maintained an affectionate relation to their princes.
Hexagram 08 Line Image 1XIANG-U-08-02比之初六，有它吉也。From the seeking union with its object shown in the first yielding line, this will bring other advantages.
Hexagram 08 Line Image 2XIANG-U-08-03比之自內，不自失也。The movement towards union and attachment proceeds from the inward mind: the party concerned does not fail in what is proper to themself.
Hexagram 08 Line Image 3XIANG-U-08-04比之匪人，不亦傷乎！This line or image shows that union is sought with such as ought not to be associated with: but will not injury be the result?
Hexagram 08 Line Image 4XIANG-U-08-05外比於賢，以從上也。Union is sought by the party intended here with one beyond themself, and in this case with a worthy object: the person is following the ruler above them.
Hexagram 08 Line Image 5XIANG-U-08-06顯比之吉，位正中也。舍逆取順，失前禽也。邑人不誡，上使中也。The good fortune belonging to the most illustrious instance of seeking union and attachment appears in the correct and central position of the fifth firm line.
Hexagram 08 Line Image 6XIANG-U-08-07比之无首，无所終也。The person seeks union and attachment without taking the first step to such an end: there is no possibility of a good issue.
Hexagram 09 Great ImageXIANG-U-09-01風行天上，小畜，君子以懿文德。The trigram representing the sky, and that representing wind moving above it, form Xiao Xu. The noble person, following this image, adorns the outward manifestation of their virtue.
Hexagram 09 Line Image 1XIANG-U-09-02復自道，其義吉也。The person returns and pursues their own path: it is right that there should be good fortune.
Hexagram 09 Line Image 2XIANG-U-09-03牽復在中，亦不自失也。By the attraction of the person represented by the former line the person returns to its own course, and is in the central place: neither will the person err in what is due from them.
Hexagram 09 Line Image 3XIANG-U-09-04夫妻反目，不能正室也。Husband and wife look on each other with averted eyes: the person represented by line three is like a husband who cannot maintain correctly their relations with their wife.
Hexagram 09 Line Image 4XIANG-U-09-05有孚。惕出。上合志也。The person is possessed of sincerity; their ground for apprehension is dismissed: the subjects of the lines above agree in aim with them.
Hexagram 09 Line Image 5XIANG-U-09-06有孚攣如，不獨富也。The person is possessed of sincerity, and draws others to unite with them: the person does not use only their own rich resources.
Hexagram 09 Line Image 6XIANG-U-09-07既雨既處，德積載也。君子征凶，有所疑也。The rain has fallen and the onward progress is stayed: the power denoted in the figure has accumulated to the full. If the noble person prosecute their measures, this brings misfortune: the person will find themself obstructed.
Hexagram 10 Great ImageXIANG-U-10-01上天下澤，履；君子以辨上下，定民志。The trigram representing the sky above, and below it that representing the waters of a marsh, form Li. The noble person, following this image, discriminates between high and low, and gives settlement to the aims of the people.
Hexagram 10 Line Image 1XIANG-U-10-02素履之往，獨行願也。The person treads their accustomed path and goes forward: singly and exclusively the person carries out their long-cherished wishes.
Hexagram 10 Line Image 2XIANG-U-10-03幽人貞吉，中不自亂也。A quiet and solitary man, to whom, being steadfast and right, this brings good fortune: holding the due mean, the person will not allow themself to be thrown into disorder.
Hexagram 10 Line Image 3XIANG-U-10-04眇能視，不足以有明也。跛能履，不足以與行也。咥人之凶，位不當也。武人為于大君，志剛也。A one-eyed man who thinks that the person can see: the person is not fit to see clearly. A lame man who thinks that the person can tread well: one cannot walk along with them. The ill fortune of being bitten arises from the place not being the proper one for them. A mere bravo acting the part of a great ruler: this is because of their aims being too violent.
Hexagram 10 Line Image 4XIANG-U-10-05愬愬終吉，志行也。The person becomes full of cautious caution, and in the end this brings good fortune: their aim takes effect.
Hexagram 10 Line Image 5XIANG-U-10-06夬履貞厲，位正當也。The person treads resolutely; and though the person be steadfast and right, there is danger: this is due to their being in the position that is correct and appropriate to them.
Hexagram 10 Line Image 6XIANG-U-10-07元吉在上，大有慶也。This will bring great good fortune, and that in the occupancy of the topmost line: this is great matter for congratulation.
Hexagram 11 Great ImageXIANG-U-11-01天地交，泰；后以財成天地之道，輔相天地之宜，以左右民。The trigrams for heaven and earth in communication together form Tai. The sage sovereign, in harmony with this, fashions and completes their regulations after the courses of heaven and earth, and assists the application of the adaptations furnished by them,; in order to benefit the people.
Hexagram 11 Line Image 1XIANG-U-11-02拔茅征吉，志在外也。The good fortune of advance, as suggested by the emblem of the grass pulled up, arises from the will of the party intended being set on what is external to themself.
Hexagram 11 Line Image 2XIANG-U-11-03包荒，得尚于中行，以光大也。The person bears with the uncultivated, and proves themself acting in accordance with the due mean: for their intelligence is bright and their capacity is great.
Hexagram 11 Line Image 3XIANG-U-11-04无往不復，天地際也。There is no going away so that there will not be a return refers to this as the point where the interaction of heaven and earth takes place.
Hexagram 11 Line Image 4XIANG-U-11-05翩翩不富，皆失實也。不戒以孚，中心願也。The person comes fluttering down, not relying on their own rich resources: both the person and their neighbours are out of their real place where they are. They have not received warning, but come in the sincerity of their hearts: this is what they have desired in the core of their hearts.
Hexagram 11 Line Image 5XIANG-U-11-06以祉，元吉。中以行願也。By such a course there is happiness, and this will bring great good fortune: the person represented by the line employs the virtue proper to their central position to carry their wishes into effect.
Hexagram 11 Line Image 6XIANG-U-11-07城復于隍，其命亂也。The image gives this practical lesson: the city wall returned back into the moat shows how the governmental orders have long been in disorder.
Hexagram 12 Great ImageXIANG-U-12-01天地不交，否；君子以儉德辟難，不可榮以祿。The trigrams of heaven and earth, not in intercommunication, form Bi. The noble person, following this image, restrains the manifestation of) their virtue, and avoids the calamities that threaten them. There is no opportunity of conferring on them the glory of official reward.
Hexagram 12 Line Image 1XIANG-U-12-02拔茅貞吉，志在君也。The image gives this practical lesson: the good fortune through firm goodness, suggested by the pulling up of the grass, arises from the will of the parties intended being bent on serving the ruler.
Hexagram 12 Line Image 2XIANG-U-12-03大人否，亨。不亂羣也。The person of mature character, comporting themself as the distress and obstruction require, will have success: the person does not allow themself to be disordered by the herd of lesser people.
Hexagram 12 Line Image 3XIANG-U-12-04包羞，位不當也。That their shame is folded in their breast is because of the inappropriateness of their position.
Hexagram 12 Line Image 4XIANG-U-12-05有命无咎，志行也。The person acts in accordance with the ordination of Heaven, and commits no error: the purpose of their mind can be carried into effect.
Hexagram 12 Line Image 5XIANG-U-12-06大人之吉，位正當也。The good fortune of the great person arises from the correctness of their position.
Hexagram 12 Line Image 6XIANG-U-12-07否終則傾，何可長也。This line or image shows that the distress and obstruction having reached its end, it is overthrown and removed: how could it be prolonged?

Hexagram 13 Line Image 1XIANG-U-13-02出門同人，又誰咎也。The representative of the union of men is just issuing from their gate: who will blame them?

Hexagram 13 Line Image 5XIANG-U-13-06同人之先，以中直也。大師相遇，言相克也。The first action of the representative of the union of men here described arises from their central position and straightforward character. The meeting secured by their great host indicates that the opponents of it have been overcome.
Hexagram 13 Line Image 6XIANG-U-13-07同人于郊，志未得也。The representative of the union of men appears in the suburbs: their object has not yet been attained.

Hexagram 14 Line Image 1XIANG-U-14-02大有初九，无交害也。The image gives this practical lesson: this first line, undivided, of Da You shows no approach to what is injurious.

Hexagram 14 Line Image 6XIANG-U-14-07大有上吉，自天佑也。This line or image shows that the good fortune attached to the topmost line of Da You arises from the help of Heaven.

Hexagram 15 Line Image 6XIANG-U-15-07鳴謙，志未得也。可用行師，征邑國也。Their humility has made itself recognised: but all their aims have not yet been attained. The person may employ the force of arms, but only in correcting their own towns and state.

Hexagram 16 Line Image 1XIANG-U-16-02初六鳴豫，志窮凶也。The subject of the first line proclaims their pleasure and satisfaction: this brings misfortune; their wishes have been satisfied to overflowing.

Hexagram 16 Line Image 3XIANG-U-16-04盱豫有悔，位不當也。The person looks up for favours, while the person indulges the feeling of satisfaction; this will bring cause for regret: this is shown by the position not being the appropriate one.
