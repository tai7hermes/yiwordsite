01 乾 大象XIANG-U-01-01天行健，君子以自強不息。
Heaven moves with unflagging strength. A responsible person takes this image as a reason to cultivate character and keep improving without giving up.
01 乾 小象 1XIANG-U-01-02潛龍勿用，陽在下也。
The hidden dragon should not yet act because the yang line remains at the bottom. Its strength exists, but the appropriate occasion to display it has not arrived.
01 乾 小象 2XIANG-U-01-03見龍在田，德施普也。
The dragon appearing in the field signifies virtue spreading outward. Worthy conduct is beginning to benefit people beyond the individual.
01 乾 小象 3XIANG-U-01-04終日乾乾，反復道也。
Working diligently throughout the day means repeatedly returning to the proper course. Constant effort must be guided by principles rather than mere activity.
01 乾 小象 4XIANG-U-01-05或躍在淵，進无咎也。
The dragon poised to leap from the depths can advance without blame. It is beginning to act at the moment when advancement is appropriate.
01 乾 小象 5XIANG-U-01-06飛龍在天，大人造也。
The flying dragon in Heaven signifies the emergence of a capable and virtuous leader. The time has come for such a person to accomplish important work.
01 乾 小象 6XIANG-U-01-07亢龍有悔，盈不可久也。
The dragon soaring too high brings regret because fullness cannot last indefinitely. Excessive ascent, however impressive, eventually meets its limit.
01 乾 小象 7XIANG-U-01-08用九，天德不可為首也。
The use of all nine lines teaches that heavenly virtue does not depend on any one dragon claiming supremacy. Strength is best exercised without a contest for first place.
02 坤 大象XIANG-U-02-01地勢坤，君子以厚德載物。
Earth receives and supports everything with its depth and breadth. The exemplary person learns from it to develop generous character and carry the needs of others.
02 坤 小象 1XIANG-U-02-02履霜堅冰，陰始凝也。馴致其道，至堅冰也。
The first frost shows that yin has begun to condense. As this small change advances gradually, hard ice will follow; beginnings therefore deserve attention.
02 坤 小象 2XIANG-U-02-03六二之動，直以方也。不習无不利，地道光也。
The movement of the second yin line is straight and square. Its natural conduct is beneficial without rehearsal because the way of Earth is clear and open.
02 坤 小象 3XIANG-U-02-04含章可貞，以時發也。或從王事，知光大也。
Keeping excellence within and remaining steadfast means releasing one’s gifts at the right time. Serving the ruler without claiming achievement shows a broad understanding of duty.
02 坤 小象 4XIANG-U-02-05括囊无咎，慎不害也。
Tying up the mouth of the sack avoids blame because careful restraint prevents harm. There are moments when silence offers better protection than display.
02 坤 小象 5XIANG-U-02-06黃裳元吉，文在中也。
The yellow lower garment is greatly auspicious because its refinement is held within. Modest appearance can express centered, balanced character.
02 坤 小象 6XIANG-U-02-07龍戰于野，其道窮也。
Dragons fighting in the open field show that this course has reached an impasse. When opposing forces become extreme, conflict is difficult to avoid.
02 坤 小象 7XIANG-U-02-08用六永貞，以大終也。
Perseverance in the use of six is favorable because it enables a great completion. Receptive strength fulfils its work through steadfast continuity.
03 屯 大象XIANG-U-03-01雲雷，屯；君子以經綸。
Clouds and thunder mark the difficulty of beginnings. The exemplary person responds by untangling confusion, organizing affairs, and laying the foundations of good government.
03 屯 小象 1XIANG-U-03-02雖磐桓，志行正也。以貴下賤，大得民也。
Although progress is hesitant, the intention remains directed toward what is right. A person of higher standing gains the people’s support by humbly approaching those below.
03 屯 小象 2XIANG-U-03-03六二之難，乘剛也。十年乃字，反常也。
The difficulty of the second line comes from a yielding line riding a firm one. The long delay before marriage reflects an eventual return to an ordinary, fitting course.
03 屯 小象 3XIANG-U-03-04即鹿无虞，以從禽也。君子舍之，往吝窮也。
Pursuing deer without a guide means chasing prey blindly. The wise person abandons the pursuit because pressing onward would end in frustration.
03 屯 小象 4XIANG-U-03-05求而往，明也。
Going forward to seek the marriage shows discernment. The act becomes favorable because the purpose and occasion are finally clear.
03 屯 小象 5XIANG-U-03-06屯其膏，施未光也。
The rich nourishment cannot yet be shared widely, so its beneficial effects remain limited. Resources alone do not guarantee that help reaches others.
03 屯 小象 6XIANG-U-03-07泣血漣如，何可長也。
Tears of blood flow in the final line: how could such distress continue? Endless paralysis and grief cannot provide a sustainable way forward.
04 蒙 大象XIANG-U-04-01山下出泉，蒙；君子以果行育德。
A spring flows from beneath the mountain. Seeing this image of youthful uncertainty, the exemplary person acts decisively while patiently nurturing moral character.
04 蒙 小象 1XIANG-U-04-02利用刑人，以正法也。
Corrective discipline is useful when it establishes a clear rule. Education should release people from ignorance rather than impose punishment for its own sake.
04 蒙 小象 2XIANG-U-04-03子克家，剛柔接也。
The son’s ability to manage the household arises when firmness and gentleness connect. Successful guidance requires these complementary qualities.
04 蒙 小象 3XIANG-U-04-04勿用取女，行不順也。
The warning against taking the woman concerns conduct that does not proceed in the proper way. Attraction without integrity leads to an unsuitable union.
04 蒙 小象 4XIANG-U-04-05困蒙之吝，獨遠實也。
The distress of ignorance results from being isolated from what is real. Refusing instruction and remaining distant from trustworthy knowledge brings regret.
04 蒙 小象 5XIANG-U-04-06童蒙之吉，順以巽也。
The fortunate childlike learner is receptive and modest. Willingness to learn is more valuable than pretending to know everything.
04 蒙 小象 6XIANG-U-04-07利禦寇，上下順也。
Defending against bandits succeeds because higher and lower positions cooperate. Proper correction protects people instead of turning force into aggression.
05 需 大象XIANG-U-05-01雲上於天，需；君子以飲食宴樂。
Clouds gather above Heaven, but the rain has not yet arrived. The exemplary person waits without agitation, enjoying ordinary food, fellowship, and rest.
05 需 小象 1XIANG-U-05-02需于郊，不犯難行也。利用恆，无咎，未失常也。
Waiting in the outskirts avoids entering danger prematurely. Maintaining a steady way of life prevents mistakes and preserves one’s proper course.
05 需 小象 2XIANG-U-05-03需于沙，衍在中也。雖小有言，以終吉也。
Waiting on the sand allows room for movement without losing balance. Minor criticism need not spoil an ultimately favorable outcome.
05 需 小象 3XIANG-U-05-04需于泥，災在外也。自我致寇，敬慎不敗也。
Waiting in the mud places one close to trouble; the threat is still outside. If one has attracted attackers, alert and respectful caution may prevent defeat.
05 需 小象 4XIANG-U-05-05需于血，順以聽也。
Waiting amid bloodshed calls for a willingness to heed circumstances. Listening and yielding appropriately offers a path away from immediate peril.
05 需 小象 5XIANG-U-05-06酒食，貞吉，以中正也。
Food and drink during the wait are favorable because the person occupies a balanced and proper position. Composure is not the same as neglect.
05 需 小象 6XIANG-U-05-07不速之客來，敬之終吉。雖不當位，未大失也。
Unexpected visitors are treated respectfully, and the ending is fortunate. Though the position is not ideal, the essential requirements have not been seriously lost.
06 訟 大象XIANG-U-06-01天與水違行，訟；君子以作事謀始。
Heaven and water move in opposing directions, portraying contention. Before beginning an undertaking, the exemplary person carefully considers its origins and terms.
06 訟 小象 1XIANG-U-06-02不永所事，訟不可長也。雖小有言，其辯明也。
Not prolonging the dispute acknowledges that contention should not endure. Minor objections may arise, but the issues can eventually be clarified.
06 訟 小象 2XIANG-U-06-03不克訟，歸逋竄也。自下訟上，患至掇也。
An unsuccessful litigant withdraws and takes refuge. A subordinate who persists in suing a superior readily brings trouble upon himself in this historical setting.
06 訟 小象 3XIANG-U-06-04食舊德，從上吉也。
Living on inherited virtue and following those above can end favorably. One should undertake service without insistently demanding personal credit.
06 訟 小象 4XIANG-U-06-05復即命渝，安貞，不失也。
Changing course after a failed dispute means returning to one’s proper obligations. Peaceful constancy keeps a person from greater loss.
06 訟 小象 5XIANG-U-06-06訟，元吉，以中正也。
Judging a lawsuit with great good fortune rests on fairness and balance. Justice requires a decision rooted in what is centered and right.
06 訟 小象 6XIANG-U-06-07以訟受服，亦不足敬也。
An official robe won through litigation is not worthy of honor. A contentious victory does not by itself establish moral respectability.
07 師 大象XIANG-U-07-01地中有水，師；君子以容民畜衆。
Water lies within Earth, like a gathered force held in reserve. The exemplary person receives the people generously and fosters their shared strength.
07 師 小象 1XIANG-U-07-02師出以律；失律，凶也。
An army must depart under discipline. When military order breaks down, the expedition risks misfortune, whatever its intentions.
07 師 小象 2XIANG-U-07-03在師中吉，承天寵也。王三錫命，懷萬邦也。
Good fortune for the commander comes from receiving the ruler’s trust. Repeated appointments also impose responsibility for protecting the many states.
07 師 小象 3XIANG-U-07-04師或輿尸，大无功也。
An army returning with corpses has accomplished nothing worthy of praise. Poor command is measured by human loss as well as failure of objectives.
07 師 小象 4XIANG-U-07-05左次无咎，未失常也。
Withdrawing and encamping to one side is not blameworthy. A measured retreat need not violate proper military practice.
07 師 小象 5XIANG-U-07-06長子帥師，以中行也。弟子輿尸，使不當也。
The mature leader commands by the central way; placing an unfit younger man in charge invites casualties. Choosing the wrong commander has grave consequences.
07 師 小象 6XIANG-U-07-07大君有命，以正功也。小人勿用，必亂邦也。
The great ruler apportions honors to recognize genuine achievement. Unworthy people must not be entrusted with government, lest they disrupt the state.
08 比 大象XIANG-U-08-01地上有水，比；先王以建萬國，親諸侯。
Water spreads over Earth and joins with it. Former kings learned from this image to establish states and maintain close relationships with their regional lords.
08 比 小象 1XIANG-U-08-02比之初六，有它吉也。
The first line’s sincere attachment may bring unexpected good fortune. Trust formed at the outset can attract help from beyond the original circle.
08 比 小象 2XIANG-U-08-03比之自內，不自失也。
Joining with others from a genuine inner commitment prevents self-betrayal. The relationship is sound because it begins in sincerity.
08 比 小象 3XIANG-U-08-04比之匪人，不亦傷乎！
Associating with the wrong people is a painful mistake. Companionship without judgment can injure rather than support a person.
08 比 小象 4XIANG-U-08-05外比於賢，以從上也。
Openly aligning with the worthy outside oneself means following what is higher and better. Loyalty should attach to virtue, not mere familiarity.
08 比 小象 5XIANG-U-08-06顯比之吉，位正中也。舍逆取順，失前禽也。邑人不誡，上使中也。
Visible alliance is fortunate because the ruler’s position is centered and correct. In the hunting image, what resists is released while what comes willingly is accepted; subjects feel no need for alarm.
08 比 小象 6XIANG-U-08-07比之无首，无所終也。
Association without a beginning or head cannot reach a proper conclusion. A community lacking a coherent basis has little prospect of lasting.
09 小畜 大象XIANG-U-09-01風行天上，小畜，君子以懿文德。
Wind passes high across the sky, indicating a small gathering of strength. The exemplary person cultivates grace in conduct and refinement in character.
09 小畜 小象 1XIANG-U-09-02復自道，其義吉也。
Returning to one’s own right path is fortunate. The movement succeeds because it accords with what ought to be done.
09 小畜 小象 2XIANG-U-09-03牽復在中，亦不自失也。
Being drawn back toward the right course still preserves the center. Joining others in correction need not mean losing independent judgment.
09 小畜 小象 3XIANG-U-09-04夫妻反目，不能正室也。
The husband and wife turn against each other because the household relationship is not properly ordered. A damaged vehicle mirrors this internal discord.
09 小畜 小象 4XIANG-U-09-05有孚。惕出。上合志也。
Sincerity helps fear and danger recede because the yielding line shares the purpose of those above. Trust gives cooperation practical force.
09 小畜 小象 5XIANG-U-09-06有孚攣如，不獨富也。
Being bound together in good faith means not enjoying wealth alone. Prosperity has greater value when neighbors can share in it.
09 小畜 小象 6XIANG-U-09-07既雨既處，德積載也。君子征凶，有所疑也。
Rain has finally fallen because virtue and resources have accumulated. Yet further advance is suspect: completion calls for caution, not a new reckless campaign.
10 履 大象XIANG-U-10-01上天下澤，履；君子以辨上下，定民志。
Heaven stands above and the lake below, each in its proper place. The exemplary person clarifies roles and distinctions so that people understand their responsibilities.
10 履 小象 1XIANG-U-10-02素履之往，獨行願也。
Walking simply on one’s own path fulfills a personal resolve. A modest start needs no borrowed grandeur.
10 履 小象 2XIANG-U-10-03幽人貞吉，中不自亂也。
The secluded person stays fortunate because inner balance is not upset by outside demands. A tranquil path arises from steadiness of mind.
10 履 小象 3XIANG-U-10-04眇能視，不足以有明也。跛能履，不足以與行也。咥人之凶，位不當也。武人為于大君，志剛也。
Weak vision cannot supply clarity, and impaired walking cannot keep pace. The tiger’s bite follows an unsuitable position; the warrior’s ambition is strong but unwise.
10 履 小象 4XIANG-U-10-05愬愬終吉，志行也。
Fearful caution while treading near the tiger brings a good ending. Such restraint enables the person’s intention to be fulfilled.
10 履 小象 5XIANG-U-10-06夬履貞厲，位正當也。
Decisive walking remains dangerous even in a proper position. Rank and authority do not cancel the need to consider the consequences of firmness.
10 履 小象 6XIANG-U-10-07元吉在上，大有慶也。
Great fortune at the top signifies a cause for widespread celebration. Looking back over one’s conduct allows a fitting completion.
11 泰 大象XIANG-U-11-01天地交，泰；后以財成天地之道，輔相天地之宜，以左右民。
Heaven and Earth exchange their influences, producing peace and growth. The ruler supports this process by helping nature’s work and ensuring that people benefit from it.
11 泰 小象 1XIANG-U-11-02拔茅征吉，志在外也。
Pulling up grass brings its roots and companion shoots. Going forward together is fortunate because the group’s intention reaches beyond itself.
11 泰 小象 2XIANG-U-11-03包荒，得尚于中行，以光大也。
Broad-minded conduct accords with the middle way and makes virtue shine more widely. Inclusion must be joined to fairness.
11 泰 小象 3XIANG-U-11-04无往不復，天地際也。
Nothing moves outward without eventually returning. Rise and decline belong to the changing boundary between Heaven and Earth.
11 泰 小象 4XIANG-U-11-05翩翩不富，皆失實也。不戒以孚，中心願也。
Neighbors descend lightly without relying on wealth. Their trust reflects a heartfelt desire for fellowship rather than a calculated bargain.
11 泰 小象 5XIANG-U-11-06以祉，元吉。中以行願也。
The marriage alliance brings exceptional fortune because a centered intention can be carried out. Proper relationships translate purpose into action.
11 泰 小象 6XIANG-U-11-07城復于隍，其命亂也。
When a wall collapses back into the moat, government commands have become confused. The image warns that established order can break apart.
12 否 大象XIANG-U-12-01天地不交，否；君子以儉德辟難，不可榮以祿。
Heaven and Earth fail to communicate, illustrating stagnation. The exemplary person reduces outward display, preserves integrity, and refuses honors that would expose him to danger.
12 否 小象 1XIANG-U-12-02拔茅貞吉，志在君也。
The grass and its roots hold together in upright resolve. Even during obstruction, the intention remains directed toward a worthy ruler.
12 否 小象 2XIANG-U-12-03大人否，亨。不亂羣也。
A great person bears stagnation without mingling indiscriminately with the crowd. Keeping principle intact matters more than fitting in.
12 否 小象 3XIANG-U-12-04包羞，位不當也。
Concealed shame results from occupying an unsuitable position. A compromised role can force a person to hide what ought to be corrected.
12 否 小象 4XIANG-U-12-05有命无咎，志行也。
Acting under a legitimate mandate is free from blame and allows the underlying purpose to advance.
12 否 小象 5XIANG-U-12-06大人之吉，位正當也。
The great person’s favorable outcome comes from occupying the correct position. Authority must remain aligned with responsibility.
12 否 小象 6XIANG-U-12-07否終則傾，何可長也。
Stagnation eventually overturns; it cannot last forever. A blocked situation contains the possibility of its own reversal.
13 同人 大象XIANG-U-13-01天與火，同人；君子以類族辨物。
Heaven and fire rise together in the image of fellowship. The exemplary person groups people and things by kind while recognizing the distinctions between them.
13 同人 小象 1XIANG-U-13-02出門同人，又誰咎也。
Going beyond the gate to join with others gives no cause for reproach. Genuine fellowship should not be limited to one household.
13 同人 小象 2XIANG-U-13-03同人于宗，吝道也。
Associating only with one’s own clan is regrettable. Such narrow loyalty restricts the possibility of wider cooperation.
13 同人 小象 3XIANG-U-13-04伏戎于莽，敵剛也。三歲不興，安行也。
Troops hidden in brushwood confront a strong opponent. Their failure to move for three years reveals the danger and difficulty of that opposition.
13 同人 小象 4XIANG-U-13-05乘其墉，義弗克也，其吉，則困而反則也。
Climbing the wall but refraining from attack means accepting that assault is not justified. Restraint allows the person to return to the appropriate rule.
13 同人 小象 5XIANG-U-13-06同人之先，以中直也。大師相遇，言相克也。
The early tears of the allied people reflect honest inner purpose. After overcoming opposition, the armies can finally meet.
13 同人 小象 6XIANG-U-13-07同人于郊，志未得也。
Fellowship on the outskirts carries no regret, but the larger aspiration remains unrealized. Peaceful contact is not yet complete unity.
14 大有 大象XIANG-U-14-01火在天上，大有；君子以遏惡揚善，順天休命。
Fire shines high in Heaven, indicating great possession. The exemplary person restrains harmful conduct, promotes goodness, and honors Heaven’s favorable charge.
14 大有 小象 1XIANG-U-14-02大有初九，无交害也。
At the beginning of great possession, harmful association has not yet formed. Keeping clear of corrupt influence preserves one’s advantage.
14 大有 小象 2XIANG-U-14-03大車以載，積中不敗也。
A large wagon can carry heavy stores because its load is held securely at the center. Resources require dependable support.
14 大有 小象 3XIANG-U-14-04公用亨于天子，小人害也。
A duke’s offering to the Son of Heaven entails responsibilities a petty person cannot bear. High honors without suitable character bring harm.
14 大有 小象 4XIANG-U-14-05匪其彭，无咎；明辨晰也。
Refraining from ostentation avoids blame because the person sees the situation clearly. Knowing what not to display is a form of discernment.
14 大有 小象 5XIANG-U-14-06厥孚交如，信以發志也。威如之吉，易而无備也。
Authentic trust inspires purpose, while appropriate dignity prevents complacency. Authority can remain accessible without surrendering necessary care.
14 大有 小象 6XIANG-U-14-07大有上吉，自天佑也。
Great good fortune at the summit is described as help from Heaven. The blessing is associated with worthy conduct rather than mere accumulation.
15 謙 大象XIANG-U-15-01地中有山，謙；君子以裒多益寡，稱物平施。
A mountain rests within Earth, a picture of modesty. The exemplary person reduces what is excessive and adds to what is lacking, distributing things fairly.
15 謙 小象 1XIANG-U-15-02謙謙君子，卑以自牧也。
The deeply modest person governs the self through humility. Self-respect does not require superiority over others.
15 謙 小象 2XIANG-U-15-03鳴謙貞吉，中心得也。
Expressed modesty is fortunate because it comes from the heart. Genuine humility is an inward attainment, not a performance.
15 謙 小象 3XIANG-U-15-04勞謙君子，萬民服也。
A worker who remains modest despite achievement gains the trust of the people. Earned authority has no need of boasting.
15 謙 小象 4XIANG-U-15-05无不利撝謙；不違則也。
Actively showing modesty is favorable because it does not violate proper rules. Courtesy and effectiveness can coexist.
15 謙 小象 5XIANG-U-15-06利用侵伐，征不服也。
The expedition is directed against those who refuse proper order. In its ancient political setting, humility does not mean surrendering responsibility.
15 謙 小象 6XIANG-U-15-07鳴謙，志未得也。可用行師，征邑國也。
Although modest conduct is well known, the aspiration is still not wholly achieved. The permitted military action concerns restoring order in one’s own territory.
16 豫 大象XIANG-U-16-01雷出地奮，豫。先王以作樂崇德，殷薦之上帝，以配祖考。
Thunder breaks forth from Earth, creating an image of joyful movement. Ancient kings established music to honor virtue and offered solemn sacrifices to Heaven and the ancestors.
16 豫 小象 1XIANG-U-16-02初六鳴豫，志窮凶也。
Voicing delight too proudly leads to misfortune when ambition has reached its limit. Satisfaction should not become self-glorification.
16 豫 小象 2XIANG-U-16-03不終日，貞吉；以中正也。
Not waiting even a whole day to distinguish what is right is fortunate. Clear judgment comes from an inwardly centered and upright position.
16 豫 小象 3XIANG-U-16-04盱豫有悔，位不當也。
Looking upward in restless pursuit of pleasure produces regret because the position is unsuitable. Approval-seeking disturbs good judgment.
16 豫 小象 4XIANG-U-16-05由豫，大有得；志大行也。
The great gathering around a source of enthusiasm allows a far-reaching purpose to be realized. Trust turns common joy into collective achievement.
16 豫 小象 5XIANG-U-16-06六五貞疾，乘剛也。恆不死，中未亡也。
Persistent illness in the fifth line comes from resting above a firm line. The central position nevertheless prevents total collapse.
16 豫 小象 6XIANG-U-16-07冥豫在上，何可長也。
Blind delight at the top cannot endure. Enjoyment disconnected from judgment eventually becomes exhausted.
17 隨 大象XIANG-U-17-01澤中有雷，隨；君子以嚮晦入宴息。
Thunder rests within the lake. The exemplary person follows the rhythm of the day, withdrawing at dusk to rest rather than forcing continuous activity.
17 隨 小象 1XIANG-U-17-02官有渝，從正吉也。出門交有功，不失也。
A change of office is favorable if it follows what is right. Going outside to form relationships can produce results without compromising one’s principles.
17 隨 小象 2XIANG-U-17-03系小子，弗兼與也。
Binding oneself to the child excludes the grown man. Not every competing attachment can be maintained at the same time.
17 隨 小象 3XIANG-U-17-04系丈夫，志舍下也。
Following the mature person means leaving the lesser attachment behind. The direction of one’s loyalty shapes what is possible.
17 隨 小象 4XIANG-U-17-05隨有獲，其義凶也。有孚在道，明功也。
Gaining followers can be dangerous when acquisition becomes an end in itself. Sincere adherence to the way makes the purpose clear.
17 隨 小象 5XIANG-U-17-06孚于嘉，吉；位正中也。
Trust placed in what is excellent is fortunate because the position is right and central. The quality of what one follows matters.
17 隨 小象 6XIANG-U-17-07拘系之，上窮也。
Binding and tying someone at the very top shows that the situation has gone too far. Forced attachment cannot continue without limit.

Wind moves beneath a mountain, stirring up what has decayed. The exemplary person awakens the community and cultivates character in order to correct entrenched faults.
