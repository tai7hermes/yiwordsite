Hexagram 01 Words Commentary Unit 1WENYAN-01-01元者，善之長也。亨者，嘉之會也。利者，義之和也。貞者，事之幹也。君子體仁足以長人，嘉會足以合禮，利物足以和義，貞固足以幹事。君子行此四德者，故曰：「乾，元、亨、利、貞。」What is called under Qian the great and originating is in man the first and chief quality of goodness; what is called the penetrating is the assemblage of excellences; what is called the beneficial is the harmony of all that is right; and what is called the steadfast and principled is the faculty of action. The noble person, embodying benevolence, is fit to preside over men; presenting the assemblage of excellences, the person is fit to show in themself the union of all propriety; benefiting all creatures, the person is fit to exhibit the harmony of all that is right; steadfast and principled, the person is fit to manage all affairs. The fact that the noble person practises these four virtues justifies the application to them of the words; Kfiien represents what is great and originating, penetrating, beneficial, steadfast and principled.
Hexagram 01 Words Commentary Unit 2WENYAN-01-02初九曰：「潛龍，勿用。」何謂也？子曰：「龍德而隱者也。不易乎世，不成乎名，遯世无悶，不見是而无悶，樂則行之，憂則違之，確乎其不可拔，潛龍也。」What is the meaning of the words under the first line undivided, The dragon lies hid in the deep; it is not the time for active doing? The Master said: There the person is, with the powers of the dragon, and yet lying hid. The influence of the world would make no change in them; the person would do nothing merely to secure their fame. The person can live, withdrawn from the world, without regret; the person can experience disapproval without trouble of mind. Rejoicing in opportunity, the person carries their principles into action; sorrowing for want of opportunity, the person keeps with them in retirement. Yes, the person is not to be torn from their root in themself. This is the dragon lying hid.
Hexagram 01 Words Commentary Unit 3WENYAN-01-03九二曰：「見龍在田，利見大人。」何謂也？子曰：「龍德而正中者也。庸言之信，庸行之謹；閑邪存其誠，善世而不伐，德博而化。易曰：『見龍在田，利見大人。』君德也。」What is the meaning of the words under the second line, The dragon shows themself and is in the field; it is favorable to see the great person? The Master said: There the person is, with the dragons powers, and occupying exactly the central place. The person is sincere even in their ordinary words, and earnest in their ordinary conduct. Guarding against depravity, the person preserves their sincerity. Their goodness is recognised in the world, but the person does not boast of it. Their virtue is extensively displayed, and transformation ensues. The language of the Yi, The dragon shows themself and is in the field; it is favorable to see the great person," refers to a rulers virtue.
Hexagram 01 Words Commentary Unit 4WENYAN-01-04九三曰：「君子終日乾乾，夕惕若，厲，无咎。」何謂也？子曰：「君子進德脩業。忠信，所以進德也；脩辭立其誠，所以居業也。知至至之，可與言幾也。知終終之，可與存義也。是故居上位而不驕，在下位而不憂，故乾乾因其時而惕，雖危无咎矣。」What is the meaning of the words under the third line, The noble person is active and vigilant all the day, and in the evening still careful and cautious; the position is dangerous, but there is no mistake? The Master said: The noble person advances in virtue, and cultivates all the sphere of their duty. Their leal-heartedness and good faith are the way by which the person advances in virtue. Their attention to their words and establishinghis sincerity are the way by which the person occupies in their sphere. The person knows the utmost point to be reached, and reaches it, therefore showing themself in accord with the first springs of things; the person knows the end to be rested in, and rests in it, therefore preserving their righteousness in accordance with that end. Therefore the person occupies a high position without pride, and a low position without anxiety. Therefore it is that, being active and vigilant, and careful also and cautious as the time requires, though their position be dangerous, the person will make no mistake.
Hexagram 01 Words Commentary Unit 5WENYAN-01-05九四曰：「或躍在淵，无咎。」何謂也？子曰：「上下无常，非為邪也。進退无恒，非離羣也。君子進德脩業，欲及時也。故无咎。」What is the meaning of the words under the fourth line, The person is as if the person were leaping up, but still is in the deep; there is no mistake? The Master said: The person finds no permanent place either above or below, but the person does not commit the error of advancing. The person may advance or recede; there is no permanent place for them: but the person does not leave their fellows. The noble person, advancing in virtue and cultivating the sphere of their duty, yet wishes to advance only at the proper time, and therefore there is no mistake.
Hexagram 01 Words Commentary Unit 6WENYAN-01-06九五曰：「飛龍在天，利見大人。」何謂也？子曰：「同聲相應，同氣相求；水流濕，火就燥，雲從龍，風從虎；聖人作而萬物覩；本乎天者親上，本乎地者親下，則各從其類也。」What is the meaning of the words under the fifth line, The dragon is on the wing in the sky; it is favorable to see the great person? The Master said: Notes of the same key respond to one another; creatures of the same nature seek one another; water flows towards the place that is low and damp; fire rises up towards what is dry; clouds follow the dragon, and winds follow the tiger: so the sage makes their appearance, and all men look to them. Things that draw their origin from heaven move towards what is above; things that draw their origin from the earth cleave to what is below: so does everything follow its kind.
Hexagram 01 Words Commentary Unit 7WENYAN-01-07上九曰：「亢龍，有悔。」何謂也？子曰：「貴而无位，高而无民，賢人在下位而无輔，是以動而有悔也。」What does “The dragon rises too high; there is regret” mean? The Master said: “The position is honored but carries no office; the person is high but has no people to lead. Capable people are below, but none can provide support. Therefore action brings regret.”.

Hexagram 01 Words Commentary Unit 19WENYAN-01-19「飛龍在天」，乃位乎天德。The dragon is on the wing in the sky: this shows that their place is based on their heavenly virtue.
Hexagram 01 Words Commentary Unit 20WENYAN-01-20「亢龍有悔」，與時偕極。The dragon exceeds the proper limit; this will bring cause for regret: the time is come to an end, and so also is their opportunity.
Hexagram 01 Words Commentary Unit 21WENYAN-01-21「乾元用九」，乃見天則。This passage develops the idea in plain terms: undivided lines appear in all these representations of the great and originating power denoted by Qian: and from what follows in the Yao the model of action afforded by heaven.
Hexagram 01 Words Commentary Unit 22WENYAN-01-22乾元者，始而亨者也。利貞者，性情也。乾始能以美利利天下，不言所利，大矣哉！大哉乾元，剛健中正，純粹精也。六爻發揮，旁通情也。時乘六龍，以御天也。雲行雨施，天下平也。The passage is saying that the creative origin of Qian means that all things begin and then develop smoothly. “Beneficial and steadfast” describes the nature and proper direction of things. Qian begins the world and benefits everything through what is good, yet it does not claim any particular benefit for itself—how great this is! Great indeed is the creative power of Qian: strong, active, balanced, correct, pure, and refined. Its six lines unfold this meaning and connect it with every situation. At the right time, one rides the six dragons and works with heaven. Clouds move, rain falls, and the world comes into harmony.
Hexagram 01 Words Commentary Unit 23WENYAN-01-23君子以成德為行，日可見之行也。潛之為言也，隱而未見，行而未成，是以君子弗用也。In the noble person their conduct is the fruit of their perfected virtue, which might be seen therefore in their daily course; but the force of that phrase, lying hid, requires them to keep retired, and not yet show themself, nor proceed to the full development of their course. While this is the case, the noble person knows that it is not the time for active doing-.
Hexagram 01 Words Commentary Unit 24WENYAN-01-24君子學以聚之，問以辯之，寬以居之，仁以行之，易曰：「見龍在田，利見大人。」君德也。The noble person learns and accumulates the results of their learning; puts questions, and discriminates among those results; dwells magnanimously and unambitiously in what the person has attained to; and carries it into practice with benevolence. What the Yi says, The dragon appears in the field: it is favorable to meet with the great person, has reference to the virtuous qualities of a ruler as therefore described.
Hexagram 01 Words Commentary Unit 25WENYAN-01-25九三，重剛而不中，上不在天，下不在田；故乾乾因其時而惕，雖危，无咎矣。In the third line there is a twofold symbol of strength, but the position is not central. Its occupant is not in heaven above, nor is the person in the field beneath. Therefore there must be active vigilance and cautious apprehension as the time requires; and though the position be dangerous, there is no mistake.
Hexagram 01 Words Commentary Unit 26WENYAN-01-26九四，重剛而不中，上不在天，下不在田，中不在人，故或之。或之者，疑之也，故无咎。In the fourth line there is the symbol of strength, but the position is not central. Its occupant is not in heaven above, nor is the person in the field beneath, nor is the person in the place of man intermediate. Hence the person is in perplexity; and being so, the person has doubts about what should be their movements, and so will give no occasion for blame.
Hexagram 01 Words Commentary Unit 27WENYAN-01-27夫大人者，與天地合其德，與日月合其明，與四時合其序，與鬼神合其吉凶；先天而天弗違，後天而奉天時，天且弗違，而況於人乎？況於鬼神乎？The person of mature character is the person who is in harmony, in their attributes, with heaven and earth; in their brightness, with the sun and moon; in their orderly procedure, with the four seasons; and in their relation to what is fortunate and what is calamitous, in harmony with the spirit-like operations of Providence. The person may precede Heaven, and Heaven will not act in opposition to them; the person may follow Heaven, but will act only as Heaven at the time would do. If Heaven will not act in opposition to them, how much less will men! how much less will the spirit-like operation of Providence!
Hexagram 01 Words Commentary Unit 28WENYAN-01-28亢之為言也，知進而不知退，知存而不知亡，知得而不知喪；其唯聖人乎？知進退存亡而不失其正者，其唯聖人乎！The force of that phrase; exceeding the proper limits; indicates the knbecause of advance but not to retire; to maintain but not to let perish; to get but not to lose. The person only is the sage who knows to advance and to retire, to maintain and to let perish; and that without ever acting incorrectly. Yes, the person only is the sage!
Hexagram 02 Words Commentary Unit 1WENYAN-02-01坤至柔而動也剛，至靜而德方。後得主而有常，含萬物而化光。坤道其順乎！承天而時行。What is shown by Kun is most gentle and weak, but, when put in motion, is hard and strong; it is most still, but is able to give every definite form. By following, it obtains its proper lord, and pursues its regular course. It contains all things in itself, and its transforming power is glorious. Yes, what docility marks the way of Kun! It receives the influences of heaven, and acts at the proper time.
Hexagram 02 Words Commentary Unit 2WENYAN-02-02積善之家，必有餘慶；積不善之家，必有餘殃。臣弒其君，子弒其父，非一朝一夕之故，其所由來者漸矣。由辨之不早辨也。易曰：「履霜，堅冰至。」蓋言順也。The family that accumulates goodness is sure to have superabundant happiness, and the family that accumulates misfortune is sure to have superabundant misery. The murder of a ruler by their minister, or of their father by a son, is not the result of the events of one morning or one evening. The causes of it have gradually accumulated,; through the absence of early discrimination. The words of the Yi, The person treads on the hoar-frost; the strong ice will come by and by, show the natural issue and growth of things.
Hexagram 02 Words Commentary Unit 3WENYAN-02-03直其正也，方其義也。君子敬以直內，義以方外，敬義立而德不孤。「直方大，不習无不利。」則不疑其所行也。Straight indicates the correctness of the internal principle, and square, the righteousness of the external act. The noble person, therefore represented, by their self-reverence maintains the inward correctness, and in righteousness adjusts their external acts. Their reverence and righteousness being therefore established, their virtues are not solitary instances or of a single class. Straight, square, and great, working their operations, without repeated efforts, in every respect beneficial: this shows how such a one has no doubts as to what the person does.
Hexagram 02 Words Commentary Unit 4WENYAN-02-04陰雖有美，含之。以從王事，弗敢成也。地道也，妻道也，臣道也。地道无成，而代有終也。Although the person represented by this divided line has excellent qualities, the person does not display them, but keeps them under restraint. If the person engage with them in the service of the king, and be successful, the person will not claim that success for themself: this is the way of the earth, of a wife, of a minister. The way of the earth is; not to claim the merit of achievement, but on behalf of heaven to bring things to their proper issue.
Hexagram 02 Words Commentary Unit 5WENYAN-02-05天地變化，草木蕃；天地閉，賢人隱。易曰：「括囊，无咎，无譽。」蓋言謹也。Through the changes and transformations produced by heaven and earth, plants and trees grow luxuriantly. If the reciprocal influence of heaven and earth were shut up and restrained, we should have a state that might suggest to us the case of men of virtue and ability lying in obscurity. The words of the Yi, A sack tied up: this will bring no ground for blame or for praise, are in reality a lesson of caution.
Hexagram 02 Words Commentary Unit 6WENYAN-02-06君子黃中通理，正位居體，美在其中，而暢於四支，發於事業，美之至也。The noble person emblemed here by the yellow and correct colour, is possessed of comprehension and discrimination. The person occupies the correct position of supremacy, but that emblem is on the lower part of their person. Their excellence is in the centre of their being, but it diffuses a complacency over their four limbs, and is manifested in their conduct of affairs: this is the perfection of excellence.
Hexagram 02 Words Commentary Unit 7WENYAN-02-07陰疑於陽必戰，為其嫌於无陽也，故稱龍焉。猶未離其類也，故稱血焉。夫玄黃者，天地之雜也。天玄而地黃。The subject of the yin or divided line thinking themself equal to the subject of the yang, or undivided line, there is sure to be a contest As if indignant at there being no acknowledgment of the superiority of the person represented by the yang line, the text uses the term dragons. But still the subject of neither line can leave their class, and therefore we have the blood mentioned. The mention of that as being both azure and yellow indicates the mixture of heaven and earth. Heavens colour is azure and earths is yellow.
