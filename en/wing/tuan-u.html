Hexagram 01 Tuan CommentaryTUAN-U-01-01大哉乾元，萬物資始，乃統天。雲行雨施，品物流形，大明終始，六位時成。 時乘六龍以御天，乾道變化，各正性命，保合大和，乃利貞。 首出庶物，萬國咸寧。Qian describes the vast creative force from which all things begin. Clouds move, rain falls, and living things take shape. The six lines unfold in their proper times, like six dragons carrying the movement of heaven. As this creative way changes, each being develops its own nature and finds harmony with the whole. When this leading force works rightly, the world can live in peace.
Hexagram 02 Tuan CommentaryTUAN-U-02-01至哉坤元，萬物資生，乃順承天。坤厚載物，德合无疆；含弘光大，品物咸亨。牝馬地類，行地无疆，柔順利貞。 君子攸行，先迷失道，後順得常。 西南得朋，乃與類行，東北喪朋，乃終有慶。安貞之吉，應地无疆。Kun describes the deep, receptive power through which all things are born and sustained. Earth follows heaven, carries everything, and supports life without limit. Its model is the mare: gentle, steady, and able to travel far. If a person tries to lead without understanding the way, they become lost. If they follow what is right, they regain a stable course. Finding companions in the southwest and letting go of them in the northeast both fit the situation. Quiet steadiness brings good fortune because it follows the boundless character of earth.
Hexagram 03 Tuan CommentaryTUAN-U-03-01屯，剛柔始交而難生，動乎險中，大亨貞。雷雨之動滿盈，天造草昧，宜建侯而不寧。Zhun shows firm and yielding forces meeting for the first time, so difficulty naturally appears. Movement begins inside danger, yet real progress is possible when people remain steady and act at the right time. Thunder and rain fill the world while everything is still confused and unsettled. Leaders and helpers should be appointed, but no one should pretend that order has already been secured.
Hexagram 04 Tuan CommentaryTUAN-U-04-01蒙，山下有險，險而止，蒙。蒙亨，以亨行，時中也。匪我求童蒙，童蒙求我，志應也。初筮告，以剛中也。再三瀆，瀆則不告，瀆蒙也。蒙以養正，聖功也。Meng describes inexperience and confusion: danger lies below the mountain, and movement comes to a stop. Progress is possible when teaching is given at the right time. The teacher does not chase after the inexperienced student; the student must sincerely ask to learn. A first honest question deserves an answer. Repeated careless questioning shows disrespect, so no further answer is given. Guiding beginners toward what is right is the work of a sage.
Hexagram 05 Tuan CommentaryTUAN-U-05-01需，須也，險在前也；剛健而不陷，其義不困窮矣。需，有孚，光亨，貞吉，位乎天位，以正中也。利涉大川，往有功也。Xu means waiting because danger lies ahead. The inner strength of the hexagram is firm, but it does not rush into danger, so it avoids being trapped. Sincerity brings clarity and success, and steadiness brings good fortune, because the fifth line is central, correct, and in the place of leadership. Crossing the great river is beneficial when the time is right because well-timed action can accomplish real work.
Hexagram 06 Tuan CommentaryTUAN-U-06-01訟，上剛下險，險而健，訟。訟，有孚，窒，惕，中吉，剛來而得中也。終凶，訟不可成也。利見大人，尚中正也。不利涉大川，入于淵也。Song means conflict. Strength is above and danger is below, showing how determination can turn into dispute when risk is present. Even when a claim is sincere, it may still meet resistance. Staying alert and stopping before the conflict goes too far brings good fortune; pushing the dispute to the bitter end brings misfortune. It is helpful to seek a fair and mature judge, but it is not a good time to take on a major undertaking.
Hexagram 07 Tuan CommentaryTUAN-U-07-01師，眾也。貞，正也。能以眾正，可以王矣。剛中而應，行險而順，以此毒天下，而民從之，吉又何咎矣。Shi means an organized group or army. Steadfastness here means keeping the group directed toward what is right. A capable leader who can guide many people with discipline may take on great responsibility. The firm central line receives support, showing leadership that earns cooperation. Acting in danger while remaining responsive to the people can bring good fortune, but the burden and hardship involved should never be taken lightly.
Hexagram 08 Tuan CommentaryTUAN-U-08-01比，吉也。比，輔也，下順從也。原筮，元永貞，无咎，以剛中也。不寧方來，上下應也。後夫凶，其道窮也。Bi means joining with and supporting one another, so it can bring good fortune. Those below willingly gather around a firm and central leader. The relationship remains sound only if that leader has lasting integrity and stays steady. People who feel unsettled will come seeking connection because the upper and lower parts respond to each other. Whoever arrives too late misses the proper time for joining and meets misfortune.
Hexagram 09 Tuan CommentaryTUAN-U-09-01小畜，柔得位而上下應之，曰小畜。健而巽，剛中而志行，乃亨。密雲不雨，尚往也。自我西郊，施未行也。Xiao Xu describes a small amount of restraint and accumulation. One yielding line holds its proper place, and the lines above and below respond to it. Firm strength rises but is temporarily held back. Inner strength is joined with outward gentleness, allowing the plan to move forward. Dense clouds without rain show that conditions are gathering but are not yet ready to release their full result.
Hexagram 10 Tuan CommentaryTUAN-U-10-01履，柔履剛也。說而應乎乾，是以履虎尾，不咥人，亨。剛中正，履帝位而不疚，光明也。Lu means walking carefully. A yielding line steps on firm strength, like treading on a tiger's tail, yet avoids being bitten because it responds with openness and respect. The firm central line holds the position of leadership without guilt. The brightness of the hexagram shows that people must recognize differences in rank and responsibility and act with proper conduct.
Hexagram 11 Tuan CommentaryTUAN-U-11-01泰，小往大來，吉亨。則是天地交而萬物通也，上下交而其志同也。內陽而外陰，內健而外順，內君子而外小人，君子道長，小人道消也。Its main message is this: tai means that the small departs and the great approaches, bringing good fortune and progress. Heaven and earth interact, so all things communicate and develop. Those above and below connect and share a common purpose. Yang is within and yin is outside; strength is within and receptiveness is outside; noble people advance while lesser people withdraw. The way of the noble person grows, and the way of the lesser person declines.
Hexagram 12 Tuan CommentaryTUAN-U-12-01否之匪人，不利君子貞，大往小來。則是天地不交而萬物不通也，上下不交而天下无邦也。內陰而外陽，內柔而外剛，內小人而外君子，小人道長，君子道消也。The hexagram explains the situation this way: pi describes a failure of people to understand and unite with one another. This does not favor the noble person’s steadfast course: the great departs and the small approaches. Heaven and earth do not interact, so all things are blocked. Those above and below do not communicate, so society loses order. Yin, weakness, and lesser people are within; yang, strength, and noble people are outside. The way of lesser people grows while the way of noble people declines.
Hexagram 13 Tuan CommentaryTUAN-U-13-01同人，柔得位得中，而應乎乾，曰同人。同人曰，同人于野，亨。利涉大川，乾行也。文明以健，中正而應，君子正也。唯君子為能通天下之志。In Tong Ren the weak line has the place of influence, the central place, and responds to the corresponding line in Qian above; therefore comes its name of Tong Ren or Union of men. Tong Ren says: The language, Tong Ren appears here as we find it in the remote districts of the country, indicating progress and success, and that it is favorable to cross the great stream, is moulded by its containing the strength symbolled in Qian. Then we have the trigram indicating elegance and intelligence, supported by that indicating strength; with the line in the central, and its correct, position, and responding to the corresponding line above: all representing the correct course of the noble person. It is only the noble person who can comprehend and affect the minds of all in the world.
Hexagram 14 Tuan CommentaryTUAN-U-14-01大有，柔得尊位，大中而上下應之，曰大有。其德剛健而文明，應乎天而時行，是以元亨。In Da You the weak line has the place of honour, is clearly central, and the strong lines above and below respond to it. Hence comes its name of Da You Having what is Great. The attributes of its component trigrams are strength and vigour with elegance and brightness. The ruling line in it responds to the ruling line in the symbol of heaven, and consequently its action is all at the proper times. In this way it is said to indicate great progress and success.
Hexagram 15 Tuan CommentaryTUAN-U-15-01謙，亨，天道下濟而光明，地道卑而上行。天道虧盈而益謙，地道變盈而流謙，鬼神害盈而福謙，人道惡盈而好謙。謙尊而光，卑而不可逾，君子之終也。Qian indicates progress and success. It is the way of heaven to send down its beneficial influences below, where they are brilliantly displayed. It is the way of earth, lying low, to send its influences upwards and there to act. It is the way of heaven to diminish the full and augment the humble. It is the way of earth to overthrow the full and replenish the humble. Spiritual Beings inflict serious trouble on the full and bless the humble. It is the way of men to hate the full and love the humble. Humility in a position of honour makes that still more brilliant; and in a low position men will not seek to pass beyond it. Therefore it is that the noble person will have a good issue to their undertakings.
Hexagram 16 Tuan CommentaryTUAN-U-16-01豫，剛應而志行，順以動，豫。豫，順以動，故天地如之，而況建侯行師乎？天地以順動，故日月不過，而四時不忒；聖人以順動，則刑罰清而民服。豫之時義大矣哉！The hexagram explains the situation this way: yu arises when the firm line receives a response and its purpose can be carried out: receptiveness works through movement. Heaven and earth also move through this kind of harmony; how much more important is it when appointing leaders or mobilizing an army. Because nature moves in accord with order, the sun, moon, and seasons do not lose their timing. When sages act in accord with order, punishments are just and the people accept them. The timing and meaning of Yu are important.
Hexagram 17 Tuan CommentaryTUAN-U-17-01隨，剛來而下柔，動而說，隨。大亨貞，无咎，而天下隨時，隨之時義大矣哉！Its main message is this: sui means following. The firm trigram places itself below the yielding one, while movement joins with joy. Great progress is possible through steadfastness, with no blame. The world follows what is appropriate to the time. The timing and meaning of following are therefore important.
Hexagram 18 Tuan CommentaryTUAN-U-18-01蠱，剛上而柔下，巽而止，蠱。蠱，元亨，而天下治也。利涉大川，往有事也。先甲三日，後甲三日，終則有始，天行也。In Ku we have the strong trigram above, and the weak one below; we have below pliancy, and above stopping: these give the idea of K u a Troublous Condition of affairs verging to ruin. Ku indicates great progress and success: through the course shown in it, everyone in the world, this will bring good order. This will bring advantage in crossing the oreat stream: the person who advances will encounter the business to be done. The person should weigh well, however, the events of three days before the turning-point, and those to be done three days after it: the end of confusion is the beginning of order; such is the procedure of Heaven.
Hexagram 19 Tuan CommentaryTUAN-U-19-01臨，剛浸而長。說而順，剛中而應，大亨以正，天之道也。至于八月有凶，消不久也。In Lin the strong lines gradually increasing and advancing. The lower trigram is the symbol of being pleased, and the upper of being compliant. The strong line is in the central position, and is properly responded to. There is great progress and success, along with steadfastness: this is the way of Heaven. In the eighth month this brings misfortune: the advancing power will decay after no long time.
Hexagram 20 Tuan CommentaryTUAN-U-20-01大觀在上，順而巽，中正以觀天下。觀，盥而不荐，有孚顒若，下觀而化也。觀天之神道，而四時不忒，聖人以神道設教，而天下服矣。The great Manifester occupies an upper place in the figure, which consists of (the trigrams Kwan shows its subject like a worshipper who has washed their hands, but not yet presented their offerings; with sincerity and an appearance of dignity commanding reverent regard: all beneath look to them and are transformed. When we contemplate the spirit-like way of Heaven, how the four seasons proceed without error. The sages, following this image spirit-like way, laid down their instructions, and everyone in the world yield submission to them.
Hexagram 21 Tuan CommentaryTUAN-U-21-01頤中有物，曰噬嗑，噬嗑而亨。剛柔分，動而明，雷電合而章。柔得中而上行，雖不當位，利用獄也。The hexagram explains the situation this way: the existence of something between the jaws gives rise to the name Shih Ho Union by means of biting through the intervening article. The Union by means of biting through the intervening article indicates the progress denoted by the hexagram.
Hexagram 22 Tuan CommentaryTUAN-U-22-01賁，亨；柔來而文剛，故亨。分剛上而文柔，故小利有攸往。剛柔交錯，天文也；文明以止，人文也。觀乎天文，以察時變；觀乎人文，以化成天下。Its main message is this: bi means adornment. A yielding line comes to decorate firmness, allowing progress; a firm line rises to decorate what is yielding, so only small advances are favorable. The interweaving of firm and yielding forms the patterns of heaven. Clarity joined with restraint forms human culture. By observing heavenly patterns, we recognize seasonal change; by observing human culture, we educate and transform society.
Hexagram 23 Tuan CommentaryTUAN-U-23-01剝，剝也，柔變剛也。不利有攸往，小人長也。順而止之，觀象也。君子尚消息盈虛，天行也。The hexagram explains the situation this way: bo means stripping away: yielding lines are replacing the firm lines. Going forward is not favorable because the influence of lesser people is growing. Receptiveness joined with stopping shows that the noble person should understand the image and stop. The noble person values the cycles of decrease and increase, fullness and emptiness, which follow the movement of heaven.
Hexagram 24 Tuan CommentaryTUAN-U-24-01復亨；剛反，動而以順行，是以出入无疾，朋來无咎。反復其道，七日來復，天行也。利有攸往，剛長也。復其見天地之心乎？Fu indicates the progress of what it means: it is the coming back of what is intended by the undivided line. Its subjects actions show movement directed by accordance with natural order. Hence the person finds no one to distress them in their exits and entrances, and friends come to them, and no error is committed. The person will return and repeat their proper course; in seven days comes their return: such is the movement of the heavenly revolution. This will bring advantage in whatever direction movement is made: the strong lines are growing and increasing. Do we not see in Fu the mind of heaven and earth?
Hexagram 25 Tuan CommentaryTUAN-U-25-01无妄，剛自外來，而為主於內。動而健，剛中而應，大亨以正，天之命也。其匪正有眚，不利有攸往。无妄之往，何之矣？ 天命不佑，行矣哉？The hexagram explains the situation this way: in Wu Wang, a firm line comes from outside and becomes the leading force within. Movement joins with strength, and the central firm line receives a response. This is why great progress depends on acting correctly and in accord with Heaven. If the conduct is not right, trouble follows and it is not favorable to proceed. Without innocence and Heaven’s mandate, where could one rightly go?
Hexagram 26 Tuan CommentaryTUAN-U-26-01大畜，剛健篤實輝光，日新其德，剛上而尚賢。能止健，大正也。不家食吉，養賢也。利涉大川，應乎天也。In the trigrams composing Da Xu we have the attributes of the greatest strength and of substantial solidity, which emit a brilliant light; and indicate a daily renewal of their virtue by the person represented by it. The strong line is in the highest place, and suggests the value set on talents and virtue; there is power in the upper trigram to keep the strongest in restraint: all this shows the great correctness required in the hexagram. The good fortune attached to the subjects not seeking to enjoy their revenues in their own family shows how talents and virtue are nourished. It is favorable to cross the great stream: the fifth line, representing the ruler, is responded to by the second, the central line of Qian, representing Heaven.
Hexagram 27 Tuan CommentaryTUAN-U-27-01頤貞吉，養正則吉也。觀頤，觀其所養也；自求口實，觀其自養也。天地養萬物，聖人養賢，以及萬民；頤之時義大矣哉！1 indicates that with steadfastness this brings good fortune: when the nourishing is correct, this brings good fortune. We must look at what we are seeking to nourish: we must look at those whom we wish to nourish. We must by the exercise of our thoughts seek the proper aliment: we must look to our own nourishing of ourselves. Heaven and earth nourish all things. The sages nourish men of talents and virtue, by them to reach to the myriads of the people. Great is the work intended by this nourishing in its time!
Hexagram 28 Tuan CommentaryTUAN-U-28-01大過，大者過也。棟橈，本末弱也。剛過而中，巽而說行，利有攸往，乃亨。大過之時義大矣哉！Its main message is this: da Guo means that the firm lines are in excess. The supporting beam bends because both ends are weak. Although firmness is excessive, the central lines remain balanced. Gentleness and joy support action, so it is favorable to go forward and progress is possible. The demands of this exceptional time are great.
Hexagram 29 Tuan CommentaryTUAN-U-29-01習坎，重險也。水流而不盈，行險而不失其信。維心亨，乃以剛中也。行有尚，往有功也。天險不可升也，地險山川丘陵也，王公設險以守其國，坎之時用大矣哉！Kan repeated shows us one defile succeeding another. This is the nature of water; it flows on, without accumulating its volume so as to overflow; it pursues its way through a dangerous defile, without losing its true nature. That the mind is penetrating is shown by the strong line in the centre. That action following this image will be of high value tells us that advance will be followed by achievement. The dangerous height of heaven cannot be ascended; the difficult places of the earth are moun tains, rivers, hills, and mounds. Kings and princes arrange, by means of such strengths, to maintain their territories. Great indeed is the use of what is here taught about seasons of danger.
Hexagram 30 Tuan CommentaryTUAN-U-30-01離，麗也；日月麗乎天，百谷草木麗乎土，重明以麗乎正，乃化成天下。柔麗乎中正，故亨；是以畜牝牛吉也。The hexagram explains the situation this way: li means being attached to. The sun and moon have their place in the sky. All the grains, grass, and trees have their place on the earth. The double brightness of the two trigrams adheres to what is correct, and the result is the transforming and perfecting all in the world. The weak second line occupies the middle and correct position, and gives the indication of a free and successful course; and, moreover, nourishing docility like that of the cow will lead to good fortune.
