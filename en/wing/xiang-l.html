31 咸 大象XIANG-L-31-01山上有澤，咸；君子以虛受人。
A lake rests upon a mountain: this is Influence. The exemplary person welcomes others with an open and receptive mind.
31 咸 小象 1XIANG-L-31-02 181Yao咸其拇，志在外也。
The stirring of the big toe shows that the intention is already directed outward, although movement has barely begun.

Remaining still is fortunate even amid danger, because yielding appropriately avoids harm.
31 咸 小象 3XIANG-L-31-04 183Yao咸其股，亦不處也。志在隨人，所執下也。
Feeling the influence in the thighs prevents remaining still; merely following others reveals a low and dependent purpose.
31 咸 小象 4XIANG-L-31-05 184Yao貞吉悔亡，未感害也。憧憧往來，未光大也。
Steadfastness removes regret because influence has not brought harm. Restless thoughts, however, cannot accomplish something broad.
31 咸 小象 5XIANG-L-31-06 185Yao咸其脢，志末也。
Influence felt in the back shows that the aspiration has reached only an outer or limited part of the person.
31 咸 小象 6XIANG-L-31-07 186Yao咸其輔，頰，舌，滕口說也。
Influence confined to jaw, cheeks, and tongue is little more than persuasive speech, not deep or lasting feeling.
