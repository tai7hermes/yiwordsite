單元OriginalTranslation
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 1 Unit 1XUGUA-01-01有天地，然後萬物生焉。The sequence moves forward for this reason: after heaven and earth came into existence, all things were born.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 1 Unit 2XUGUA-01-02盈天地之間者，唯萬物，故受之以屯；屯者盈也，屯者物之始生也。This is how the next hexagram follows: what fills the space between heaven and earth is nothing but all things; therefore Zhun follows. Zhun means fullness and is the beginning of things coming to birth.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 1 Unit 3XUGUA-01-03物生必蒙，故受之以蒙；蒙者蒙也，物之穉也。The sequence moves forward for this reason: things newly born are necessarily immature; therefore Meng follows. Meng means immaturity; the youth of things.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 1 Unit 4XUGUA-01-04物穉不可不養也，故受之以需；需者飲食之道也。This is how the next hexagram follows: young things must be nourished; therefore Xu follows. Xu concerns the way of food and drink.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 1 Unit 5XUGUA-01-05飲食必有訟，故受之以訟。The sequence moves forward for this reason: food and drink inevitably give rise to contention; therefore Song follows.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 1 Unit 6XUGUA-01-06訟必有眾起，故受之以師；師者眾也。This is how the next hexagram follows: contention inevitably causes crowds to arise; therefore Shi follows. Shi means a multitude.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 1 Unit 7XUGUA-01-07眾必有所比，故受之以比；比者比也。The sequence moves forward for this reason: a multitude must have those with whom it associates; therefore Bi follows. Bi means association.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 1 Unit 8XUGUA-01-08比必有所畜也，故受之以小畜。This is how the next hexagram follows: association necessarily involves accumulation; therefore Xiao Xu follows.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 1 Unit 9XUGUA-01-09物畜然後有禮，故受之以履。The sequence moves forward for this reason: after things are accumulated, ritual order becomes possible; therefore Lu follows.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 1 Unit 10XUGUA-01-10履而泰，然後安，故受之以泰；泰者通也。The sequence moves forward for this reason: by treading the proper way one reaches peace, and then security; therefore Tai follows. Tai means free communication.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 1 Unit 11XUGUA-01-11物不可以終通，故受之以否。This is how the next hexagram follows: things cannot remain forever in free communication; therefore Pi follows.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 1 Unit 12XUGUA-01-12物不可以終否，故受之以同人。The sequence moves forward for this reason: things cannot remain forever obstructed; therefore Tong Ren follows.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 1 Unit 13XUGUA-01-13與人同者，物必歸焉，故受之以大有。This is how the next hexagram follows: those who unite with others necessarily attract things to themselves; therefore Da You follows.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 1 Unit 14XUGUA-01-14有大者不可以盈，故受之以謙。The sequence moves forward for this reason: one who possesses greatness must not let it overflow; therefore Qian follows.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 1 Unit 15XUGUA-01-15有大而能謙，必豫，故受之以豫。This is how the next hexagram follows: one who possesses greatness and can remain modest will surely have joy; therefore Yu follows.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 1 Unit 16XUGUA-01-16豫必有隨，故受之以隨。The sequence moves forward for this reason: joy inevitably brings followers; therefore Sui follows.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 1 Unit 17XUGUA-01-17以喜隨人者，必有事，故受之以蠱；蠱者事也。This is how the next hexagram follows: one who gladly follows others will necessarily have affairs to manage; therefore Gu follows. Gu means affairs requiring correction.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 1 Unit 18XUGUA-01-18有事而后可大，故受之以臨；臨者大也。The sequence moves forward for this reason: only after there are affairs to manage can greatness develop; therefore Lin follows. Lin means greatness approaching.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 1 Unit 19XUGUA-01-19物大然後可觀，故受之以觀。This is how the next hexagram follows: only after things become great can they be observed; therefore Guan follows.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 1 Unit 20XUGUA-01-20可觀而后有所合，故受之以噬嗑；嗑者合也。After something can be observed, union becomes possible; therefore Shi The person follows. The person means joining.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 1 Unit 21XUGUA-01-21物不可以苟合而已，故受之以賁；賁者飾也。The sequence moves forward for this reason: things must not merely unite carelessly; therefore Bi follows. Bi means adornment.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 1 Unit 22XUGUA-01-22致飾然後亨，則盡矣，故受之以剝；剝者剝也。This is how the next hexagram follows: when adornment is carried to completion, prosperity reaches its limit; therefore Bo follows. Bo means stripping away.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 1 Unit 23XUGUA-01-23物不可以終盡，剝窮上反下，故受之以復。The sequence moves forward for this reason: things cannot be exhausted forever. When stripping away reaches the top, it returns below; therefore Fu follows.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 1 Unit 24XUGUA-01-24復則不妄矣，故受之以无妄。This is how the next hexagram follows: with return, one no longer acts falsely; therefore Wu Wang follows.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 1 Unit 25XUGUA-01-25有无妄然後可畜，故受之以大畜。The sequence moves forward for this reason: only with freedom from falsehood can there be accumulation; therefore Da Xu follows.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 1 Unit 26XUGUA-01-26物畜然後可養，故受之以頤；頤者養也。This is how the next hexagram follows: after things are accumulated, they can be nourished; therefore Yi follows. Yi means nourishment.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 1 Unit 27XUGUA-01-27不養則不可動，故受之以大過。The sequence moves forward for this reason: without nourishment, there can be no movement; therefore Da Guo follows.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 1 Unit 28XUGUA-01-28物不可以終過，故受之以坎；坎者陷也。This is how the next hexagram follows: things cannot remain forever in excess; therefore Kan follows. Kan means a pitfall.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 1 Unit 29XUGUA-01-29陷必有所麗，故受之以離；離者麗也。The sequence moves forward for this reason: what falls into a pit must attach itself somewhere; therefore Li follows. Li means attachment.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 2 Unit 1XUGUA-02-01有天地然後有萬物，有萬物然後有男女，有男女然後有夫婦，有夫婦然後有父子，有父子然後有君臣，有君臣然後有上下，有上下然後禮儀有所錯。This is how the next hexagram follows: after heaven and earth exist, there are all things; after all things, male and female; after male and female, husband and wife; after husband and wife, father and child; after father and child, ruler and minister; after ruler and minister, superior and subordinate. Once superior and subordinate exist, rites and proper conduct have places in which to be arranged.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 2 Unit 2XUGUA-02-02夫婦之道，不可以不久也，故受之以恆；恆者久也。The sequence moves forward for this reason: the way of husband and wife cannot fail to endure; therefore Heng follows. Heng means duration.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 2 Unit 3XUGUA-02-03物不可以久居其所，故受之以遯；遯者退也。This is how the next hexagram follows: things cannot remain forever in the same place; therefore Dun follows. Dun means withdrawal.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 2 Unit 4XUGUA-02-04物不可終遯，故受之以大壯。The sequence moves forward for this reason: things cannot withdraw forever; therefore Da Zhuang follows.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 2 Unit 5XUGUA-02-05物不可以終壯，故受之以晉；晉者進也。This is how the next hexagram follows: things cannot remain forever strong; therefore Jin follows. Jin means advance.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 2 Unit 6XUGUA-02-06進必有所傷，故受之以明夷；夷者傷也。The sequence moves forward for this reason: advance necessarily brings the possibility of injury; therefore Ming Yi follows. Yi means injury.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 2 Unit 7XUGUA-02-07傷於外者，必反其家，故受之以家人。This is how the next hexagram follows: one who is injured outside necessarily returns home; therefore Jia Ren follows.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 2 Unit 8XUGUA-02-08家道窮必乖，故受之以睽；睽者乖也。The sequence moves forward for this reason: when the way of the household reaches an impasse, estrangement follows; therefore Kui follows. Kui means estrangement.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 2 Unit 9XUGUA-02-09乖必有難，故受之以蹇；蹇者難也。This is how the next hexagram follows: estrangement necessarily brings difficulty; therefore Jian follows. Jian means difficulty.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 2 Unit 10XUGUA-02-10物不可終難，故受之以解；解者緩也。This is how the next hexagram follows: things cannot remain forever in difficulty; therefore Xie follows. Xie means release and easing.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 2 Unit 11XUGUA-02-11緩必有所失，故受之以損。The sequence moves forward for this reason: relaxation necessarily entails some loss; therefore Sun follows.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 2 Unit 12XUGUA-02-12損而不已，必益，故受之以益。This is how the next hexagram follows: when decrease continues without stopping, increase necessarily follows; therefore Yi follows.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 2 Unit 13XUGUA-02-13益而不已，必決，故受之以夬； 夬者決也。The sequence moves forward for this reason: when increase continues without stopping, a decisive break necessarily follows; therefore Guai follows. Guai means decision.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 2 Unit 14XUGUA-02-14決必有所遇，故受之以姤；姤者遇也。This is how the next hexagram follows: decision necessarily leads to an encounter; therefore Gou follows. Gou means encounter.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 2 Unit 15XUGUA-02-15物相遇而后聚，故受之以萃；萃者聚也。The sequence moves forward for this reason: after things encounter one another, they gather; therefore Cui follows. Cui means gathering.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 2 Unit 16XUGUA-02-16聚而上者，謂之升，故受之以升。This is how the next hexagram follows: what gathers and rises is called Sheng; therefore Sheng follows.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 2 Unit 17XUGUA-02-17升而不已，必困，故受之以困。The sequence moves forward for this reason: if rising continues without stopping, exhaustion necessarily follows; therefore Kun follows.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 2 Unit 18XUGUA-02-18困乎上者，必反下，故受之以井。This is how the next hexagram follows: what is exhausted above necessarily returns below; therefore Jing follows.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 2 Unit 19XUGUA-02-19井道不可不革，故受之以革。The sequence moves forward for this reason: the way of the well cannot avoid renewal; therefore Ge follows.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 2 Unit 20XUGUA-02-20革物者莫若鼎，故受之以鼎。The sequence moves forward for this reason: nothing transforms things better than the cauldron; therefore Ding follows.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 2 Unit 21XUGUA-02-21主器者莫若長子，故受之以震；震者動也。This is how the next hexagram follows: no one is better suited to preside over the ritual vessel than the eldest son; therefore Zhen follows. Zhen means movement.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 2 Unit 22XUGUA-02-22物不可以終動，止之，故受之以艮；艮者止也。The sequence moves forward for this reason: things cannot remain forever in motion; they must be stopped. Therefore Gen follows. Gen means stopping.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 2 Unit 23XUGUA-02-23物不可以終止，故受之以漸；漸者進也。This is how the next hexagram follows: things cannot remain forever stopped; therefore Jian follows. Jian means gradual advance.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 2 Unit 24XUGUA-02-24進必有所歸，故受之以歸妹。The sequence moves forward for this reason: advance necessarily has a destination; therefore Gui Mei follows.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 2 Unit 25XUGUA-02-25得其所歸者必大，故受之以豐；豐者大也。This is how the next hexagram follows: one who reaches the place to which one belongs necessarily becomes great; therefore Feng follows. Feng means abundance and greatness.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 2 Unit 26XUGUA-02-26窮大者必失其居，故受之以旅。The sequence moves forward for this reason: when greatness is exhausted, one necessarily loses one’s dwelling; therefore Lu follows.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 2 Unit 27XUGUA-02-27旅而無所容，故受之以巽；巽者入也。This is how the next hexagram follows: a traveler who has nowhere to be received must enter somewhere; therefore Xun follows. Xun means entering.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 2 Unit 28XUGUA-02-28入而后說之，故受之以兌；兌者說也。The sequence moves forward for this reason: after entering, one rejoices; therefore Dui follows. Dui means joy.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 2 Unit 29XUGUA-02-29說而后散之，故受之以渙；渙者離也。This is how the next hexagram follows: after joy, things disperse; therefore Huan follows. Huan means dispersion and separation.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 2 Unit 30XUGUA-02-30物不可以終離，故受之以節。This is how the next hexagram follows: things cannot remain forever dispersed; therefore Jie follows.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 2 Unit 31XUGUA-02-31節而信之，故受之以中孚。The sequence moves forward for this reason: restraint gives rise to trust; therefore Zhong Fu follows.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 2 Unit 32XUGUA-02-32有其信者，必行之，故受之以小過。This is how the next hexagram follows: one who possesses trust necessarily puts it into action; therefore Xiao Guo follows.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 2 Unit 33XUGUA-02-33有過物者，必濟，故受之既濟。The sequence moves forward for this reason: one who surpasses things necessarily brings them across; therefore Ji Ji follows.
Sequence of Hexagrams Chapter 2 Unit 34XUGUA-02-34物不可窮也，故受之以未濟；終焉。This is how the next hexagram follows: things cannot be exhausted; therefore Wei Ji follows, and with it the sequence comes to an end.
