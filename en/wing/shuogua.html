Discussion of Trigrams Chapter 1 Unit 1SHUOGUA-01-01大哉乾元，萬物資始，乃統天。雲行雨施，品物流形，大明終始，六位時成。 時乘六龍以御天，乾道變化，各正性命，保合大和，乃利貞。 首出庶物，萬國咸寧。Qian describes the vast creative force from which all things begin. Clouds move, rain falls, and living things take shape. The six lines unfold in their proper times, like six dragons carrying the movement of heaven. As this creative way changes, each being develops its own nature and finds harmony with the whole. When this leading force works rightly, the world can live in peace.

If a person tries to lead without understanding the way, they become lost. If they follow what is right, they regain a stable course. Finding companions in the southwest and letting go of them in the northeast both fit the situation. Quiet steadiness brings good fortune because it follows the boundless character of earth.

Discussion of Trigrams Chapter 6 Unit 1SHUOGUA-06-01大有，柔得尊位，大中而上下應之，曰大有。其德剛健而文明，應乎天而時行，是以元亨。In Da You the weak line has the place of honour, is clearly central, and the strong lines above and below respond to it. Discussion of Trigrams Chapter 6 Unit 2SHUOGUA-06-02謙，亨，天道下濟而光明，地道卑而上行。天道虧盈而益謙，地道變盈而流謙，鬼神害盈而福謙，人道惡盈而好謙。謙尊而光，卑而不可逾，君子之終也。Qian indicates progress and success. It is the way of heaven to diminish the full and augment the humble. It is the way of earth to overthrow the full and replenish the humble. Spiritual Beings inflict serious trouble on the full and bless the humble. It is the way of men to hate the full and love the humble. Humility in a position of honour makes that still more brilliant; and in a low position men will not seek to pass beyond it.
