Ten Wings (易傳十翼)

The Yizhuan (Ten Wings) develops the Zhouyi's hexagram and line symbols into discourse on cosmic change, human action, moral cultivation, political order, and symbolic cognition. The "Ten Wings" are ten chapter units, not necessarily ten independent works completed simultaneously. This project divides the Ten Wings into 750 fixed content units.

Fixed codes and unit_ids do not change after publication, ensuring that website links, App bookmarks, and offline data continue to point to the same content.
