About YiWord

YiWord Yi = Word of Modus Operandi

YiWord is a Yijing (I Ching) reference website and companion app providing original text, translation, and commentary in three languages: Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, and English.

Content Sources

Content is based on the Chinese Text Project (ctext.org), Wikisource, the Academia Sinica Hanji Database, and references the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy and Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy. See the Introduction for the full source list.

Fixed ID System

All content uses fixed IDs (hexagram_id, unit_id, wing_id) that do not change after publication, ensuring that links, bookmarks, and offline data remain consistent.

Usage Boundaries

This site positions the Yijing as a classical cultural, philosophical, and structured reflection tool. Divination is a historical and cultural function, but obtained hexagrams should not be claimed to have scientifically verified predictive accuracy. This site does not provide medical, legal, financial, or investment advice.
